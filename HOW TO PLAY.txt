ROKUGAN TRAVELER - how to play (alpha)

START
  Double-click "Play Rokugan Traveler.bat" (in this folder, or the shortcut on your desktop).
  The title screen opens: New Game, Continue, Config, Exit Game.

NEW GAME
  The prologue asks which traveler you begin with. Each New Game follows one traveler's road:
    Hiruma Natsuko (Crab), Kakita Rensei (Crane), Kitsuki Gendo (Dragon), Akodo Shizue (Lion),
    Isawa Haruki (Phoenix), Shosuro Mitsue (Scorpion), Ide Tamotsu (Unicorn), Sayo (Ronin).
  Each road is four chapters. At the end of Chapter 4 a road-marker (or a letter) points to the Plains of
  Thunder: the finale, where all eight travelers meet. After the credits, the clear save returns to the
  Shrine of Osano-Wo and the post-game opens (the deep Shadowlands, Shiro Hiruma, the Orochi).
  Alpha note: there is no single save that walks all eight roads yet; the finale joins the other seven in
  for you, brought up to your level.

CONTROLS (keyboard)
  Arrow keys   move (a gamepad should work too)
  Z            confirm / talk / examine ("Check")
  X            back / jump
  C            menu (party, items, equipment)
  Esc          pause
  (The game shows these prompts in the corner of the screen.)
  In battle: Attack, Skill, Boost (spend VP for extra hits / stronger spells), Item, Defend, Talent, Call
  (a guest or hireling, and the finale's exploits), Flee.

SAVING
  Save at shrines, waystones and inns (the menu's Save, or the save prompts after big scenes).
  Saves are kept in _tools\rpgcobo-tool\savedata.

KNOWN ALPHA LIMITS
  The game runs in RPG-Cobo's test-play mode (no packaged .exe yet).
  Some optional content listed in NOTES.md is not built yet (post-game P4: Hikaru's Endless Strike, Purity of
  Shinsei). Report anything odd and where it happened.
