# Spells in flight

Void Bolt is Haruki's early single-target Void spell, S391. His staff hand remains planted when he wears a polearm. His free hand gathers the ink-dark knot, opens two fingers and releases. Two pale cold rings make the core legible against daylight while the dark facets retain a silhouette against bright scenery.

The core leaves the moving finger socket and travels for 480 ms on a restrained world-space arc. Short dark-lavender traces remain behind it, and the circling wrist trace makes the cast origin easy to find. The trail clears quickly as the core arrives. Four thin fractures ring a landed impact, using the attack's stock hit callback at the target's body. Misses fly just past the target and retain their original hit action. Blocks, skips, damage, targeting, and the existing critical presentation pipeline retain their original rules.

| Beat | Time into the attack action |
| --- | --- |
| Haruki looks to his fingers as the knot gathers | 0–185 ms |
| Core and wake cross to the actual resolved target | 185–665 ms |
| Existing hit callback applies the original outcome | Arrival, then stock timings |

The one intentional timing change is the short visual lead-in before the stock hit. S391 retains its original damage formula, Void cost, critical chance, hit sound, hit delay, and 650 ms impact wait. The projectile is attached to Haruki's spell release and still runs on ordinary and Void critical casts. If presentation resources are unavailable, the normal attack and its old FX485 effect are still available.

## What already travels

Yosai's approved S460 Void morph puts a continuous plume in 3D from the dragon's mouth across the opposing line. Ordinary S460 and S335 also emit a directional stream across the line. The generic S014 Shot uses the engine's projectile move. S301, S303, S304, S319, S347 and S363 currently resolve mainly at the target. Void Bolt adds the first traveling stage for that set.

## Element routes

| Route | Source to target |
| --- | --- |
| Fire | A short wick trails an ember headed into an orange and red plume. Use a wider fan or a low line for area flame. Give lightning invocations their own branching shape instead of drawing a flame to stand in for them. |
| Water | A narrow stream twists once around a fluid core, with a short spray at arrival. Turn the ribbon into a ring around the resolved center for Whirlpool. Reserve cooler white droplets for recovery. |
| Air | A lean swept streak reaches the target first. Two pale trails reveal the invisible push. Yari of Air can keep the shaft shape, while Tempest uses a short fan from one cast origin. |
| Earth and Jade | Fewer larger facets take a lower, weighted path and disperse into short debris on contact. A grounded swell stays on the caster when the invoked effect is defensive. |
| Void | Ink-dark facets, a thin pale rim and a curved wake. Use a narrow eclipse break when it lands. Void recovery, suppression and strength effects stay with the subject appropriate to the action. |

Each route can reuse the hand socket, 3D path sampler and attack-action rendezvous. Give it its own release, silhouette, palette and impact. Group spells can use one flight plus a split or pulse at the existing destination. Keep healing and wards close to the selected allies, and keep stances and invocations centered on the actor.

## Daylight review

With Haruki P104 and his default staff on M811, cast S391 at a visible foe such as the Kappa. Haruki needs one Void Point. A second target at another distance makes the arc and impact alignment easy to compare. Check an ordinary cast and a forced landed Void critical, including one damage popup per target and the normal sound. Watch for the violet core gathering in his free hand, traversing the lane, and fracturing on contact.
