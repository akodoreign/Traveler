# Shared A100 collar refit

The native A100 robe had a closed raised neck cap that followed the head. The revised garment has an open, lower neckline with a sewn facing. The neck rim follows the spine with up to 30% neck influence. The base material, existing UVs outside the collar, dye routes and separate A100 decal are preserved. The revised mesh serves the shared item and 188 default routes. Grandmother's fitted variant derives from that collar.

Kitchen Girl and Iuchi Farmer tested the shared female and male rigs. Grandmother and Gonbei covered a short female build and a broad male build. All four passed Coast Road capture at rest, in guard and through the walk. ## Grandmother

The body and robe use 0.75 of the original scale in depth, height and breadth. A dedicated shared-female variant softens the shoulders, thins the upper torso and arm volume, and follows the same 50 joints. The A100 robe and decal are shaped together. The lower neck seat keeps its bind positions and the upper head keeps the original world-space size. The verified portrait retains its albedo and all seven portable expression controls.
