# Real instrument recordings - credits

The kabuki rehearsal (tools/gen_music.py t_kabuki_act, MU128-MU131) and the miko dances (t_kagura MU126, t_jhk MU127)
play one-shots cut from these Freesound recordings (tools/real_samples.py -> samples/). The CC BY sounds require credit
to their authors, as below; CC0 needs none, but they are listed too. No free biwa recording exists: the Hard dance's
biwa part is the real shamisen played low.
