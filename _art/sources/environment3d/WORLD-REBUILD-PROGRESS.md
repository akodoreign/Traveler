## Working rules

1. Lead with readable shapes and a tight color palette. Inspect Comfy meshes for baked scenery, white backdrops, and stray geometry before staging them. Make structures such as doors and shelves from clean, separately modeled pieces when fit matters.
2. Use the actual doorway cut and floor grade for the door and stoop. Bind ajar leaves to live entrance events. Walk into and out of caves and doors using the native game so that the visible opening and trigger can be checked together.
3. Build and review daylight frames at 3840 × 2160 in the two isolated profiles. Check both gameplay distance and close angles. Keep animated details and background props outside the travel and formation lanes.
4. Use `install_environment_rows.py --preserve-existing` when publishing a new group. Keep existing row and shared asset bytes intact. Archive the map receipt, art recipe, source assets, and selected daylight samples before moving to the next group.
5. Style Traveler's trees like miniature trees in a model railway diorama. Preserve each species' silhouette and foliage. The coastal pines illustrate the miniature finish. Use textured meshes directly for this finish, give the branches and canopy a gentle breeze, and add only a few drifting leaves to trees in fall color. `diorama_trees.py` contains the mesh and leaf setup.
6. Give boats the same miniature diorama finish. Fit the hull to the way the boat is moored and keep fine plank seams, rails, and tie-off details legible at gameplay distance.
7. Center the frame, step, and trigger on the actual doorway. On the ordinary two-block cut, the frame and step use the cut's left edge as their origin while the fixed door slab uses its center. Keep the hinge offset and place the portal at the center of the cut. Check floor grade on both sides.

8. Reuse finished Traveler props where they fit. Props are interchangeable, including textured 3D meshes such as the quarry tents and their animated flaps. Build each map's terrain for its own layout. Fit shared props to the ground and leave entrances and gameplay lanes clear.
