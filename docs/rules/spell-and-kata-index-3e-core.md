## *Masters of Magic* (3e) — only 16 spells (indexed with `scratchpad/mom_spells.py`)
The book covers Dragon, Mantis and Phoenix magic: schools, tattoos, Kitsu, storm magic and the Isawa secrecy tiers.
It is **not** a big spell list. File: `L5R - 3E - Masters of Magic.ocr.clean.txt`.
- **Multi-element spells** (Introduction, ≈ lines 910–1008). They have no names, only element pairs. A cast uses
  **one spell slot of each element**, can't be cast alongside another spell, and there is no Void version.
  (*Prayers and Treasures* has the earlier multi-element system, which this book corrects.)
  - Air+Earth ML3 — an illusory double that can touch things.
  - Air+Fire ML2 — wind cone + flame darts: −1k1 to all actions, no ranged attacks, damage each round.
  - Air+Water ML2 — 6-month animal-companion bond.
  - Earth+Fire ML2 — summon a burning stone weapon (DR 4k3) for self or an ally.
  - Earth+Water ML2 — water wall: pushes foes back; −5 to Air and Fire attacks against you.
  - Fire+Water ML3 — 2k2 fire blast that heals you 1k1 (capped at the damage dealt).
  → In Octopath these become **"Harmony" skills** for Haruki: they need **two elements Attuned**, or one Attuned
  plus a higher SP cost, and **can't be Boosted**.
- **Phoenix First Tier secret spells** (≈ lines 8718–9000). Each is given only by an **Elemental Master** → perfect
  **story rewards in Haruki's arc** (the Council has to trust the half-souled boy).
  - Air: *Dreamwalker* ML4 (control a sleeper's dreams — Mieko scenes!) · *Vision of Comfort* ML2 (crowds see you
    as someone they trust).
  - Earth: *Fortify the Soul* ML3 (blocks crit damage on an ally, N times) · *Wandering Stone* ML5 (teleport
    anchor → field fast-travel).
  - Fire: *A Fire Divided* ML3 (HP transfer from a willing donor) · *Invigorating Flame* ML3 (a weapon buff with
    fire "charges" for big hits).
  - Water: *Frozen Tomb* ML4 (freeze a foe; ends if it takes damage) · *Find the Source* ML2 (ritual: vision of who
    cast a known spell → a mystery tool).
  - Void: *Yumi of the Void* ML1 (a bow of nothingness, power = VP spent) · *Saving Fortune* ML4 (store lucky
    rolls → banked crits).
- Appendix One covers the Brotherhood of Shinsei: monk schools, the Asako "Golden Arm" kiho technique. Read it if a
  monk guest is added (kiho.md).
