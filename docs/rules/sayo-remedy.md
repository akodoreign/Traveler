# Rules — Sayo's Talent "Remedy" (Octopath Alfyn-style concoctions) — DRAFT v0.1

Setting basis (facts only, used as flavour):
- 5e core Medicine: healing **rebalances the body's elements** through pressure points, *correspondences* and
  herbal remedies. Healing plants include **ginseng** and **rhubarb**. **Jade petal tea** slows the Shadowlands
  Taint.
- *Emerald Empire*:
  - **Kitsune Mori** is the Empire's best herb forest.
  - The Phoenix trade for **foreign herbs** at Yobanjin Mura (a Tamotsu link).
  - **Hanabi** (fireworks) are legal because they use *native herbs*. Gaijin pepper (gunpowder) is forbidden: a
    story thread.
- 3e Medicine masteries (R3/5/7/10) set the potency tiers. Herbalism lets her **forage** in the wild.
- Her mentor **Ume-baa** (eta herbalist) taught her the "low" knowledge samurai doctors won't touch → **Anatomy**
  (a Low Skill) is a late unlock.

## 1. Mechanic
- **Remedy** (battle command, no SP): pick **1 base + 1 catalyst**. A **2nd catalyst slot** opens at **Medicine 5**.
  Ingredients are consumed.
- Ingredients come from **foraging** (field: Herbalism, from rustling plants in wild maps), shops, enemy drops,
  **Pilfer**, and Tamotsu's **Barter**.
- **Element:** every ingredient has one. The base sets the *kind* of effect; catalysts set the *element*, *target*
  and *strength*. Damaging remedies carry the catalyst's element, so **Sayo can break shields with elements**, as
  Alfyn did.
- **Potency** = f(Medicine rank) · Int (stats.md). Medicine masteries: R3 +1 tier · R5 the second catalyst ·
  R7 +1 tier · R10 +1 tier and **ingredients are kept on a crit** (3e R10: "only one dose of medicine").
- Ingredients are cheap, but **Sayo is poor** (a ronin). The economy pushes her toward foraging and Pilfer:
  her identity.

## 4. Ties to story and other systems
- **Pilfer** feeds Remedy (rare ingredients from NPCs) → there is temptation in Low-Skill play.
- **Tamotsu's Barter** unlocks the Yobanjin ingredients: a reason to travel together (Unicorn+Ronin are not a
  crossroads pair; maybe a side story).
- **Haruki's *Hand of Jurojin*** covers disease; Sayo covers poison and Taint cheaply. They overlap as healers but
  differ in kind.
- **Anatomy** (Low, an eta skill): late unlock from Ume-baa → *Diagnosis*: the next Remedy on that target gets +1
  tier. A story beat for Ronin Ch4 *The Oracle's Choice* (siding with the eta).
