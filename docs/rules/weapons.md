# Rules — Weapons & Arrows (L5R 3e → Octopath) — DRAFT v0.1

Source: *L5R 3e Core*, Book of Fire equipment (OCR clean ≈ lines 19020–19700). 3e weapon damage = **DR**
"XkY" plus Strength added to the rolled dice. Octopath weapons get **base P.Atk** (scaled from the average kept
total) and a **trait**.

| Weapon (3e DR) | Users | Octopath base power (Lv 1 tier) | Trait (from the 3e special rule) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Katana** (3k2) | Rensei, Gendo, Shizue, Sayo; Haruki (Kami Weapon) | **High** (100) | **Boost bonus:** each VP spent on a Katana attack adds **+15% damage** (3e: spend Void for +1k1 on damage). Kenjutsu 5 raises the Boost cap to 4. Only samurai carry one, so Sayo's katana is a story point (ronin are samurai caste). |
| **Yari** (2k2; thrown 1k2) | Natsuko, Shizue, Tamotsu; Haruki | Medium (80) | **+25% damage vs. Large/mounted** foes and on a charge (first turn of battle). **Reach:** hits back-row enemies. |
| **Tanto** (1k1) | Rensei, Mitsue | Low (55) | **Fast:** Speed +5%. **+Pilfer/Steal odds while equipped** (3e: Free Raise on Sleight of Hand). With Knives 5: dual-tanto. |
| **Tetsubo** (0k3, needs **Str 3**) | Natsuko (Hida kit, R2); Haruki | Medium (75) | **Armour-breaker:** ignores **25% of Def** and Def buffs; extra vs. *Carapace* (Shadowlands) enemies. **Equip requires Strength 3.** |
| **Bo** (0k2) | Gendo, Haruki | Low (50) | **Guarding staff:** Evasion **+10%** while equipped (3e: +5 TN with the Bo emphasis). Weak vs. armoured foes until Staves 5. Nonlethal option ("subdue" for magistrate arrests in Gendo's arc). |
| **Yumi** (bow, Str 3; damage by arrow) | Natsuko, Mitsue, Tamotsu, Sayo | Medium (by arrow) | Ranged; hits any row; **kata are suppressed**; −accuracy when mounted. **Arrow types** → shot variants (below). |

**Arrow types → Yumi shot skills** (any Yumi user; costs a little SP; no ammo tracking):
| Arrow (3e) | Shot skill |
|---|---|
| Willow Leaf (2k2) | Normal shot (the basic Yumi attack) |
| Armour Piercer (1k2, ignores half the armour TN) | **Piercing Shot:** lower damage, ignores 50% Def |
| Flesh Cutter (2k3; armour counts double; half range) | **Barbed Shot:** big damage vs. **unarmoured** foes, poor vs. armoured; can't hit the back row |
| Rope Cutter (1k1; free Raises vs. objects) | **Cut Shot:** field — cut ropes and banners (puzzles); battle — destroys enemy props and summons |
| Humming Bulb (0k1; whistling signal) | **Signal Shot:** no damage; **Terror** chance on beasts, or **calls a guest/follower** at once (once per battle) |

**Armour** (for kata and Cut Under the Mountain tags): 3e Light (+3 TN), Heavy (+5 TN, −Agility/Reflexes skill
penalties), Riding (heavy on foot). → Octopath armour gets a **Light / Heavy** tag.
- Heavy: +Def, Speed −5%, Evasion −5%.
- Riding armour counts as Heavy, and better when mounted (Tamotsu's Unicorn gear).

Open: the power numbers are placeholders. Final weapon tiers (fine/excellent, Kakita/Akodo blades → Rank-gated
nemuranai) go in the equipment list later. 3e named blades, OCR ≈ line 12284: *Kakita Blade* (+1k1 attack, re-roll
damage once per duel); *Akodo Blade* (4k2 katana, user's Honor counts +1).
