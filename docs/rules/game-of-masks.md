# The Game of Masks (Kyuden Bayushi)

Generated by `tools/gen_sadane.py` (the one source; plugin `RokuganSadane.sk` reads `resource/sadane/sadane.json`). Do not edit by hand.

An optional side game on Shosuro Mitsue's path, open after Scorpion Ch1. Masks are the players: each has a material, a level and the barbs (sadane pokes) it knows; more barbs as it levels. Duels are played in the castle in a 3/4 view from behind Mitsue: **Barb** (the equipped mask's list), **Swap** (another mask from the sleeve; it costs the turn), **Withdraw** (strangers only).

## Rules

- **Composure** is a mask's health; at 0 it *loses face* and can't play until restored (tea from Soshi Kagero in the Entrance reception, or the guest pavilion's tea tray). Losing a duel restores every mask; nothing else is lost.
- **Wit** drives a barb, **Poise** turns it aside, **Timing** decides who speaks first.
- **Effects**: *poise-* lowers the target's Poise a step, *timing-* its Timing, *wit+* raises the wearer's Wit, *heal* restores a third of the wearer's composure. Steps go to +/-3 (x1.25 / x0.8 each).
- **Experience** goes to the mask that wins the exchange (half to masks that played earlier in the duel). Max level 20. A level that matches a barb's level teaches it.
- **Sleeve**: 4 masks are carried into a duel; the rest wait on the mask stand (guest pavilion).
- **Random duels**: walking the castle, a masked stranger sometimes steps out. Their masks are commons; 20% leave theirs behind if she doesn't have it.
- **Rivals** see her (7 m, in front of them), walk up and challenge once; afterwards they play again on a word. Gift masks and gear on the first win; zeni every time (half on a rematch); Honor +1 from three keep champions (cap 3).

## Materials

Wood fears metal, metal fears fire, fire fears water, water fears earth, earth fears wood; stone and silk fear each other. A barb of the material a mask fears does x2; a barb of the material it overcomes does x0.5.
