# Rules — The Travelers' Schools (L5R 3e → Octopath) — DRAFT v0.1

Source: *L5R 3e Core Book Revised*, Book of Water (OCR: `_research/books_txt/L5R - 3E - Core Book Revised.ocr.clean.txt`,
lines ≈ 13311–17000). Technique summaries are **paraphrased** for design use — do not copy book text into the game.
Rank thresholds = user's level bands: **R1 Lv 1 · R2 Lv 21 · R3 Lv 41 · R4 Lv 51 · R5 Lv 61** (DESIGN §4c).

General translation rules used below:
- L5R "Free Raise" → +damage/+accuracy % or a cheaper Boost.
- "Additional attack per round" → the skill gains **+1 hit at no Boost cost** (Octopath multi-hit), or a passive
  "Follow-up" chance.
- "TN to Be Hit +X" → evasion %.
- "Exploding dice" → crit-rate increase.
- "Void Point spend" → spend **Void Points (Boost)**; Void Points = Octopath VP.
- Skill-rank **mastery abilities** (ranks 3/5/7, Book of Water lines ≈10400–11600) are the unlocks on the
  every-2-levels skill track.

|---|---|---|
| R1 (1) | **Dance the Razor's Edge** — move freely in full defence; Stealth adds to TN to be hit; rations last twice as long for (Hunting) people | **Talent "Razor's Edge" stance**: guard stance that doesn't cost her turn movement; evasion + = Stealth rank. Field: inn/ration costs halved for the party. |
| R2 (21) | **Strike Like the Wind** — bonus vs. enemies who haven't hit you yet; each miss against her raises her evasion for the fight | Passive: +damage vs. enemies that haven't hit her this battle; every enemy miss stacks +evasion (cap). |
| R3 (41) | **Fire and Shadow** — double Stealth to evasion; bonus damage vs. a known enemy type (Shadowlands) | Evasion doubled; **+damage vs. Shadowlands-type enemies** = Lore: Shadowlands rank. |
| R4 (51) | **Harness the Wind** — bonuses to Athletics/Hunting/Shadowlands lore; extra attack per round | Field: Path-Action/escape bonuses; battle: her basic attack hits twice. |
| R5 (61) | **Veil of Spirits** — more exploding dice on Stealth/Hunting, more in the Shadowlands | Crit rate up; doubled in Shadowlands maps. |
- ⚠ **Design change:** the Hiruma are *evasive* scouts, not wall-like tanks. Natsuko becomes an **evasion tank**
  (draws and dodges hits) rather than an armour tank. Her "The Wall" talent → renamed **"Razor's Edge"**; a Hida
  secondary school (Rank 2) can give her the heavy-hitter tetsubo kit (Hida R2 *The Mountain Does Not Move*).

## Sayo — Ronin Schools (mix and match)
*3e rule: ronin schools have no dojo; **ronin may move freely between the ronin schools**, taking each new Rank from
whichever school suits them, as long as they hold that school's earlier techniques (e.g. Rank 3 = Yojimbo R1 or
Duelist R3). Ronin Honor 1.5.*

Options (paraphrased):
- **Ronin Warrior** (+1 Strength): R1 *The Wolf's Technique* (bonus vs. whoever targets her) · R2 *Punish the Arrogant*
  (initiative and dice vs. whoever wounded her) · R3 *Desperate Speed* (extra attack) · R4 *The Killing Strike*
  (Void-boosted damage) · R5 *None Shall Overcome* (trade rolled for kept dice).
- **Ronin Duelist** (+1 Agility): R1 *The Wind Technique* (twice Agility to Initiative) · R2 *Taking the Measure*
  (study a foe for bonus dice) · R3 *Strike Like Wind* (extra attack; counter focus) · R4 *Testing the Measure*
  (bonus dice kept) · R5 *The Final Measure*.
- **Ronin Yojimbo** (+1 Reflexes): R1 *The Way of Sacrifice* (share full-defence with an ally) · R2 *Will Not Flesh*
  (take an ally's wounds) · R3 *Hasten the Blade* · R4 *Strength of Conviction* (extra attack) · R5 *Dashed upon the
  Stones* (attack from full defence).
- (Also present: Monkey/Toku bushi — *Toku's Lesson*… — and Master of Games courtier, ronin shugenja schools.)

**Sayo's techniques → Octopath** (core ≈ lines 16806–16920):
| Rank | Warrior (damage) | Duelist (speed/crit) | Yojimbo (protector) |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | *Wolf's Technique*: +damage or accuracy vs. **any enemy that targeted her** this turn (her choice each turn) | *Wind Technique*: Speed + (2 × Agility) → acts early | *Way of Sacrifice*: when she **Defends**, she gives half her Defend bonus to an adjacent ally |
| R2 | *Punish the Arrogant*: after taking damage, a Speed bonus next turn + Accuracy vs. the one who hurt her | *Taking the Measure*: a "Study" command (usable while Defending) puts stacks on a foe; +damage per stack, max = Rank | *Will Not Flesh*: Shaken immunity while protecting; once per turn, **takes up to X damage meant for an ally** (1 VP) |
| R3 | *Desperate Speed*: +1 hit | *Strike Like Wind*: +1 hit; in duels, negates the foe's Focus bonus | *Hasten the Blade*: after a Defend turn, a big Speed bonus next turn |
| R4 | *Killing Strike*: Rank × per day, 1 VP → +damage per hit | *Testing the Measure*: Study stacks become stronger (kept dice) | *Strength of Conviction*: +1 hit |
| R5 | *None Shall Overcome*: trade accuracy for damage freely (crit-for-power slider) | *The Final Measure* (OCR garbled; duel capstone) | *Dashed upon the Stones*: 1 VP → Defend **and** attack in the same turn |

**Octopath translation — Sayo's branching tree:** at each Rank threshold the player **chooses which ronin school's next
technique** she learns (Warrior = damage-dealer, Duelist = speed/crit, Yojimbo = protector). Her **Talent "Remedy"**
(herbal concoctions, from Medicine/Herbalism skill ranks) stays constant. This makes Sayo the most customisable
traveler — fitting "wave-woman, versatility", and honours the canon rule.
