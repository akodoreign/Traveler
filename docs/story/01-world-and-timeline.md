# Story Bible — 01: World & Timeline (DRAFT v0.1)

## The setting in one paragraph
The Emerald Empire, 1122–1123 IC. Hantei XXXVIII is old and ailing; his heir Sotorii is a petulant boy. The **Lion and
Crane** are sliding into open war over old border grudges. The **Crab** hold the Kaiu Wall against the Shadowlands and
resent an Empire that forgets them. The **Scorpion** smile, and their Champion Bayushi Shoju is quietly preparing
something no one sees coming. The **Unicorn** — returned from the Burning Sands three centuries ago — are still
outsiders. The **Phoenix** keep to their libraries and their vow of peace; the **Dragon** watch from the mountains.
Under all of it, a centuries-old evil wearing a beautiful face — **Doji Nashiko**, the Bride of Fu Leng — is pulling
threads, setting clan against clan so that when the great storm breaks, the Empire will already be bleeding.

## ✅ Canon dates verified (source: *Imperial Histories*, ch. 5 "Prelude to the Scorpion Clan Coup", pp. 128–132)
- **The Coup:** Bayushi Shoju enters court on the **14th day of the Month of the Rooster, 1123**, wearing the
  Bloodsword *Ambition*, and cuts down Hantei XXXVIII; the battle for Otosan Uchi runs to the **19th**. Sotorii is
  spirited away by Phoenix and Imperial loyalists.
  - ⚠ **Calendar correction (session 2).** The editions disagree:
    - *Of Family and Honor* (1st ed.) equates the Serpent with April.
    - The **3e core** (Book of Earth, "The Seasons", OCR ≈ line 4912) starts the year with the **Hare = Sun = April**.
      Spring = Hare, Dragon, Serpent; summer = Horse, Goat, Monkey; fall = **Rooster (≈ October)**, Dog; winter =
      Boar, Rat, Ox, (Ryoshun). *The Four Winds* (3e) follows the same order.
    - **Recommendation:** use the 3e calendar, since our rules are 3e. The Coup (14th of the Rooster) is then **early
      autumn 1123** (≈ mid-October).
  → **The game spans winter 1122 → early autumn 1123.** Ch4s fall in summer (Horse–Monkey). The finale is at the end
  of the Monkey; the epilogue is the eve of the 14th of the Rooster. ✅ **Confirmed by the user (session 2):
  October.**
- **1120:** Akodo Arasou killed at Toshi Ranbo; **Akodo Toturi** recalled from the monastery and made Lion Champion
  (Matsu Tsuko despises him). ✔ Shadow of a Doubt's Winter Court at Shiro Akodo fits winter 1122.
- **1120:** **Togashi Kokujin** succumbs to Enlightened Madness and flees the Dragon lands → his tattooed peasant
  cult (Howl of the Phoenix) is *in era* — use it in the Phoenix and Dragon arcs.
- **1120:** a Lying Darkness assassin replaces the commander of Kenson Gakka with a Shosuro impostor — Lion/Crane
  relations decline. **1119:** Isawa Tadaka becomes Master of Earth; first Naga (the Shazaar) wakes.
- **1121:** Otomo Yoroshiku kidnapped by ronin of Nightingale Village — "her lineage is illegitimate" (a canon echo
  of our Ronin arc's theme); Bon Festival undead in Ryoko Owari (moon cult).
- **1122:** the **Opium War** in Ryoko Owari; the murder at Shiro Kyotei; intrigue at Shiro Suboten; **more and more
  Naga wake in the Shinomen Mori** ("demonic snake-men" rumours) ✔ matches our finale's Naga signs.
- **1123:** the **Hare Clan** destroyed by the Scorpion (Kolat scroll; the lost Hare sword found in Shiro Hiruma —
  a possible Crab-arc tie); a Lion samurai stokes **Crane–Mantis piracy** ✔ fits Crane Ch3 (White Squid); the
  **Oracle of Fire chooses an eta woman** as successor ✔ huge for the Ronin arc; a solar eclipse frees an oni in the
  Isawa Mori → the **Darkfever** plague in Phoenix lands ✔ Phoenix arc material; **Shoju visits Togashi**, who says
  "If you do this, a bloodline will end. If you succeed, the Empire will be saved."

## Map — regions the game visits
- **Crab lands:** Kyuden Hida, the Kaiu Wall, Yasuki Yashiki, Clear Water Village, a Yasuki market town (Crab Ch1), the
  Shadowlands beyond the Wall (Lava Tree forest, poppy fields, Toshi no Kijo troll ruins).
- **Crane lands:** Garden Under Shadow City (Daidoji port), Kyuden Doji (archives), Crane coast & islands (pirate arc).
- **Lion lands:** Shiro Akodo; the minor house of Akodo Hiruto on the Lion–Phoenix–Crane border; Beiden Pass and Lord
  Goroso's forest (Crane Ch1); the Village of the Wounded Lion and the Mountain of the White Snow (Ronin arc).
- **Phoenix lands:** Shiro no Kamatori ("a castle built of fire"); Honored Treaty City; Zumiki-mihari watchtower village;
  sea-cliff torii; Kyuden Isawa (libraries).
- **Scorpion lands:** Kyuden Bayushi (hedge maze, Traitor's Grove), Ryoko Owari (City of Lies), Shiro no Soshi, the
  River of Gold and Kudo (minor clans), the plains north toward Seikitsu Pass (the City of Masks).
- **Unicorn lands:** the **Ojatara Valleys** in the Spine of the World (Shiro Iuchi in its wind tunnel, Town of Buried
  Leaves, Perfume Petal Blossom, the jade mines).
- **Dragon lands:** Shiro Kitsuki; Tamori's Furnace (volcano).
- **Mantis Isles:** Isle of Silk, Tortoise Island, ruined Kyuden Gusai (Dragon Ch1).
- **Imperial:** Otosan Uchi (and the Otosan Uchi of the year 400 in the Dragon arc).
- **Shinomen Mori:** the great forest (finale).
- **Plains of Thunder:** Shrine of Osano-Wo (finale's opening battle).

## Antagonist layers
1. **Doji Nashiko** (hidden final villain). Appears in disguise in: Crab arc (as Asako Kuhime), Crane/Crab crossroads
   (as Doji Miyako & Kuni Miyubi), and is glimpsed or felt in other arcs (the oni in Shadow of a Doubt answers to her;
   the "doctor" of a key victim; the beautiful woman with the blue eyes who gives orders). Goal for 1122–23: make the
   clans bleed each other before the storm; harvest strong souls for Fu Leng.
2. **The Bloodspeaker remnant** — Iuchiban's scattered servants and relics (O-Tamai; the Iron Box; Hioninmaru-type
   cultists). Nashiko uses them as pawns; the Phoenix, Lion and Scorpion arcs confront them.
3. **Human ambition** — Kurosho's regret, Goroso's cruelty, Kumpei's resentment, Akuro's mad vision, the Imperial agent
   who keeps the clans at war, Shoju's secret. Many chapter villains are people, not monsters.

## Tone guide
- Octopath-level: tragic, sincere, occasionally funny, never gratuitous. Atrocities from the sources are **implied**
  (aftermath, testimony, a single image) rather than shown.
- Each traveler's story should be able to stand alone; the shared thread appears as small, deliberate echoes
  (the blue-eyed woman, jade burning only bare skin, scalding sake untouched).
- Rokugani values are presented sincerely; the stories question them from inside (honour vs. compassion, testimony vs.
  truth, caste vs. worth).
