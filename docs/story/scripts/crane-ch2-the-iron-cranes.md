# Script — Crane Ch2 · "The Iron Cranes" (Crossroads: Two Faces of the Border) — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *Of Family and Honor*, Round One (Hobart, 2000). Re-dated from c. 1116 to **winter 1122–23**.
Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Owner:** Kakita Rensei · Rank 1→2, Lv 20–25. **Partner bonus:** Hiruma Natsuko (after Crab Ch2). **Stand-in:**
guest **Hiruma Goro** (Crab bodyguard).
**Octopath shape (`OCTOPATH-FIT.md`):** port town → Crab town (partner) → border camp (dungeon) → boss.
- **Gimmick:** *Charmed generals* (Suki frees them).
- **Choices:** 2. The same boss on every route.

Re-dating notes:
- Young **Daidoji Uji** and **Hida Yakamo** are the right ages for 1122.
- The Emerald Magistrates are replaced by Lady **Doji Sanae**'s quiet mission and the Crab karo's equivalent.
- Nashiko's two faces: **Doji Miyako** (a white-haired Crane courtier) and **Kuni Miyubi** (a black-ponytailed Kuni
  apprentice).

## Scene 1 — "Garden Under Shadow"
**Where/When:** Garden Under Shadow City (Daidoji port; tidal land-bridge to Crab lands), a lantern-garden dinner
**Beat:** Sanae is sent to stop a Crane–Crab border war. Every Daidoji general hangs on the words of a dazzling
courtier, **Doji Miyako**. The painter **Kakita Yumi** is sharply jealous of her. The Lion envoy **Ikoma Tadaka**
catches Miyako in a lie: she says "Doji Moteuchi" died on the *northern* border. Minutes later Tadaka is accused of
assaulting her and **cut down in an instant duel**.
- **Charm** targets: Kakita Yumi (she'll talk about Miyako); General Daidoji Shinro's wife **Eriko** (her presence
  keeps him honest; Charm her into staying close to him).
- Rensei sees the duel from the far end of the garden. Rank 1: he can't intervene in time.
RENSEI: *(after, shaking, to Sanae)* He was right. She *lied*. I heard it.
LADY SANAE: So did I. And a man is dead for hearing it. *(beat)* Say nothing yet, Rensei-san. Remember everything.
`[FLAG tadaka_lie_heard]`

## Scene 2 — "Scalding Sake" (partner bonus: Natsuko's side; stand-in Hiruma Goro)
**Where/When:** Yasuki Yashiki (across the land-bridge), a Crab dinner
**Beat:** A black-ponytailed Kuni apprentice, **Kuni Miyubi**, has every Hida general's ear. The Crab serve sake
*scalding hot* as a dare. **Miyubi drinks it without flinching**: her tell. Two Hida generals wrestle over a remark;
**Sumai** with the loser shames him into confessing the secret **Crab raid on an Asahina estate**. **Kuni Akuja**
(one-handed, burned, jealous) shows "maho relics" that Miyubi "found" there.
NATSUKO: *(watching Miyubi drain the cup)* Nobody drinks Crab sake like that. Not even Crab.
HIRUMA GORO *(stand-in)*: *(same beat)* I've seen a troll flinch at that sake.
`[SYS]` **Sumai** (partner) or **Charm** (Rensei, via a letter carried by Goro) produces the confession.

## Scene 3 — "Same Method Twice" (the crossroads deduction)
**Beat:** Back together (or by letters, with the stand-in): two courtiers, two clans, the same seduction of generals,
the same planted evidence. **Nashiko's vanity: she reuses her method.**
`[CHOICE 1]` Who goes to which camp at the **border conference**?
- *Rensei with Uji, Natsuko with Yakamo:* each camp's dialogue is better.
- *Both together:* fewer clues, but the banter bonus "Two faces, one woman" unlocks.
GENDO-STYLE NARRATION (if Gendo is in the party): "Two crimes with one fingerprint. Courts would call it
coincidence. I call it a signature."

## Scene 4 — "A Night of Tents" (dungeon)
**Where/When:** the border conference camp, night
**Beat:** Miyako/Miyubi visits **three Crane generals' tents and one Crab's**, provoking both armies to mobilise for a
"pre-emptive" strike at dawn. The camp is the dungeon: tents, sentries, and seduced guards.
- `[BATTLE]` Seduced sentries (weak to **Katana**, **Bo**; they wake up confused when broken).
- **Path actions in the camp:** Rensei *Charms* a Crane sentry into letting him pass; Natsuko *Sumai*s a Crab one.
`[CHOICE 2]` At **Daidoji Uji**'s tent: *wake him and argue* (Etiquette; he respects nerve) · *show him Tadaka's lie*
(needs `tadaka_lie_heard`; stronger). Hida Yakamo, blunt, is already half open to "an outside hand".

## Scene 5 — BOSS: "The Iron Cranes"
`[BATTLE]` **Bewitched Daidoji generals ×3** (and a Hida general if Natsuko is present).
- **Gimmick: Charmed generals.** Each is shielded by Miyako's hold. Damaging them *fuels* her (she heals between
  turns). **Breaking each general's Resolve (Suki) frees him** from the hold instead.
- Win condition: all generals freed (or defeated, with a worse epilogue line).
- Generals: Resolve 6 each. Weak: **Yari**, **Tanto**, **Water** (cold water wakes the charmed: a Remedy trick if
  Sayo is present).
DAIDOJI GENERAL (charmed): She said the Crab would come at dawn. She said only I could save her.
RENSEI: *(Iaijutsu: Focus)* She says that to everyone, general. I heard her say it twice tonight.

## Scene 6 — "Six Doors" (confrontation, no battle)
**Where/When:** Miyako's chamber in Garden Under Shadow City
**Beat:** Six floating portals open, each showing a child that someone in the room loves (Rensei: his younger
cousins; Natsuko: Squeak and the nezumi young). "Serve, and they live." **The travelers refuse.** Miyako laughs and
leaves through the smoke, flying north. (She doesn't fight: she never risks herself.)
DOJI MIYAKO: *(radiant, sincere-sounding)* I bargain honestly, you know. Ask anyone I've owned.
NATSUKO / RENSEI: *(together, or Rensei alone)* No.
DOJI MIYAKO: *(delighted)* Two of you in one night. I *do* love a pair.
`[FLAG same_woman_proven]` → the first certain proof that the travelers are chasing one woman (finale lead-in).

## Scene 7 — Ending
- Generals confess; seppuku for the most compromised. One Crab general, **Hida Chuto**, goes into the Shadowlands
  "looking for Hida" instead.
- Sanae writes a report that blames "a Scorpion provocateur" (a true lie: **Bayushi Depura** was there too,
  `depura_seen`).
- **Partner bonus reward:** Natsuko and Rensei exchange a token (a Kakita sake cup / a Hiruma map scrap). Banter:
  "The Crab who can't bow and the Crane who can't stop."
→ Crane **Ch3 "The White Squid"**.
