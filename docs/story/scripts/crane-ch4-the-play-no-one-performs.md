# Script — Crane Ch4 · "The Play No One Performs" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** orig., built on the *Mirror, Mirror* facts about Doji Nashiko (05-chapter-outlines). **All dialogue is
original.**
**Traveler:** Kakita Rensei · Rank 3, Lv 41–45 · **summer 1123 (Horse/Goat)** · Kyuden Doji.
**Octopath shape:** Kyuden Doji (hub) → the archives (dungeon) → the secret rehearsal (set-piece) → the formal duel
(boss).
- **Set-piece:** the rehearsal.
- **Choices:** 2.
- **Timing:** Lion Ch4 (Sorrow's Path) happens the same weeks. If Shizue is in the party, Rensei crosses the ford in
  her chapter on his way here.

---

## Scene 1 — "Bury It"
**Beat:** Lady **Doji Sanae** orders Rensei to help bury a terrible truth. The woman behind the Crab–Crane crisis
was **a Doji**, and the same woman, **Doji Nashiko**, destroyed the court of the Emerald Champion **Doji Komatsu**
four centuries ago. The Crane cannot have a demon in the family tree.
LADY SANAE: Find everything the archives hold on her, Rensei-san. Then we will decide what the archives held.

## Scene 2 — "The Archives" (dungeon)
**Beat:** A library maze; censor-monks; wards against reading. Enemies: paper-spirits (weak to **Fire**, **Tanto**)
and a guardian kami of the forbidden shelves.
- **Charm** the head archivist, or use *Watchful Falcon* (the kata; it lets Rensei spot hidden shelves).
- **Discovery:** a banned **noh play**, ***The Empress of Nothing***, written by a survivor of Komatsu's court. It
  names her weaknesses:
  - her colour-blindness (the stone-for-jade scene)
  - her terror of disfigurement
  - her hunger for the title **"Empress"**
  - she reads **one mind at a time**
  - **She doesn't know the play exists.**
`[SYS]` Key item: **the play script**. In the finale, each scene Rensei knows reveals one of Nashiko's shields from
the start.

## Scene 3 — "Will It Work?" (set-piece: the rehearsal)
**Beat:** Rensei secretly recruits court actors to stage the play's key scene, the "mirror trick" that made Nashiko
drop her guard. If **Mitsue** is present, she directs (a crossroads beat).
`[SYS]` The rehearsal is **3 timing prompts and 2 dialogue picks**: cue the mirror, cue the false jade, speak the
title. A good rehearsal unlocks an extra finale debuff ("Empress" bait: she drops her guard for 1 turn).
MITSUE (if present): *(from the back of the hall)* No, no. When you call her "Empress", *mean* it. She can tell
flattery from worship.
RENSEI: How do you know?
MITSUE: Because I can.

## Scene 4 — "My Father's Answer" (Choice 1)
**Beat:** His father, **Kakita Masanori**, learns of the play. The Kakita name must never be tied to a Doji demon. He
orders the play burned.
`[CHOICE 1]` *Hand the play over* (Sanae saves a copy in secret; the finale debuffs are halved) · *refuse* (leads
to the formal duel; canonical).

## Scene 5 — BOSS: "First Blood" (formal duel)
`[BATTLE]` **Kakita Masanori** (formal, non-lethal, first blood):
- Phase 1: an iaijutsu duel exchange. He has more Focus than Rensei; *One Strike Blade* gives him +Speed.
- Phase 2: kata. *Kakita's First Stance* (he uses Honor) and *Watchful Falcon*.
- Resolve 14. Weak: **Yari**, **Tanto**, **Air**. *(No extra gimmick: the rehearsal was the set-piece.)*
- The win condition is "first blood": Rensei wins when his father is **broken (Suki)** once *and* takes a crit. If
  Rensei loses, the story continues with Choice 1's other path.
KAKITA MASANORI: Your left foot still hesitates.
RENSEI: *(Strike)* Yes, Father. *(beat)* It's waiting for me to decide. *(the cut lands)*

## Scene 6 — "A Very Dull Sword" (ending)
`[CHOICE 2]` Sanae asks whether he'd keep the House's honour or save the Empire. *The Empire* · *the House* (both
answers end the same way; she was only watching how he chose).
LADY SANAE: *(burning a *blank* scroll in the brazier for the watching court)* There. The play is destroyed.
*(handing Rensei the real one under her sleeve)* A sword that only cuts what it's told to is a very dull sword,
Rensei-san.
- Masanori says nothing. That night he leaves a new hilt-wrap on Rensei's sword: the first gift he has ever given.
→ **Finale.**
`[SYS]` **Rewards:**
- Lv ~45.
- The play (a finale key item; `play_known_scenes` 1–3).
- His father's hilt-wrap (+crit for Iaijutsu Strike).
- *One Strike Blade* kata at Lv 51.
