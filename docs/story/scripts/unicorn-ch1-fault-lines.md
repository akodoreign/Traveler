# Script — Unicorn Ch1 · "Fault Lines" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *Fortune's Lost* Part I (Brandes & Hepler, 1999). Fits the era as written. **Toned down:** atrocities are
implied, never shown. Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Ide Tamotsu · **Rank 1, Status 3–4** · Lv 1–8 · **late autumn 1122** · the Ojatara Valleys (between
Shiro no Soshi and Shiro Iuchi).
**Systems introduced:**
- path action **Barter**; Talent **Barter Coin** (money as a weapon)
- the **case-file** investigation (grievances)
- Status as access: Tamotsu gets audiences others can't
- guest **Akodo Rin** (the tribunal escort)

---

## Scene 1 — "A Seat at the Small Table" (prologue)
**Where/When:** the Welcome Road inn, the Town of Buried Leaves (neutral ground)
**Cast:** Tamotsu; Unicorn Emerald Magistrate **Shinjo Tsune** (haunted by a ghost of someone he failed to
convict); Scorpion Emerald Magistrate **Shosuro Norie**; Persimmon the pony
**Beat:** The Emerald Champion has called a **war-crimes tribunal** over the unsanctioned Iuchi–Soshi war. There are
three magistrate teams. Tamotsu is the junior negotiator on Tsune's team: well liked, welcome at every table,
listened to at almost none.
SHINJO TSUNE: You'll take the refugees, Tamotsu. They like you. Everybody likes you. It's unbearable.
TAMOTSU: *(laughing)* I'm Ide, Tsune-sama. Being liked is our only weapon. I keep it sharp.
*(Persimmon bites a Scorpion yojimbo. Tamotsu apologises with a gift. The yojimbo leaves charmed and bleeding.)*
`[SYS]` **Status** tutorial: Tamotsu (Status 3–4) can request audiences from daimyo karo directly, where Rank-1
travelers would be turned away (`02-travelers` starting-status rule).

## Scene 2 — "The Camps" (Barter tutorial)
**Where/When:** refugee camps outside both castles
**Beat:** Thousands of displaced farmers. **Barter** tutorial: trade for rice and blankets from wary merchants
(Commerce gates the price). Give them to the hungry, or don't. The game remembers (`[FLAG camps_fed]`, Compassion
seed).
REFUGEE GRANDMOTHER: The Iuchi burned our village. The Soshi burned the next one. Which one shall I hate, my lord?
TAMOTSU: *(kneeling in the mud to hand her a rice sack; a samurai shouldn't)* Hate me, grandmother. I'm the one
who's late.

## Scene 3 — "The Case Files" (investigation hub)
**Beat:** Eight grievances on a case board. **Ch1 covers three as the tutorial**; the rest roll into Ch2.
1. **Assassins killed the Iuchi guard captain and the daimyo's wife.** The assassins bear Soshi shadow-brands.
   But who sent them?
2. **Salted fields at Hafuna.** An Iuchi general, born a Shinjo: "Wars are for winning."
3. **The Temple of Jizo at Motobu, destroyed.** The Soshi hid soldiers in it; the Iuchi blew it up.
- **Courts:** at **Shiro Iuchi** (triangular, built into a wind tunnel), the daimyo **Iuchi Daiyu**, "touched by the
  Sun", talks to woodchucks and has been kept ignorant of the war by his karo **Iuchi Hiroichi**. Also here: his
  nephew **Iuchi Karasu**, porcelain mask, glowing red eyes, back from months as a Shadowlands prisoner, and
  deadpan. At **Shiro no Soshi**: buffoonish daimyo **Soshi Bantaro**, and his wife **Shinobu**, the real brains.
IUCHI DAIYU: *(to a woodchuck)* He's a nice one. He brought you a carrot.
TAMOTSU: I brought you one too, my lord.
IUCHI DAIYU: *(delighted)* Nobody ever brings me carrots. They bring me reports. *(beat)* The reports are lies,
aren't they?
IUCHI KARASU: *(behind them)* Mostly. The carrots are real.
`[SYS]` Testimony mechanic (from the source: **testimony by Glory beats evidence**). Each grievance needs *a witness
of enough Glory* plus evidence. Tamotsu's Status lets him bring low-Glory witnesses to a high table, once per case.

## Scene 4 — "Old Miyahana's Cottage"
**Where/When:** a cottage in the hills
**Beat:** Tamotsu visits his old sensei, **Shinjo Miyahana**: kind, old, frightened. She trained two pupils in the
same years: Tamotsu, and a peasant boy named **Naohida**. Naohida hasn't written in a year.
SHINJO MIYAHANA: He always asked why. Why must the farmer bow? Why does the lord eat first? I told him the answers.
He didn't like them.
TAMOTSU: I didn't like them either, sensei.
SHINJO MIYAHANA: No. But you smiled while you disliked them. He never learned to smile.

## Scene 5 — "Buried Leaves" (the bombing)
**Where/When:** the Welcome Road inn, night
**Beat:** A **gunpowder bomb** destroys half the inn during the tribunal's welcome dinner. Gaijin pepper:
illegal, unthinkable. Tamotsu pulls people from the rubble (an event battle vs. fire and falling beams: an
item-heavy rescue). A **pigeon** arrives with a crude threat: *"Stop judging the dead. Judge the living."*
`[BATTLE]` *(event)* "Rubble rescue": save 4 NPCs within 6 turns. Fire hazard spreads; items and heals keep the
NPCs alive.
- **Guest: Akodo Rin** (the tribunal's Lion escort, raised as the "son" her father wanted) joins after the rescue.
AKODO RIN: That powder came from bird droppings. Someone's been mining pigeon caves.
IUCHI HIROICHI: *(offering his gaijin telescope)* Yotomo. The pigeon breeders' village. Look north.

## Scene 6 — "The Pigeon Cave" (dungeon)
**Where/When:** cliffs above Yotomo; the guerrillas' powder cave
**Beat:** A cave full of saltpeter vats, pigeons and nervous peasant guerrillas making gunpowder. **Fire is
dangerous here:** Fire attacks risk an explosion. Haruki's Fire spells are a trap; Sayo's hanabi powder even more so.
`[BATTLE]` Guerrilla peasants (they surrender easily; Tamotsu's **Settle the Account** pays them off).
`[SYS]` "Powder kegs" on the field: any Fire hit sets one off (big AoE that hurts both sides).

## Scene 7 — BOSS: "Naohida"
**Beat:** Their leader is **Naohida**, Tamotsu's fellow student, with a gaijin dao and a burning hatred of both
castles.
NAOHIDA: Look at you. Silk and a pony. They gave you a seat at the table, Tamotsu. They gave me a grave to dig.
TAMOTSU: Then come and sit in my seat. I'll stand. I've got good legs.
NAOHIDA: *(almost laughing, then not)* You always talk. Talk to this.
`[BATTLE]` **Naohida + powder guerrillas**:
- Naohida: Resolve 7. Weak: **Yari**, **Yumi**, **Water** (water spoils the powder).
- He throws **powder bombs** (big Fire AoE; they can set off kegs).
- **Barter Coin** can buy off his guerrillas mid-battle. They leave, and he's left alone.
*(At low HP Naohida throws a final bomb at the cave mouth and escapes in the smoke.)*

## Scene 8 — "Judge the Living" (ending)
**Beat:** The tribunal resumes in a half-burned inn. Tamotsu has three grievances' worth of testimony, a bomb-maker's
name he can't bring himself to give, and a woodchuck-loving lord who has finally started asking questions.
`[CHOICE]` *name Naohida to the tribunal* (truthful; Honor +, but a manhunt begins) · *say the bomber "escaped
unidentified"* (a lie; Honor −, and Naohida trusts him later)
`[FLAG naohida_named]` → Ch2 (and how Naohida's mother receives Tamotsu).
SHINJO TSUNE: You knew him.
TAMOTSU: *(quietly)* I sold him rice cakes for a copper when we were nine. He paid in pebbles. I took them.
→ **Ch2 "The Bell Without a Clapper."**
