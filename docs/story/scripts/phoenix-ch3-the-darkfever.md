**Sources:**

  forgotten Isawa estate, and it spreads the Darkfever
- orig. (05-chapter-outlines)

Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.

Mori estate.
**Octopath shape:** the city (hub) → plague villages (path actions) → the forest (dungeon) → the estate (mid-boss)
→ boss → the sealed library (reward).

## Scene 1 — "The Sun Goes Out"
**Beat:** An eclipse over Kyuden Isawa. Every kami in the city shrieks at once. Within days there are fever villages
in the forest. The two murders at the Silent Court still point at **Mieko**, who has left the court.

## Scene 2 — "Fever Roads" (path-action hub)
**Beat:** Plague villages. **Every sick NPC is a path-action target:**
- **Bless** (Theology): lead the sick to the temple-hospital
- **Remedy** (Sayo) or **Path to Inner Peace / Hand of Jurojin** (Haruki): ease them
Treating enough of them (5 of 8) unlocks the forest road. Untreated ones die in the epilogue's list of names.
- `[SYS]` **Story grant:** *Hand of Jurojin* (ML4 cure disease) is taught early by a dying temple healer.
DYING HEALER: *(pressing a scroll into his hands)* It won't cure this. Nothing cures this. But it'll teach you what
"cure" means. *(beat)* Go and find the thing that started it.
`[CHOICE 1]` A village elder begs Haruki to stay and keep treating. *Stay one more day* (more NPCs live; the boss
gains +1 Resolve, because the oni grows stronger) · *go now*.

## Scene 3 — "The Council's Hubris"
**Beat:** The Elemental Masters blame "peasant uncleanliness". **Isawa Rujo** (Master of Earth; canon: forced to
abdicate soon) waves Haruki away. Only a tired Crane, **Doji Hayato** *(orig.; an Emerald magistrate's yoriki)*,
listens. Haruki becomes his guide: the "clever magistrates" of canon.
DOJI HAYATO: The Masters say it's dirt.
HARUKI: Dirt doesn't spread in a circle.
DOJI HAYATO: …Show me the circle.

## Scene 4 — "The Circle" (map reveal)
**Beat:** **Reflective Pool** (a field spell) or Hayato's maps: the infection spreads outward from one point in the
forest. Haruki recognises it: the **Mieko family's forest house**, where he broke her soul when they were eight. That
night, his Void outburst **cracked the oni's seal**.

## Scene 5 — "The Forest Estate" (dungeon + mid-boss)
**Beat:** Rotting gardens; a child's go board left mid-game; ghostly replays of that night (non-interactive
flashbacks at 3 points). **Mieko's dark half** is here, drawn to the place of her breaking.
`[BATTLE]` **Mieko's dark half** (mid-boss): Resolve 9. Weak: **Water**, **Void**. She flees at 50%, laughing that he
"broke more than me".
`[CHOICE 2]` If Haruki kept the "Consumer" scroll (Ch2): *show it to her* (her own warning, in her own hand. She
hesitates and doesn't flee at once: one free turn for the party; Ch4 talk-down +) · *don't*.

## Scene 6 — BOSS: "Oni no Kuzushi"
`[BATTLE]` **Oni no Kuzushi** (canon name; it keeps it):
- **Gimmick: Unbalancing Breath.** Every 2 turns it breathes on one traveler, who becomes **Off-Balance** and loses
  their next turn. It **regains 2 Resolve** each time. Counterplay: *Mountain's Feet* (immunity), Defend, a guest
  call, or swapping order with Command/Speed of Flame.
- Resolve 14 (+1 if you stayed in Choice 1). Weak: **Jade**, **Water**, **Bo**.
- The Darkfever aura applies Poison each turn; Water heals cleanse it.
ONI NO KUZUSHI: *(a sound like a body falling down stairs)* You opened my door, little Void. Twelve years ago. I
waited so politely.
HARUKI: *(Drawing the Void → Boosted Path to Inner Peace, mass)* Then I'll close it. *(beat)* Politely.

## Scene 7 — "The Sealed Library" (reward)
**Beat:** The Council grudgingly opens the sealed archive to the boy who ended the plague. Haruki reads:
- how souls are split and joined
- how the **Snake Clan** was destroyed by a Shuten Doji, and what **obsidian, jade and crystal** do to them. This is
  the Unicorn crossroads hook. *Obsidian Taints its user* (user ruling).
The Master of Air grants him the First Tier secret spell ***Dreamwalker***.
- **Ending:** Hayato's report credits "the Isawa". Haruki's name isn't in it. He's relieved. He knows what Ch4 will
  cost.
→ **Ch4 "Howl."**
`[SYS]` **Rewards:**
- Lv ~35.
- *Hand of Jurojin*, *Dreamwalker*.
- Doji Hayato (a recurring NPC).
- The list of the saved and the lost (an epilogue roll).
