# Script — Phoenix Ch4 · "Howl" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *Howl of the Phoenix* (ending), **rebuilt**: the split-soul villain is our Mieko, and the "Who am I?"
question at the climax is hers. Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Isawa Haruki · Rank 3, Lv 41–45 · **summer 1123** · the Phoenix coast, the red sea-cliff torii.
**Octopath shape:** the temple town (hub) → the cliff path (dungeon) → the dream (set-piece) → the torii (boss) →
the choice.
- **Set-piece:** **Dreamwalker**: Haruki walks inside Mieko's split dream.
- **Choices:** 2.
- **Rules payoff:** Ch4 cost and reward. **−1 Void** and the passive **"Whole"** (Water heals cleanse 1 debuff;
  *Drawing the Void* twice per battle). User-approved.

---

## Scene 1 — "The Book"
**Beat:** The Phoenix are copying an old book of **Bloodspeaker-hunting secrets** for every clan: after the Silent
Court and the Darkfever, the Council finally wants allies. Mieko's dark half wants it: with it, she would know every
ward against her kind. She steals it in the night and flees toward the **red sea-cliff torii**.
ASAKO KEIKI: She'll burn it there. Or she'll read it, and then there's no ward left that can hold her.
HARUKI: She'll do neither. *(beat)* She'll wait for me. She's been waiting twelve years.

## Scene 2 — "The Cliff Path" (dungeon)
**Beat:** A storm-lashed cliff path of shrines. The enemies are Mieko's "shadows" (fragments of her dark half: weak
to **Water**, **Void**; they mimic Haruki's own spells).
- Ghostly echoes of the gentle Mieko along the path: she tends flowers and hums. Each echo is a **Bless** interaction
  that strengthens the Ch4 talk-down.

## Scene 3 — "Inside the Dream" (set-piece: Dreamwalker)
**Beat:** At the last shrine Mieko's body lies asleep. Haruki casts ***Dreamwalker*** (First Tier, Ch3).
`[SYS]` **The dream is the set-piece:** a short walk through the night they were eight.
- The children's go board; the jealous playmate; the Void lashing out (Haruki's own child-self, frozen mid-scream).
- The **two Miekos** live in the dream on two sides of a crack: the gentle one in a garden, the dark one in the ash.
- Neither remembers the other.
- Three dream interactions: *show the gentle Mieko the warning scroll* (`mieko_midnight_seen` / the Consumer scroll);
  *show the dark Mieko the child's apology note* (Phoenix Ch1); *stand where the crack began*.
CHILD-HARUKI *(frozen, an echo)*: I'm sorry I'm sorry I'm sorry I didn't mean to—
HARUKI: *(kneeling beside him)* I know. *(beat)* You don't have to say it anymore. I'll say it for both of us.

## Scene 4 — BOSS: "Mieko's Dark Half"
**Beat:** He wakes at the torii. Her dark half stands on the cliff edge with the book: black glass eyes, Void
crackling.
`[BATTLE]` **Mieko (dark half)**, a **Void-element** fight:
- Resolve 16. Weak: **Water**, **Bo**, **Void** (*Void Strike* steals her charge). She uses Void spells that **cost
  her VP**, mirroring Haruki: a duel of two Void economies.
- She *drains* VP from the party (*Essence of Void*-style). Kharmic Intent and Drawing the Void are the counters.
- **No extra gimmick** (the dream was the set-piece).
- **Final phase is a choice** (below), not damage. At 0 HP she drops to her knees; the fight ends.
MIEKO (DARK): You broke me in half. Now you'll finish the job? *(laughing)* Do it. It's what Void does.
`[CHOICE 1]` *Strike the final blow* · ***Join the halves***. **Octopath-style fake choice:** picking *Strike*
makes Haruki raise his staff and stop ("…No. Not again."), and the game moves to *Join*. Either way Haruki pours his
own Void into the crack. (A dark ending would break the chapter's arc; the choice exists so the player can *feel*
the temptation.)

## Scene 5 — "Whole"
**Beat:** Joining the halves costs Haruki part of his own Void. The two Miekos become one, fragile, remembering
*everything*.
- `[SYS]` **Permanent:** Haruki's Void stat −1; he gains the passive **"Whole"**.
MIEKO: *(one voice now, very quiet)* …Who am I?
HARUKI: *(exhausted, smiling)* I don't know. I think you get to decide. I'm sorry it took me twelve years to give it
back.
`[CHOICE 2]` Mieko's answer: *"Someone who doesn't forgive you yet."* (canonical, and honest) · *"Someone who
remembers you were eight."* (gentler). Both are fine; the Octopath rule means the ending beat stays the same.

## Scene 6 — Ending
**Beat:** Mieko lives, fragile and unforgiving, and alive. She gives the book back to Haruki, with one page marked:
a warning about "the Bride of Fu Leng who wears faces", in Mieko's *own* old handwriting (her good half had been
investigating Nashiko before she was broken).
MIEKO: You'll need this more than I do. *(beat)* Bring it back. Then we'll talk.
- At dawn, by the torii, two wolves howl, one white and one black (an echo of the source's epilogue), and then only
  one.
→ **Finale.**
`[SYS]` **Rewards:**
- Lv ~45.
- **"Whole"** passive; Void −1.
- The Bloodspeaker-hunting book (a finale key item: one of Nashiko's shields starts revealed).
- *Void Strike* / *Armor of the Emperor* are on the recommended picks (`haruki-spells.md` §4).
