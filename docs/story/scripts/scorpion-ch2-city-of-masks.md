# Script — Scorpion Ch2 · "City of Masks" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *City of Masks* (anonymous), using its **Appendix B** version: the masks are a sentient soul-parasite, and
there is no Lying Darkness.
- **Re-dated:** the city was sealed in dreams centuries ago and falls back into the world in winter 1122.
- **Softened:** no torn-apart bodies; the stitched faces are shown as cloth masks sewn on.
- Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Shosuro Mitsue · Rank 1→2, Lv 20–25 · **winter 1122–23** · the plains north of Shiro no Soshi.
**Octopath shape:** rest-stop town → the city (hub + dungeon districts) → the Temple District → boss.
- **Set-piece:** the **mask gate** (the city's NPCs ignore the unmasked; masks drain SP).
- **Choices:** 2.

---

## Scene 1 — "Rumours on the Road"
**Beat:** Travellers swear that a city appeared on the plains *overnight*: a city on no map. Rumours (an
Octopath-style NPC chatter list): it was built in one night; its people are faceless; the Emperor's lost heir hides
there; "being there is like watching a play: they talk to each other, never to you."
- **Rest stop:** the too-hospitable Unicorn hot-spring village **Hisatu Kensu**, where nobody will speak of the city.
  That night every party member has a **mask dream** (a faceless monk; a smith forging your face).
MITSUE: *(waking, touching her own face)* …Still mine. *(beat)* Probably.

## Scene 2 — "Meant for You"
**Beat:** On the road, a **dying Lion scout** presses a **red-and-black mask** into Mitsue's hands ("It's meant for
you") along with travel papers bearing the chop of a lord dead three hundred years. The mask is warm. (It holds the
soul of **Soshi Giseiki**, the shugenja who sealed the city.)
`[SYS]` Key item: **Giseiki's mask**. Equipping it lets Giseiki speak in field banter (a ghostly second voice).

## Scene 3 — "The Faceless Are Not Answered" (the mask gate)
**Where/When:** the city's gate and the Hospitality District
**Beat:** Everyone wears a mask and dresses three centuries out of date. They order sake and never drink it. **The
unmasked are ignored.** NPCs won't respond to talk or path actions until the party wears masks.
- **Getting masks:** the blacksmith **Kaiu Naname**, the Badger carver **Ichiro Itami** (a cultist), a tattooist,
  a silk seller. Each mask is an equipment accessory with a material.
- `[SYS]` **Mask set-piece rules** (this chapter's single mechanic):
  - In the city, an equipped mask **drains 5% max SP per "hour"** (each district visit). The clock pushes the
    chapter forward.
  - **Material reactions** (from the source): citizens respond to mask materials. Wood fears metal; metal fears fire,
    and so on. The *right* mask makes an NPC talk; the wrong one makes them flee. This is a dialogue-gate puzzle
    with 4 key NPCs.
  - Remove a mask at a shrine (a save point) to restore SP. Never stay masked too long.
MASKED SAKE-SELLER: *(to the unmasked Mitsue, looking straight through her)* I have nothing to say to the faceless.
*(Mitsue puts on a lacquered mask.)*
MASKED SAKE-SELLER: *(warmly)* Ah! Welcome, welcome. You've been away so long.

## Scene 4 — "The Districts" (investigation)
**Beat:** The missing magistrate teams. Across the Merchant and Temple districts:
- **Yasuki Kasamashi:** a con man selling fake-jade trinkets; he wears a *real* jade mask.
- **Togashi Sumi:** a riddler with a tattooed face. "You seek Master Giseiki? He's on the face of the one who wears
  him."
- **Kakita Yuko:** a 12-year-old puppeteer whose puppets have the party's faces.
- **Miya Hatomu:** an old cartographer, disgraced for mapping a city that "didn't exist". He's here, vindicated
  and weeping.
- Set piece (softened): a citizen collapses in the market, and the masked crowd steps around him without noticing.
  Mitsue notices.
`[SYS]` **Eavesdrop** on 3 masked pairs gives the history: centuries ago the masks began to *eat* their wearers.
Giseiki bound every soul into a mask to stop them spreading, and hid the whole city in the Realm of Dreams.

## Scene 5 — "The Shadow Hunters"
**Beat:** Black-clad survivors led by the governor **Shosuro Osuko** (white porcelain mask; she owns the Sweet Cup
sake house) reveal themselves. They have hunted the mask-cult's monks for three hundred years of dream-time.
SHOSURO OSUKO: You're a Shosuro. *(studying Mitsue's mask)* No: you're several. Good. We'll need all of you.
`[CHOICE 1]` *Give Giseiki's mask to Osuko* (she performs the counter-ritual; safer for Mitsue) · *wear it
yourself* (Giseiki speaks through Mitsue, and the ritual is stronger, but she risks losing herself; key banter, and
a −1 Void "scar" until the chapter ends).
`[FLAG mitsue_wore_giseiki]`

## Scene 6 — "The Temple District" (dungeon)
**Beat:** Monks with **cloth faces sewn over their own** guard the Monastery of the Silent Self. They try to rip off
the party's masks.
- `[BATTLE]` Stitched-face monks: Resolve 4, weak to **Tanto**, **Fire**. **Break → their sewn face tears free**
  (the soul inside escapes) and they collapse, freed.

## Scene 7 — BOSS: "Abbot Munashii"
**Beat:** The abbot **Munashii**, a retired Akodo, prays to a vast mask-shaped shadow to let the masks out into the
Empire.
`[BATTLE]` **Abbot Munashii**:
- Resolve 12. **Only crystal (item) hurts him fully**; other attacks do half (`enemies.md` spirit-touched). Also
  weak to **Katana**, **Fire**, **Air**.
- Phase 2: he puts on his true mask and *copies the party's buffs*.
- No extra gimmick (the masks set-piece is the chapter's one).
ABBOT MUNASHII: A mask is a mercy, child. No more being anyone. No more having to *choose* who.
MITSUE: *(Many Faces: Steal)* Choosing is the only part that's mine.
`[CHOICE 2]` *Free the city* (Giseiki's ritual releases every soul: the city fades at dawn, and Hatomu's map becomes
true history) · *seal it again* (it slips back into dreams; safer, sadder; Osuko's people remain trapped).

## Scene 8 — Ending
**Beat:** At dawn the plain is empty: or a dream of a city, fading. Miya Hatomu holds his map to the light.
MIYA HATOMU: Thirty years they laughed. *(laughing himself, crying)* I'll never prove it now. I don't care. I *saw*
it.
MITSUE: *(taking off her last mask, checking her face in a puddle)* …Still mine.
