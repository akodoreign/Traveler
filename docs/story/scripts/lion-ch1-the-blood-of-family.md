# Script — Lion Ch1 · "The Blood of Family" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *The Blood of Family* (Hanrahan, 2000). Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Akodo Shizue · **Rank 1**, Lv 1–8 · **late autumn 1122** · Akodo Hiruto's castle on the
Lion–Phoenix–Crane border.
**Crossroads:** shares Scenes 7–8 with **Phoenix Ch1** (Haruki). If both are in the party, the dialogue merges.
**Systems introduced:**
- Talent **Command**; path action **Duel**
- the **spirit-wasteland** map debuff (spells −1 tier)
- the **jade-chain compass** (hot/cold search)
- a three-route climax (DESIGN: Octopath-style flags)
- guests: **Akodo Ijako** (sodan-seizo), **Shiba Ashimita**

---

## Scene 1 — "Smoke on the Magonojo" (prologue)
**Where/When:** the Magonojo river at night
**Beat:** Shizue's cousin **Akodo Gusai** (hot-headed, Matsu-trained) leads a raid on Phoenix warehouses that were
secretly supplying the Crane army. Shizue is the most junior officer. She is told to hold the horses, and she does.
`[BATTLE]` Tutorial: warehouse guards. Teaches **Command**: give an ally an extra action, or move them up the turn
order.
*(Flames. Across the river, a small roadside shrine is being smashed by Gusai's men: orders from his cousin
**Akodo Ozaki**, who wants this war badly.)*
SHIZUE: Gusai! The shrine is not a warehouse!
AKODO GUSAI: *(laughing, soot-faced)* Then it shouldn't have been standing next to one!
*(Inside the shrine a lacquered box breaks open. Something in it is a skull, bound in a cut jade chain. The chain is
in pieces. A samurai, **Akodo Hojiki**, cuts his hand on the jade and wraps the wound with a little stone.)*
`[FLAG shrine_broken]`

## Scene 2 — "The Phoenix Arrive"
**Where/When:** Hiruto's castle, a week later
**Beat:** Phoenix envoys arrive, led by arrogant heir **Isawa Kamasari**, with an Imperial magistrate, **Miya
Yachiko**. The old Phoenix scholar **Isawa Hoju** has died by blood magic, and the Phoenix have traced the maho
here. Lord **Akodo Hiruto**, calm at the centre of his house, orders *his own children and niece* to investigate.
AKODO HIRUTO: Find the truth. If it is a Lion's truth, it is still the truth.
SHIZUE: *(bowing)* And if the truth starts a war, uncle?
AKODO HIRUTO: Then we will at least know what we are fighting about. That will be rare.
`[SYS]` Hub opens: the castle (courtyard, chapel, bathhouse, barracks, the guest wing for the Phoenix).

## Scene 3 — "Where the Kami Went" (investigation hub)
**Beat:** The castle is a **spirit wasteland**: the local kami have been devoured (spells −1 tier on this map).
Clues:
- **Akodo Ijako** (Shizue's cousin, a sodan-seizo who walks the Lands of the Dead in a mask of reeds and mud)
  speaks with the dead. She joins as a guest.
- **Hoju's kindly ghost:** he can say only that "the one who bound him has returned, and wears a sleeping man".
- **A peasant's ghost** accuses Hojiki of murder. *It is lying*: it is O-Tamai wearing a ghost's shape. **Discern**
  (if Gendo is present) or Ijako's *Mask Rite* reveals that the ghost casts no reflection in the lantern-water.
- **Bathhouse:** on the floor, a small jade stone. Hojiki believes it is still bound to his wound, but it was
  swapped for an illusioned pebble.
- **Ozaki's quarters:** a looted statue of Isawa from the shrine (Ozaki ordered the desecration).
AKODO IJAKO: *(lifting her mask)* The dead in this house are frightened, Shizue. The dead are never frightened.
`[SYS]` Clue board UI: 5 clues needed to proceed. Missing ones raise later boss difficulty, never block.

## Scene 4 — "Escalation"
**Beat:** Events fire as the clue count rises:
1. Scouts report Crane troops on the border.
2. **Kitsu Togawa** is choked from inside in the chapel.
3. The visiting Matsu sensei **Matsu Nagai** is possessed and attacks the Phoenix.
`[BATTLE]` **Possessed Matsu Nagai** (mid-boss). Resolve 5. Weak: **Jade** (Hojiki's real stone), Water. Break
the possession to save him.
- After: Isawa Kamasari demands Hojiki in chains. **Duel path action**: Shizue may challenge Kamasari's yojimbo,
  **Shiba Ashimita** (haunted by his grandfather's screaming ghost), to earn one more day. Win or lose honourably, and
  Ashimita respects her.

## Scene 5 — "A Good Man, Technically Guilty"
**Where/When:** the barracks
**Beat:** Confront **Hojiki**. He is a good man whose sleeping body was stolen each night. His brother **Ryuzoji**
hides an arm wound: he should have been on the raid, and wasn't.
AKODO HOJIKI: If my hands did it, then I did it. Let the Phoenix have me.
SHIZUE: *(quietly)* No. If your hands did it, then someone is wearing your hands. I want the someone.
`[CHOICE]` *Hand Hojiki to the Phoenix* (they leave; the climax gets harder) · *Keep him under Lion guard* (the
Phoenix are furious; Yachiko smiles a little too much)

## Scene 6 — "The Fire-Coloured Castle"
**Where/When:** **Shiro no Kamatori** (five towers; glazed, copper-edged walls that blaze in sunset)
**Beat:** Shizue rides to the Phoenix castle under truce, with Ashimita as guide, to learn what was bound. (If Haruki
is recruited, this is where they meet; see Phoenix Ch1.) Hoju's unfinished family history: a Bloodspeaker, **O-Tamai**
("beloved of Iuchiban"), possessed a young Phoenix shugenja, **Kaiki**, for two years. The elders bound the spirit
by killing Kaiki and chaining the skull in jade. Hoju forged the chains.
- **Key item: the jade chains.** The lock grows **cold** near O-Tamai: a hot/cold compass on the map.
- The four despoiled shrines: a short field-search sequence with the compass.

## Scene 7 — "Three Ways to End It" (shared with Phoenix Ch1)
Back at Hiruto's castle, O-Tamai grows impatient. The compass is ice-cold everywhere.
`[CHOICE]` (one route per playthrough):
- **A · The Binding:** summon O-Tamai into a body (Hojiki volunteers; or Shizue offers herself, and Ijako stops
  her) and wrap the jade chains. This is the canon route.
- **B · The Duel:** the Crane duelist **Kakita Mizochi** arrives under truce to settle the border by iaijutsu.
  O-Tamai whispers to Shizue, offering "Hojiki's" maho to guarantee the Lion's win. *Refusing it is an Honor choice.*
  Shizue (or her champion) duels Mizochi clean; O-Tamai, refused, lashes out.
- **C · The Red Shadow:** a red shadow creeps across the court wall toward **Lord Hiruto** to possess him. Shizue
  must reach her uncle first: a chase sequence.

## Scene 8 — BOSS: "O-Tamai" (shared)
`[BATTLE]` **O-Tamai in a borrowed body + red-shadow oni**:
- **Soul damage:** the oni's attacks drain **SP/VP** instead of HP.
- O-Tamai: Resolve 8. Weak: **Jade**, Fire, Katana. Each Suki lets the party land one **jade chain** (key-item
  command). **Three chains → bound** (win).
- The spirit-wasteland debuff ends when the battle is won.
O-TAMAI: *(through the borrowed mouth, many voices)* Lions! You broke my prison to burn a granary. I owe you a war.
Let me pay my debt!
SHIZUE: *(Command: "Now!")* We'll take it in jade.

## Scene 9 — "The Weight of a Raid" (ending)
- O-Tamai is bound again. The Phoenix take the skull home, which leads to Phoenix Ch2.
- **Miya Yachiko** writes her report: "inconclusive". Shizue sees her smile at the word. `[FLAG yachiko_suspected]`
  → Lion Ch3.
- **Gusai** is punished lightly ("he is the lord's son"). **Ozaki** is sent away. Hiruto tells Shizue, alone:
  AKODO HIRUTO: My son started a war for a granary. My nephew wanted one for glory. You stopped one for the truth.
  *(beat)* I cannot reward you for it. It would shame them. *(he gives her his own go stones instead)*
- Hiruto's karo announces that Shizue will attend **Toturi's Winter Court** as the most junior member of the
  retinue. → **Ch2 "Shadow of a Doubt."**

- Hiruto's go stones (accessory: +1 Command use per battle).
- Jade-chain fragment (Jade element for one weapon).
- Guests Ijako and Ashimita.
- First kata pick at Lv 5: *Striking as Water*.
