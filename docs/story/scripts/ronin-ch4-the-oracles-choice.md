# Script — Ronin Ch4 · "The Oracle's Choice" — DRAFT v0.1

**Sources:** **canon 1123** (*Imperial Histories* ch. 5): the Oracle of Fire chooses a young eta woman; the Scorpion
try to change the choice and are foiled "by travelling samurai in the area". Plus orig. (05-chapter-outlines). **All
dialogue is original.**
**Traveler:** Sayo · Rank 3, Lv 41–45 · **summer 1123** · the volcano-side shrine town of the Oracle of Fire.
**Octopath shape:** the shrine town (hub) → the eta quarter → the poisoned well → the night race (set-piece) → boss
→ the ascension.
- **Set-piece:** the **night race** to the crater shrine.
- **Choices:** 2 (one of them is a fake choice, per OCTOPATH-FIT).
- **User rule:** Sayo gains **no Oracle powers**. She gets one small keepsake.

---

## Scene 1 — "The Fire Talks to Me"
**Beat:** Thousands of pilgrims crowd the shrine town. **Akari** *(orig.)*, the eta woman Sayo nursed through a fever
years ago, has sent for her.
AKARI: The fire talks to me, Sayo. It says my name. It says it like a *lord* would. *(beat)* I'm scared.
SAYO: *(hugging her)* Fire's like that. Loud and full of itself. Let's find out what it wants.

## Scene 2 — "Three Hands Reaching" (hub)
**Beat:** Three factions arrive:
- **Scorpion agents** want a different successor.
- **Order of the Snake** scouts want Sayo's Ch3 secret: proof that *blood means nothing*, to bring down a lineage.
- **Temple priests** want the eta girl gone quietly.
Path actions everywhere: **Eavesdrop** (Mitsue), **Discern** (Gendo), **Pilfer** (Sayo: a Scorpion cipher-letter),
**Bless** (Haruki: calm the frightened pilgrims).
- **Hiro** (eta undertaker, guest) and **Ume-baa** are in the eta quarter.

## Scene 3 — "The Eta Quarter"
**Beat:** Samurai can't enter without defilement, so it's a solo cutscene walk for Sayo (OCTOPATH-FIT). Hiro and
Ume-baa hide Akari. Ume-baa teaches Sayo **Anatomy** ("the low knowledge": *Diagnosis*, `sayo-remedy.md` §4).
UME-BAA: Samurai doctors heal from across the room so they don't have to touch you. *(taking Sayo's hands)* We heal
from here.
- `[SYS]` Hiro's claw-mark side story concludes here if started (the weretiger curse is cured with a jade Remedy, or
  he holds it off by will; both are fine).

## Scene 4 — "The Poisoned Well"
**Beat:** The Scorpion poison the eta quarter's well, so the priests can call Akari "unclean". Sayo traces the poison
(the Poison skill) and brews the counter (Rhubarb + Moxa party purge).
`[BATTLE]` Scorpion agents at the well (weak to **Yumi**, **Katana**; they flee at low HP).
- **The blue-eyed woman**, wearing a different face for each faction (the same method twice), whispers to all of
  them. Sayo sees her, because nobody hides from a ronin girl. `[FLAG sayo_saw_nashiko]`

## Scene 5 — "Somebody" (fake choice)
**Beat:** The Order offers to make Sayo *somebody*: a name, a house, a place. All she has to do is hand over the Ch3
secret.
`[CHOICE 1]` *Accept* · *Refuse*. **Fake choice:** on *Accept*, Sayo opens her mouth, laughs, and says no anyway.
SAYO: A name? *(beat)* I've had lots of names. "Wave-girl." "Ronin's brat." "You there." *(grinning)* Sayo's the only
one I picked. I'll keep it.

## Scene 6 — "The Night Race" (set-piece)
**Beat:** The Oracle will ascend at dawn. Sayo must get Akari to the crater shrine alive.
`[SYS]` **Chase set-piece:** rooftops, the pilgrim crowd, the switchback stair. Three short battles in a row (Scorpion
assassins), with **no full heal between them** (items and Remedy only), and a few timed route prompts.
`[CHOICE 2]` The route: *the crowded pilgrim road* (Bless: Haruki parts the crowd; fewer fights) · *the cliff path*
(Athletics; faster, but two more battles).

## Scene 7 — BOSS: "The Naga's Champion"
**Beat:** At the top of the stair waits the **Great Naga's champion**, the Order's guardian: a naga warrior,
honourable and not evil, sent to make sure the "right" choice happens.
`[BATTLE]` **Naga champion**:
- Resolve 16. Weak: **Air**, **Yari**, **Fire**; resists Water.
- No extra gimmick (the race was the set-piece).
- **Talk-down after Suki:** when broken, Sayo can speak to it (the judgement from Ronin Ch2: "of no line, free to
  carry what no line can"). It lowers its spear and lets them pass. Honor +.
NAGA CHAMPION: The Order guards the true lines.
SAYO: *(pointing at Akari)* There's a true line. It goes from her to the fire. *(beat)* Guard *that*.

## Scene 8 — "The Ascension"
**Beat:** Not a fight: a race against the dawn. The old **Oracle of Fire** leaves his body for the Fire Dragon's
court. **Akari takes the mantle.** The flames bow to an eta woman. The priests kneel. Some of them weep. Some of them
are furious. Sayo gets **no powers**.
AKARI (the new Oracle; her voice now carries like a bonfire): Sayo. *(beat, almost herself again)* …It still says my
name like a lord. *(smiling)* But it says yours like a friend.
- **Keepsake:** the **ember charm**: once per battle, a small Fire hit *or* a revive (user rule: modest).
- **Ending beat:** Sayo keeps the Ch3 secret for the villagers who'd die in the war it would start, not for any
  lord. She chooses her family at last: the other seven.
→ **Finale.**
