# Script — Crane Ch1 · "Heaven's Glory" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *…And Does Not Descend* (Hanrahan, 1999). Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Kakita Rensei · **Rank 1**, Lv 1–8 · **late autumn 1122** · Lion lands near Beiden Pass.
**Systems introduced:**
- Talent **Iaijutsu** (Focus → Strike) and the **duel** battle format
- path action **Charm** (Etiquette)
- field puzzle (the lantern lures); a Rank-1 "nobody" stealth beat
- guests: **Matsu Kiku**, **Kitsuki Seigen**

Static NPCs keep their source names. **Lady Doji Sanae** is the renamed pregen honour-keeper (`04-cast` §B4).

---

## Scene 1 — "The Over-Sharpened Sword" (prologue)
**Where/When:** Kakita Dueling Academy, the graduation kata
**Beat:** Rensei performs his graduation kata flawlessly. His father, **Kakita Masanori** *(orig.)*, notes one
imperfection: "Your left foot hesitated." Rensei is assigned as yojimbo to **Lady Doji Sanae**, "Keeper of the
House Honour". Nobody tells him what that means.
`[BATTLE]` Tutorial: a bokken bout vs. a senior student. Teaches **Focus** (skip a turn, build stacks) and
**Strike** (one huge hit); a perfect Strike at max stacks always crits.
KAKITA MASANORI: Adequate.
RENSEI: *(bowing, very still)* Thank you, Father.
*(Later, alone, he practises the kata eleven more times. The left foot does not hesitate. He does it again anyway.)*

## Scene 2 — "The Keeper of the House Honour"
**Where/When:** on the road west, Sanae's palanquin
**Cast:** Rensei; Lady Doji Sanae (courteous, tired, terrifyingly observant); **Kitsuki Seigen** (her world-weary
investigator, guest)
**Beat:** Sanae explains, sparingly. A Kakita prodigy, **Kakita Morishu**, shamed his house and vanished. A Lion lord
near Beiden Pass complains of a "Crane bandit". Their task: make the problem disappear, *quietly*.
LADY SANAE: My work, Rensei-san, is to see that certain things never happened. You are here to keep me alive while I
arrange it.
RENSEI: And if what never happened… did happen?
LADY SANAE: *(smiling at last)* Then you will learn something they do not teach at the Academy.
KITSUKI SEIGEN: *(not looking up from his notes)* They teach nothing useful at the Academy. That's why it's so
expensive.

## Scene 3 — "Pleasant Eaves" (hub village, dusk)
**Where/When:** Pleasant Eaves of Mountains' Cloak Forest Village
**Beat:** Village youths are beating **Ebisu**, a gentle eta firewood-gatherer, who tried to trade a *priceless
wakizashi* for sake. Rensei intervenes. The sword is a Kakita masterwork, ***Heaven's Glory Reflected in the Soul's
Steel***. Everyone who looks at it measures themselves against the ideal samurai.
`[CHOICE]` stop the youths with **Charm** (Etiquette: "Would you strike a man before a Kakita blade?") · with steel
(the youths flee; Honor −) · *buy* Ebisu's safety (costs money; Ebisu is grateful)
*(Rensei looks at the blade, and sees himself, falling short.)*
`[SYS]` Key item: **Heaven's Glory** (it can't be equipped yet; it is Morishu's). The headman **Torishigawa**
watches all of this very carefully.
- **Guest: Matsu Kiku** (a gentle Matsu on pilgrimage, afraid of her own battle-joy) offers to help. She joins.

## Scene 4 — "The Ghost Maiden" (forest dungeon, night)
**Where/When:** Mountains' Cloak forest
**Beat:** A night forest full of **paper lanterns on pulleys** (fake ghost-lights) and a weeping "ghost maiden" who
lures travellers toward a **pit**. A puzzle: track which lanterns are really moving and cut the right pulley lines.
Bandits ambush.
- `[BATTLE]` Forest bandits (weak to **Katana**, **Yumi**; they surrender at low HP, and sparing them matters later).
- **Discovery:** two Crane samurai stripped and tied to a tree: **Kakita Adoru** (furious, talkative) and **Kakita
  Yasurugi** (silent). They carried the wakizashi and a letter *inviting* Morishu to commit seppuku, sent by his own
  father, **Kakita Morihoshi**.
KAKITA ADORU: Untie us, boy! We are on our lord's business!
RENSEI: What business?
*(Yasurugi only looks at Rensei. It is the calmest look Rensei has ever received, and the coldest.)*

## Scene 5 — "The Green Ronin's Village"
**Where/When:** the bandit camp: a hidden village of runaway peasants
**Beat:** The "bandits" are farmers fleeing **Lord Akodo Goroso**'s taxes. Their champion, the **Green Ronin**, is
Morishu. Big, jovial **Imuro** (Bo fighter) pretends to be the Green Ronin to protect him. Morishu's secret wife
**Sariko** is pregnant.
- **News arrives:** at dawn Morishu stumbled out of the forest in a panic and was **captured by Goroso's sergeant,
  Akodo Tama**. He will be executed in the castle yard at dawn tomorrow.
IMURO: He's the best of us, Crane. He saved Torishigawa from two Lions with two cuts. Then he sat in the mud and
shook for an hour.
RENSEI: *(quietly)* Two cuts… and then he shook.
IMURO: You understand that?
RENSEI: …I understand the second part.
`[SYS]` Inn-style rest; **Travel Banter** (Kiku/Seigen on what "honour" costs).

## Scene 6 — "Castle on the Made Hill"
**Where/When:** Lord Goroso's four-storey castle guarding Beiden Pass
**Beat:** Sanae requests an audience. Goroso (scarred, missing two fingers) is **not evil**: he is a strict believer
in the Celestial Order who thinks himself wronged. He grants Sanae a brief audience. Rensei, a Rank 1 yojimbo,
**stands in the corridor and is ignored**.
- **Rank-1 advantage:** in the corridor Rensei overhears a "merchant" with swords under his cloak, **Bayushi
  Depura**, telling Goroso's sergeant how *useful* a public execution would be. Nobody notices a boy in white.
- **Charm** targets inside: Goroso's idealistic daughter **Seoko** (she can open the cells); his wife **Lady
  Tatanko** (she values profit; the village's lost labour is costing her).
`[FLAG depura_seen]` → Crane/Crab crossroads; Depura is the architect of Morishu's fall.

## Scene 7 — "Four Ways to Dawn" (the choice)
Sanae lays out the options with no expression at all:
1. **Hidden shame:** a blade slipped into Morishu's cell, and a quiet seppuku. The House is clean.
2. **Public death:** let the execution proceed. The House is shamed, but not involved.
3. **Rescue:** the bandits strike from inside the crowd. A battle; Goroso is humiliated, and war-bait for the Lion.
4. **The duel:** demand trial by challenge. Goroso will name a champion; Morishu fights for his life.
`[CHOICE]` (all four are playable; **4** is the canonical "best" route and the one the chapter is balanced around).
LADY SANAE: You are deciding what kind of Crane you will be, Rensei-san. Take your time. You have until dawn.

## Scene 8 — BOSS: "The Silent Blade" (Kakita Yasurugi)
- **Route 4 (duel):** Goroso names as his champion the man who wants Morishu dead most: **Kakita Yasurugi**, freed
  and on his lord's business. Morishu can't stop shaking. **Rensei steps in as Morishu's second, and fights
  himself.**
- **Routes 1–3:** Yasurugi hunts them anyway, in the cell corridor, the crowd or the forest edge. Same boss.

`[BATTLE]` **Kakita Yasurugi** (an iaijutsu duel boss):
- Phase 1, **the duel**: an Assessment → Focus → Strike exchange (duel UI). Focus too greedily and he Strikes first.
- Phase 2, **skirmish**: he uses *Kakita's First Stance* and the *Way of the Crane*. Resolve 8. Weak: **Yari**,
  **Tanto**, **Air** (he fights perfectly against katana).
- Matsu Kiku's *Battle-Joy* can break him but debuffs her after; a choice for the player.
YASURUGI: *(his only line in the game, after he loses)* …Better. *(he bows to Rensei and walks away into the trees)*

## Scene 9 — "And Everything Passes" (ending)
- **Duel route:** Morishu lives. Goroso, bound by the trial, grants him the village as a ronin under his protection,
  grudgingly. Torishigawa (the real schemer behind the rebellion) bows to Sanae, and Sanae bows back.
- **Hidden shame / public death:** Morishu dies. Sariko's child will be born a peasant. Rensei carries the sword back.
- **Rescue:** Morishu escapes into the forest for good. Goroso writes to the Crane in fury: one more spark for the
  Lion–Crane war.
- **All routes:** Sanae files her report. Rensei doesn't know what it says. He suspects it says nothing happened.
- **The sword:** in the duel route Morishu gives *Heaven's Glory* to Rensei: "It shows you what you should be. I
  can't bear to look anymore. You still can." In the other routes Sanae returns it to the Kakita vaults.
  (`[SYS]`: the duel route grants Rensei's **ultimate weapon**, early, but locked to a story level. It unlocks at R2.)

RENSEI: *(on the road, alone with Sanae)* The letter his father sent. The death poem. Did my father ever…
LADY SANAE: Your father is a hard man who would never write such a letter. *(beat)* That is why I make sure no one
ever has to find out whether that's true.
→ **Ch2 "The Iron Cranes"** (the Crab/Crane crossroads; Sanae is sent to Garden Under Shadow City).
