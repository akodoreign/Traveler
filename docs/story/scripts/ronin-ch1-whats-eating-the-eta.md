# Script — Ronin Ch1 · "What's Eating the Eta" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *Honor and Eta* (Zenz; D&D 3e Oriental Adventures scenario set in Rokugan). Plot facts only; **all
dialogue is original**. The city **Cakon** keeps its source name (a non-canon city, on Crane land in our map).
**Traveler:** Sayo · **Rank 1, no Status** · Lv 1–8 · **late autumn 1122**.
**Systems introduced:**
- Talent **Remedy** (base + catalyst, `sayo-remedy.md`); path action **Pilfer** (a Low Skill: Honor risk)
- the **caste gate** (places only Sayo can enter); first **ronin school** choice (Warrior / Duelist / Yojimbo)
- guest **Hiro**; the "bridge knock-off" mini-boss

---

## Scene 1 — "Born on the Water" (prologue)
**Where/When:** a river barge, morning; the Cakon docks
**Cast:** Sayo; the barge-folk; **Ume-baa** *(orig.)*, the old eta herbalist who taught her (in a flashback)
**Beat:** A short memory: Sayo, age nine, gathering herbs with Ume-baa outside an eta village, because no samurai
doctor would teach a ronin's daughter. Then the present: Sayo steps off the barge into Cakon with a basket, a
borrowed-looking katana and a straw hat.
UME-BAA: *(flashback)* Samurai say we're unclean because we touch the dead. Child, everyone touches the dead
eventually. We just do it with clean hands.
`[CHOICE]` **First ronin school** (sets her R1 technique and trait bonus, `schools.md`):
- **Warrior:** *Wolf's Technique*: bonus vs. anyone who targets her
- **Duelist:** *Wind Technique*: she acts early
- **Yojimbo:** *Way of Sacrifice*: she shares her Defend
`[BATTLE]` Tutorial: dock thugs harassing a fish-seller. Teaches **Remedy** (Ginseng + Rice wine heal) and the
Katana Boost bonus.

## Scene 2 — "Silver for the Musicians"
**Where/When:** Cakon market
**Beat:** A young Crane noble with a **black eye**, **Mibu Kagemaro**, throws silver to the street musicians, then
quietly hires "bold and honourable citizens". The facts:
- A mercenary, **Daigoro**, killed the noble Mibu Ieko in a jealous brawl over the poet-geisha **O-Sode**.
- Judge **Sono Kentanaga** (of a rival family) added a clause: if the execution isn't carried out in three days,
  Daigoro walks free.
- No eta will act as executioner while something is **devouring them** in their village.
- Kagemaro must not be seen to be involved.
MIBU KAGEMARO: You are… a ronin?
SAYO: Ronin's daughter. Even worse, my lord. I don't even have a lord to have lost.
MIBU KAGEMARO: *(embarrassed, genuine)* Then you're free to go where I can't. That's worth silver.
SAYO: Couldn't you do the execution yourself?
MIBU KAGEMARO: *(recoiling)* I would become *eta*.
SAYO: *(cheerfully)* There. Now you know what you're paying me for.

## Scene 3 — "Alley, Gate, Beggar"
**Beat:** Three street encounters:
- **Ambush:** **Tetsuo**, a ronin from Daigoro's band, and a disguised Lion samurai jump her in an alley.
  `[BATTLE]`
- **The beggar-monk at the gate:** save him from a stray dog and he gives a riddle (*"Under the right arm of the
  strong is the gift…"*) leading to treasure buried under a cedar between two red stones. A side quest.
- **Pilfer** tutorial: lift Tetsuo's purse; it holds Daigoro's band's payment chit from **someone in the Sono
  household**. `[FLAG sono_link]` *(Pilfer is a Low Skill: a witnessed failure costs town Honor; `skills.md`.)*

## Scene 4 — "The Village Outside the Walls" (caste gate)
**Where/When:** the eta village beyond the river gate
**Beat:** Samurai party members **won't enter** ("defilement"). They wait at the bridge, and Sayo goes in alone
(`[SYS]` the **caste gate**: a solo segment, unless the party includes nobody who objects).
- An **all-faiths shrine** built from salvage; frightened families; empty houses where people were taken.
- **Hiro**, a huge toothless undertaker with a rusty no-dachi, survived the beast. He joins as a guest.
- The eta believe the creature *punishes their unclean souls* and that they deserve it.
HIRO: It took my brother from his bed. He didn't scream. Good men don't scream when they're being punished.
SAYO: *(furious, quietly)* Then it's not punishment. It's dinner. And I'm going to spoil its appetite.
`[SYS]` Guest **Hiro**: *No-dachi Sweep* (AoE, 2 uses). His later side story: he was clawed, and the curse may take
him (a seed for Ronin Ch4 or a side story).

## Scene 5 — "The Tengu Bridge" (mini-boss)
**Where/When:** a log bridge over an irrigation canal
**Beat:** Two **crow-headed tengu** duel on the log *just to humiliate travellers*. They demand she cross "with
dignity".
`[BATTLE]` **Two crow-tengu**:
- **Knock-off mechanic:** every heavy hit or Knockdown can *knock a combatant off the log* (out of the fight for 2
  turns, soaked).
- Weak: **Yumi**, **Air**. Pride gimmick: compliment them (Etiquette check) and they lose their next turn preening.
CROW-TENGU A: Cross, wave-girl, and be laughed at!
SAYO: *(already halfway across, on her hands)* I've been laughed at by better birds.

## Scene 6 — "Goblins With Baskets"
**Beat:** Six **bakemono** goblins with baskets of stolen pilgrim loot. Pilfer them back mid-battle (Many-hands
chaos), or beat them and return the loot to the shrine (Honor +).
`[BATTLE]` Bakemono ×6. Weak: **Katana**, **Fire**, **Jade**.

## Scene 7 — BOSS: "Tomie Cho" (the mud hut)
**Where/When:** a mud hut at the edge of the rice paddies, at dusk
**Beat:** An old woman offers Sayo tea. She is **Tomie Cho**, a **weretiger**: the last of a family cursed for
drinking from a sacred well. She is too old to hunt rabbits now, so she turned to the eta, who are easy prey.
TOMIE CHO: *(pouring tea, smiling)* Sit, child. You smell of herbs. Ume's girl, aren't you? She always smelled of
herbs too.
SAYO: *(not sitting)* You knew Ume-baa.
TOMIE CHO: I knew everyone in that village. *(the smile widens, and widens)* I've been visiting them.
`[BATTLE]` **Tomie Cho**, three forms:
1. **Old woman** inside the hut: hard to hit (cover). **Fire flushes her out**: burning the hut (Hanabi Remedy, or
   a torch) starts phase 2 early.
2. **Hybrid:** claw combos; Terror roar.
3. **Tiger:** fast, Resolve 9. Weak: **Fire**, **Yari**, **Yumi**.
- Hiro's *No-dachi Sweep* does double damage to her (he's waited for this).

## Scene 8 — "Clean Hands" (ending)
**Beat:** The eta return to work. On the third day, at the edge of the clause, **Hiro performs the execution** with
his rusty no-dachi, which has been cleaned and sharpened all night. Daigoro dies; the law is satisfied. Kagemaro
owes Sayo **giri**, a debt of obligation he can never be seen to repay.
- `[FLAG sono_link]`: Sayo can tell Kagemaro that the Sono household paid Daigoro's band. That starts his side
  story (a family feud).
MIBU KAGEMARO: *(from a distance; he can't stand too close)* I can't thank you in public.
SAYO: I know. *(grinning)* Throw some silver to the musicians. I'll know what it means.
*(Epilogue: Hiro rubs the claw marks on his arm when he thinks no one's watching.)*
→ **Ch2 "The Noble and the Ronin"** (the pilgrim Jinnai, the Village of Floating Flowers).
