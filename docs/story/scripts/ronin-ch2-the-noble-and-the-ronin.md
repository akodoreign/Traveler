# Script — Ronin Ch2 · "The Noble and the Ronin" — DRAFT v0.1

**Sources:** *Adventure Ideas* ("The Noble and the Ronin" hook) + *Entanglements & Tied Snakes* (the Village of
Floating Flowers, the Order of the Snake). Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Sayo · Rank 1→2, Lv 20–25 · **winter 1122–23** · the river road → the Village of Floating Flowers
(between Crab, Scorpion and Sparrow lands).
**Octopath shape:** road → river barge → village hub (path actions) → the snake den (dungeon) → boss.
- **Set-piece:** the "Only Snakes may pass" trap room.
- **Choices:** 2.
**New guest:** **Jinnai** *(orig.)*, a lay brother of the Order of Osano-Wo (monk guest, `kiho.md` §2).

---

## Scene 1 — "Apprehend the Criminal!"
**Where/When:** a river road in winter
**Beat:** A spoilt young lord, **Ikoma Sadanobu** *(orig.; a Lion governor's son)*, has his retainers beat a devout
ronin pilgrim, **Jinnai**, for not stepping off the road fast enough. Then he shouts to the nearest passer-by to help
"apprehend the criminal". The passer-by is Sayo.
IKOMA SADANOBU: You! Peasant! Hold that man!
SAYO: *(looking at the bleeding pilgrim, then at the lord)* Which one of you is the criminal again? I'm new.
`[BATTLE]` Sadanobu's retainers (weak to **Katana**, **Yumi**; they flee at half strength).
- Sayo is now **branded a bandit**. **Pilfer** Sadanobu's dropped travel writ (Low Skill: Honor risk), which helps
  later; or leave it.

## Scene 2 — "Barge Medicine"
**Where/When:** a rice barge downriver
**Beat:** Sayo patches Jinnai's ribs (**Remedy** tutorial with 2 catalysts: Medicine 5 isn't needed for the
story-scripted brew). Jinnai's prayer beads are carved with a tiny snake. He is calm, wry, and in more pain than he
admits.
JINNAI: You'll be hunted for this.
SAYO: I was born on a barge, monk. I've been "hunted" by every toll-keeper on three rivers. They never catch me at
low tide.
- **Guest: Jinnai** joins (*Freezing the Lifeblood*, *Chi Protection*; passive Kharmic counter).

## Scene 3 — "Floating Flowers" (hub)
**Beat:** A river stop of soap-opera villagers (each a side story):
- a sleazy **Yasuki merchant** in love with a branded **eta woman** (Sayo's old patient; she remembers her)
- the geisha-house **Madame** from Ryoko Owari
- the beloved **harbourmaster**
- **Path actions:** Pilfer, Remedy for the sick, Barter if Tamotsu is present.
- Wanted posters with Sayo's face (badly drawn). The villagers decide the drawing is too ugly to be her.
VILLAGE GRANDMOTHER: *(comparing the poster to Sayo)* No, no. That girl has a nose like a radish. You're much
prettier. Go away before they notice.

## Scene 4 — "The Storm on the Jetties"
**Beat:** Sadanobu's retainers arrive in force during a winter storm.
`[BATTLE]` **The storm of retainers**: a normal hard battle (no gimmick; the budget goes to the trap room). Retainers
surrender at low HP.
`[CHOICE 1]` *Spare the retainers* (the village shelters Sayo; Honor +) · *drive them into the river* (faster, but
the village is frightened of her).

## Scene 5 — "Under the Floor Mats" (dungeon)
**Beat:** Jinnai disappears at night. Sayo follows him to the **harbourmaster's house**: beneath the floor mats, a
den of carved stone snakes. The **Order of the Snake**: a century-old secret society that removes *usurpers* and
protects true lines. Its members bear hidden snake tattoos and barely know one another. **Jinnai is one of them.**

## Scene 6 — "Only Snakes May Pass" (set-piece)
**Beat:** The trap room is white sand under carved serpents. Crawl through it on your belly, or be bitten by
shadow-snakes.
`[SYS]` Each party member walks or crawls (a single prompt for the party):
- **Samurai who crawl** lose face (Honor −1, with a comic line each).
- **Those who walk** take shadow-snake damage and poison.
- **Sayo crawls happily** (no Honor loss: she has none to lose, and she doesn't care).
SAYO: *(already on her belly)* Come on. The sand's warm.
RENSEI (if present): *(standing very straight)* I would rather be bitten.
NATSUKO (if present): *(crawling)* I've crawled through worse on the Wall. Bigger snakes, too.

## Scene 7 — "The Judgement"
**Beat:** A giant stone serpent's mouth leads to a bodiless **voice of judgement** (the Great Naga, unseen). It weighs
Sayo and finds her "**of no line at all — and therefore free to carry what no line can**".

## Scene 8 — BOSS: "The Order's Enforcers"
**Beat:** Enforcers who take Sayo for a spy attack in the den.
`[BATTLE]` **Snake enforcers + shadow-snake summons**:
- Enforcers: Resolve 7. Weak: **Katana**, **Fire**, **Jade**.
- Shadow-snakes (summoned each 2 turns): Resolve 2. Weak: **Fire**, **Air**. Poison bites.
- Jinnai **stops the fight** when an enforcer is broken ("She passed the judgement!"). Battle won.
`[CHOICE 2]` *Promise to keep the Order's secret* (canonical) · *refuse to promise* (Jinnai trusts her anyway; the
Order watches her).

## Scene 9 — Ending
**Beat:** Jinnai's house guarded a secret on the **Mountain of the White Snow**, and he is the last of it. His old
wound, opened again, will not let him climb.
JINNAI: The Order isn't mine to give you. The mountain is. *(pressing the snake beads into her hand)* Climb it for
me, wave-girl. Find out what my family was guarding. Then decide whether it should stay guarded.
SAYO: And you?
JINNAI: *(smiling)* I'll go back to my shrine. The Osano-Wo monks will want to know why I'm late.
→ **Ronin Ch3 "The Village of the Wounded Lion."** Jinnai reappears in the **finale's Act 1**, at his order's
Shrine of Osano-Wo.
`[SYS]` **Rewards:**
- Lv ~25 (R2: her second ronin technique, from any of the three schools).
- *Striking as Void* at the Lv 25 pick (`kata.md`).
- The snake beads (key item).
- Jinnai (guest, and his side stories).
