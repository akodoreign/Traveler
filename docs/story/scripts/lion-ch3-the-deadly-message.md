# Script — Lion Ch3 · "The Deadly Message" — DRAFT v0.1

**Sources:** the *Adventure Ideas* hook "The Deadly Message" + orig. (05-chapter-outlines). Plot facts only; **all
dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Akodo Shizue · Rank 2, Lv 30–35 · **spring thaw 1123** · Shiro Akodo → the mountain hermitage of
Kaze-no-Tani.
**Octopath shape:** the castle (brief) → the road village → the waystation → the mountain pass (dungeon) → the
hermitage → boss.
- **Gimmick:** none in battle. The **sealed message** is a story set-piece (choice 1).
- **Choices:** 2.
- **Fit fix:** both routes end with **the same boss** (Yachiko's killers). Shōgen's test duel is a **Duel
  path-action battle** that only route A triggers.

---

## Scene 1 — "Three Lines"
**Beat:** Toturi's karo **Akodo Kagehisa** *(orig.)* hands Shizue a lacquered tube with Toturi's seal: deliver it to
"the old man of Kaze-no-Tani", and speak to no one. The audience lasts three lines.
SHIZUE: *(after, alone)* A Rank 1 messenger. Nobody would miss one. *(beat)* That's why it's me.

## Scene 2 — "The Burned Bridge"
**Beat:** A village of Lion refugees from Crane border raids. A bandit chief holds the ford.
- **Duel** path action: the bandit chief.
- **Guest: Akodo Ijako** joins (cousin, sodan-seizo).
- An **"Imperial courier inspector"** with Miya papers asks to examine sealed messages. Refusing politely costs
  nothing; refusing rudely costs Glory. (Foreshadows **Miya Yachiko**.)

## Scene 3 — "Kill These Messengers" (Choice 1)
**Where/When:** a waystation in a sleet storm
**Beat:** The tube cracks in the cold, and the seal breaks. Three words in Toturi's hand: ***Kill these
messengers.***
- Banter, if present: Gendo ("evidence is testimony"), Mitsue ("burn it"), Rensei ("deliver it; that is the
  point").
- Ijako's ancestor at a roadside shrine: "A message is a blade. Who holds the hilt?"
`[CHOICE 1]` **A · Deliver it anyway** (the honour route) · **B · Turn back and confront the karo**.
`[FLAG message_route]`

## Scene 4 — "The Pass" (dungeon)
**Beat:** A snowy mountain pass. "Bandits" who are ronin paid in Imperial coin. **Discern / Eavesdrop / Interrogate**
finds a **Miya travel writ** and a letter to a *Crane* general in the same hand.
- `[BATTLE]` Ronin ambushers (weak to **Yari**, **Yumi**; they surrender to Cite the Law).

## Scene 5A — "The Old Man of Kaze-no-Tani" (route A only)
**Beat:** The retired sensei **Akodo Shōgen** *(orig.)* reads the message and laughs: "Toturi remembers." It is an
old Akodo test: he teaches only those who deliver *knowing* what it says.
`[BATTLE]` *(Duel path-action battle)* **Shōgen's test**: a duel exchange, then two rounds of kata. Win or lose
honourably, and he agrees to come down from the mountain. `[FLAG shogen_joined]`
AKODO SHŌGEN: *(after)* You read it. You came anyway. Why?
SHIZUE: Because if my lord wanted me dead, I wanted to hear him say it to my face. And if he didn't, someone else
did, and I wanted to know who.
AKODO SHŌGEN: *(delighted)* Ha! He's sent me a strategist. *(beat)* Finally.

## Scene 5B — "The Karo's Answer" (route B only)
**Beat:** Back at Shiro Akodo, the karo says she failed the test but proved loyal. "The old man stays on his
mountain." `[FLAG shogen_joined = false]`. He sends her back up the pass to fetch Shōgen's reply letter anyway, and
straight into the same ambush as route A.

## Scene 6 — BOSS: "The Miya Killers" (both routes)
**Beat:** Yachiko's hired killers have one order: the message must **never arrive**, whatever Shizue decides. They
strike at the hermitage gate (A) or on the pass (B).
`[BATTLE]` **A Seppun-trained duelist + 2 archers**:
- Duelist: Resolve 10. Weak: **Yari**, **Tanto**, **Earth**. Archers: Resolve 4. Weak: **Katana**, **Air**.
- **Command** shines here (move the archers' targets up the turn order; Akodo Formation vs. the arrows).
`[CHOICE 2]` *Spare the duelist* (he names Yachiko: a stronger Ch4 case) · *finish him* (the letters name her anyway,
but with less weight).

## Scene 7 — Ending
**Beat:** The killers' letters prove that **Miya Yachiko** has been feeding *both* armies false reports, keeping the
clans bleeding so none can challenge the Hantei. Toturi reads them in silence and says one word.
AKODO TOTURI: Again.
*(Yachiko escapes: her Imperial status protects her. She will be at Sorrow's Path.)*
→ **Ch4 "Sorrow's Path."**
