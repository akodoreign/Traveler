# Script — Scorpion Ch4 · "The Eve" (Crossroads: Tamori's Furnace) — DRAFT v0.1

**Sources:**
- canon 1123: Shoju visits the Kami Togashi (*Imperial Histories* ch. 5)
- *Blood and Iron* epilogue: the Iron Box can only be destroyed in **Tamori's Furnace**
- orig.

Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**. Togashi's canon answer is **paraphrased**, never quoted.
**Owner:** Shosuro Mitsue · Rank 3, Lv 41–45 · **summer 1123 (Horse month)**. **Partner bonus:** Kitsuki Gendo
(after Dragon Ch2). **Stand-in:** Togashi Yosai.
**Octopath shape:** mountain road town → Kyuden Togashi (story set-piece) → volcano dungeon → boss.
- **Gimmick:** the boss copies a traveler's face (the *Stolen Face*).
- **Choices:** 1 (the locked "tell someone" choice is a designed non-choice).

---

## Scene 1 — "The Box Is Talking Again"
**Beat:** The Iron Box (carried since Ch1, −1 VP per battle) speaks in Mitsue's sleep in her own voice. Norie's
letter: destroy it at **Tamori's Furnace** in Dragon lands, the only fire hot enough.
IRON BOX (Mitsue's voice): *Open me. You're so tired of being nobody. I could make you everyone.*
MITSUE: *(awake, to the box)* Everyone is exactly the problem.

## Scene 2 — "A Troupe of Pilgrims" (road town)
**Where/When:** a Dragon mountain town below Kyuden Togashi
**Beat:** Mitsue travels disguised as the head of a pilgrim troupe (Many Faces).
- **Partner bonus:** **Gendo** knows the paths and the Tamori monks: "I'll want to know what's in the box. I'll
  settle for knowing what it does."
- **Stand-in:** **Togashi Yosai** joins: "A cursed box of a Bloodspeaker's heart! Wonderful. That's nineteen."
- A **dying Bayushi courier** in the inn mistakes Mitsue's mask for a friend's. **Eavesdrop** his fever-talk:
  muster dates, garrisons moving toward **Otosan Uchi**.

## Scene 3 — "The High House of Light" (story set-piece)
**Where/When:** Kyuden Togashi
**Beat:** The Scorpion Champion's procession arrives. Mitsue, a Rank 3 nobody, enters with the servants in a new
face (a **Many Faces** cutscene trigger, per OCTOPATH-FIT). In the garden she overhears **Bayushi Shoju** ask the
**Kami Togashi** for guidance. *(Paraphrase in game:)* Togashi answers that if Shoju does this, a family line will
end, and that if he succeeds, the Empire will be saved. Shoju hears what he wants to hear.
*(Camera holds on Mitsue's hands as she pours tea. They don't shake. She waits until she's alone, and then they
do.)*
`[SYS]` The **Eavesdrop** prompt here can't fail: the scene is fated.

## Scene 4 — "Duty's Knife" (the locked choice)
**Beat:** She knows. The game offers the choice to tell someone (the party, the Emperor, Norie). **Those options are
greyed out**, each with a one-line reason in Mitsue's voice ("Norie would have to report me." · "They'd die for
knowing." · "Who would believe a nobody?"). The only active option: *keep walking*.
MITSUE: *(internal)* Duty isn't a leash. It's the promise you keep when you know exactly what it will cost.
- **Partner bonus:** Gendo saw the courier's orders too. Later, at the volcano, he quietly burns his notes in front
  of her. `[FLAG gendo_keeps_secret]`

## Scene 5 — "Tamori's Furnace" (dungeon)
**Where/When:** the volcano: lava tubes, ash-shrines, the Tamori monks' heat-wards
- `[BATTLE]` Fire elementals (weak to **Water**, **Bo**), ash-ghouls (weak to **Jade**).
- Heat: no gimmick (the budget goes to the boss); the volcano is just the setting.

## Scene 6 — BOSS: "The Stolen Face" (Bloodspeaker remnant)
**Where/When:** the crater's mouth
**Beat:** A Bloodspeaker remnant who has hunted the Box since Kudo: a maho-tsukai wearing a stolen face.
`[BATTLE]` **Bloodspeaker remnant**:
- **Gimmick: Stolen Face.** At 66% and 33% HP he takes the face of a party member and copies their last-used skill
  (a mirror). His weaknesses then become **that traveler's weapon types**. Use them, or break the copy.
- Maho: pays HP to cast; inflicts **Taint**. Weak: **Jade**, Katana, Air. Resolve 12.
- The Iron Box's curse (−1 VP) is active one last time.
BLOODSPEAKER: *(in Mitsue's face)* You and I are the same, Shosuro. We wear what we need.
MITSUE: We're not the same. *(Many Faces: Steal, and she takes back her face)* I always give mine back.

## Scene 7 — Ending
**Beat:** The Box goes into the fire. It screams in her voice and then in nobody's. The curse lifts (VP penalty
removed; `[SYS]` the Box leaves the inventory).
- Mitsue gives each traveler a **Scorpion "Favor"** pendant (a one-use reward each).
- She walks back toward Otosan Uchi. The Coup is a few months away (14th of the Rooster ≈ October).
- **Partner bonus:** Gendo walks with her as far as the pass. Neither speaks. At the fork, he bows lower than his
  rank requires.
→ The finale's lead-in (Mitsue's glimpse of the blue-eyed woman in Shoju's procession: a servant's face, smiling).
`[SYS]` **Rewards:**
- Lv ~45 (R3: *The Shadow Has No Form*).
- *Blackened Sight* kata pick at 51.
- The Favor pendants.
- `gendo_keeps_secret` (epilogue line).
