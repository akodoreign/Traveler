# Script — Lion Ch4 · "Sorrow's Path" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** orig. (05-chapter-outlines), built on *Shadow of a Doubt* (the Sorrow's Path banner) and *Of Family and
Honor* (Nashiko's "same method twice"). **All dialogue is original.**
**Traveler:** Akodo Shizue · Rank 3, Lv 41–45 · **summer 1123 (Horse/Goat)** · Kanashimi no Komichi, the old
Lion–Crane battlefield.
**Octopath shape:** the Lion camp (hub) → night investigation (path actions) → **hold the ford** (set-piece event
battle) → boss.
- **Set-piece:** hold the ford.
- **Choices:** 2.
- **Flags used:** `shogen_joined` (Ch3), `atsuko_promise` (Ch2), the Matsu Tadanobu letters (Crane Ch3), the Yachiko
  letters (Ch3).

## Scene 1 — "The Same Ground"
**Beat:** Both armies are back on **Sorrow's Path**, where Matsu Joruri won centuries ago. The Crane banner the oni
stole in Ch2 was taken here. Uncle **Hiruto**'s contingent; cousin **Gusai**, eager; the Matsu vanguard under champion
**Matsu Rokuro** *(orig.)*. **Miya Yachiko** is here as an "Imperial observer". If `shogen_joined`, old Shōgen is in
the command tent: party buff "Old Lion's Counsel" (+10% damage this chapter).
AKODO SHŌGEN: Every Lion general who died here thought he was winning. *(beat)* Think the other thing, girl.

## Scene 2 — "The Forged Apology" (night investigation)
**Beat:** A Crane apology letter delivered at night was swapped for an insult. Every witness describes a lovely
courtier **with blue eyes**. **Discern / Eavesdrop / Interrogate** in camp.
- Evidence: Yachiko's letters (Ch3) and **Matsu Tadanobu's letters** (Crane Ch3, if Rensei finished it, or found
  here on a courier) prove the war was *engineered*: by the Imperial agent and the Lion pirate-patron alike.
**"She uses the same method twice."** So the Lion's own conciliatory letter to the Crane was surely swapped too.
Someone must cross the river before dawn.

## Scene 3 — "Across the River" (Choice 1)
`[CHOICE 1]` Who crosses?
- *Rensei* (if recruited; a crossroads beat; he's on his way to Kyuden Doji)
- *Daidoji Atsuko* (if `atsuko_promise` was kept honestly; she repays the debt)
- *Shizue herself* (the hardest: she can't hold the ford; the event battle is led by Gusai or Hiruto, with weaker
  buffs)
DAIDOJI ATSUKO: *(at the riverbank, at night)* You told me one true thing in my cell, Lion. Here's mine: I'd have
crossed anyway. *(beat)* But I like that you asked.

## Scene 4 — "Gusai" (night)
**Beat:** Matsu Rokuro has been ridden by one of Nashiko's errand-demons (a Fear-type). He'll charge at dawn
regardless. **Gusai** wants to charge with him for glory.
- **Duel** path action vs. Gusai (non-lethal), or **Command** dialogue (needs R3 and Hiruto's backing). Win → Gusai
  holds his men back.
AKODO GUSAI: I started this war in one night, cousin. Let me end it in one morning.
SHIZUE: You can't end a war by starting a battle. *(drawing)* I'll prove it on you if I have to.
- **Obsidian temptation:** in Gusai's tent, an **obsidian blade** a "Crane deserter" sold him: the surest weapon
  against a Fear-demon. **Using it gives Taint** (user ruling; `enemies.md`). It's available as a battle item.

## Scene 5 — "Hold the Ford" (set-piece event battle)
`[SYS]` **Defence-wave battle: 5 waves**, then the boss. Hold the ford until the go-between reaches the Crane
general.
1. Matsu berserkers charging by mistake: knock them down, don't kill them (Knockdown and Subdue earn Honor).
2. A Crane counter-charge.
3. Archers on the far bank.
4. The burning bridge (a hazard wave: fire tiles).
5. The demon's terror wave (party Terror; *Courage of the Seven Thunders*, *Death Trance* and kata help).
- **Command, Akodo Formation, Battle Cry** are the stars. The chosen go-between's arrival ends the waves.

## Scene 6 — BOSS: "The Possessed Champion"
`[BATTLE]` **Matsu Rokuro**, ridden by the Fear-demon:
- Resolve 16. Weak: **Jade**, **Yari**, **Water**; **obsidian** too (the blade from Gusai's tent: big Suki damage,
  Taint +1 to the user per hit).
- Terror aura each turn (both armies cower). No extra gimmick beyond the aura (the ford was the set-piece).
- Breaking the possession frees Rokuro **alive**.
THE DEMON (in Rokuro's voice): You're afraid, little strategist. I can *taste* it.
SHIZUE: Of course I'm afraid. *(Command: "Now!")* Only fools and Matsu aren't. *(beat)* Sorry, Rokuro-san.

## Scene 7 — "One Battle" (Choice 2)
**Beat:** Mid-ford, the two generals meet. The battle stops; the war does not.
`[CHOICE 2]` Shizue's report to Toturi: *the truth: the Lion charged first* (her own cousin's men; Honor +, Glory −;
canonical) · *blame the demon alone* (Glory +, Honor −).
- **Yachiko**, exposed by the letters, is recalled to the capital "for consultation". She smiles at Shizue as she
  leaves: it won't be the last time.
- **The courtier's empty tent:** a Crane court mask, white hair-dye, a smooth stone carved like jade. The trail
  points north-east → the finale.
SHIZUE: *(to Hiruto, looking at the ford)* I couldn't stop the war. Only one battle.
AKODO HIRUTO: *(go stones in hand)* One is enough today.
→ **Finale.**
