# Script — Scorpion Ch1 · "Blood and Iron" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *Blood and Iron* (Laderoute, 3e), re-dated from ~1168 to **late autumn 1122**. Re-dating changes:
- the Champion who sends them is **Bayushi Shoju**, from afar, through Mitsue's sensei
- the famine becomes a local **rice blight**
- Kokujin's madness is fresh (1120)

Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Shosuro Mitsue · **Rank 1**, Lv 1–8 · Kyuden Bayushi → the town of **Kudo** on the River of Gold.
**Systems introduced:**
- path action **Eavesdrop** (the adventure's own opening minigame); Talent **Many Faces** (steal / disguise / copy)
- the **Iron Box** (a cursed key item, carried to Ch4)
- the **Suki-to-disarm** boss gimmick
- statuses Taint and Terror

---

## Scene 1 — "A Memorial in Red" (prologue)
**Where/When:** Kyuden Bayushi, a memorial service in the gardens
**Cast:** Mitsue; her sensei **Shosuro Norie** (Emerald Magistrate, "as fair as Scorpions get")
**Beat:** Mitsue attends as a nobody: a servant one hour, a minor cousin the next. Norie tests her: *"Bring me three
true rumours before the incense burns down."*
`[SYS]` **Eavesdrop tutorial:** garden and dinner conversations, a timing and position minigame with numbered
rumours. The three that matter:
1. A **Miya caravan of artefacts from a ruined capital** passed through.
2. Bandits, and an *unknown band* fighting them, on the River of Gold.
3. Two courtiers shared **blood nightmares** on the two nights the caravan stayed here.
SHOSURO NORIE: Three. And you were a lady's maid for two of them. Who were you for the third?
MITSUE: *(in a stranger's voice, then her own)* Nobody, sensei. Nobody is very easy to be.
SHOSURO NORIE: *(not smiling)* Be careful. Some of us forget how to stop.

## Scene 2 — "The Willow Teahouse"
**Where/When:** a teahouse on a pond peninsula, a secret meeting
**Beat:** Norie relays orders "from the Champion, who does not know your name, which is why you are useful". The
Scorpion are short of agents. Mitsue will be made a temporary **yoriki** to a Mantis chief magistrate and sent to
**Kudo**, a farm town in unaligned land (once Hare land) jointly governed by the **Kitsune (Fox)** and **Suzume
(Sparrow)** clans. It's being considered as the seat of a new Minor Clans Court, and violence is escalating.
`[SYS]` **Many Faces** tutorial: disguise as a teahouse servant to slip out unseen (a field disguise).

## Scene 3 — "Traitor's Grove" (the road south)
**Beat:** Rice blight in the fields. **Hyashi no Uragirimono** (Traitor's Grove), where Scorpion traitors are
punished: a quiet, horrible place. Murders in the border town of **Shutai**; the Ronin Plains.
- `[BATTLE]` Road bandits (weak to **Tanto**, **Yumi**). Teaches **Many Faces**' *Steal*.
- **Banter tag** (once a second traveler is recruited): Mitsue tells three different stories about the burn on her
  hand, and all of them are lies.

## Scene 4 — "Kudo" (investigation hub)
**Where/When:** Kudo, east bank of the River of Gold, near the Shinomen Mori
**Beat:** A town of suspects:
- The **Kitsune governor** isn't himself (he's dead and replaced by a shape-stealing oni of the Kommei brood).
- The **Suzume governor's "assassination attempt"** was probably invented.
- The **Mantis magistrate** and his staff are down with a virulent "pneumonia".
- Townsfolk from one district are quietly fleeing.
- The caravan was robbed *in town*. Every stolen item was found dumped by the river **except a small iron box**.
- **Eavesdrop** targets: the Suzume governor's steward; Kitsune Hana (the Kitsune aide; *sympathetic*); a Tortoise
  envoy (a red herring: he's only smuggling).
- **Many Faces:** disguise as a Kitsune servant to search the governor's rooms. The "governor" eats raw river fish
  at night.
MITSUE: *(as a servant, bowing to the "governor")* Your fish, my lord.
"GOVERNOR": *(too many teeth for a moment)* …Leave it. Leave the plate. Leave.
`[FLAG governor_fake]`

## Scene 5 — "The Nudge"
**Beat:** The sick vanish overnight. Something blots out the moon, flying west across the river. The fake governor
is gone with them.
`[BATTLE]` The **Kommei-brood oni** caught at the river crossing (mid-boss). Resolve 6. Weak: **Jade**, **Fire**. It
**swaps two party members' commands** mid-turn (the body-swap gimmick, foreshadowing Crab Ch3's Oni no Kommei).

## Scene 6 — "Across the River" (dungeon)
**Where/When:** the west bank, turning into a Shadowlands marsh
**Beat:**
- A drifting **barge of bloodless corpses**: the Mantis magistrate, and the *real* Kitsune governor.
- Animals flee the woods. Trees bleed. The forest is becoming a marsh.
- **Taint** tutorial: the marsh tiles add Taint stacks. Jade charms and resting at an emergency shrine clear them.
- `[BATTLE]` Marsh things: blood-leeches, Tainted animals.

## Scene 7 — BOSS: "The Heart in the Clearing"
**Where/When:** a heaving, heart-like clearing around a pit of blood
**Beat:** **Kitsune Hana**, possessed by a **Greater Kansen** (a corrupted kami; a yellow-green cloud), holds the
**Iron Box** and is summoning the **Oracle of Blood**.
`[BATTLE]` **Kansen-possessed Hana**:
- **Ritual counter:** 10 turns; each turn after that risks completion (a game-over-style event: the Oracle rises).
- **Suki = disarm:** when her Resolve hits 0 she **drops the Iron Box** and the counter stops. **Many Faces' Steal**
  can then *take* the Box.
- The Kansen is hurt fully only by **crystal** and magic; physical attacks do half. Weak: Air, Water.
- Phase 2 (if the counter passes 7): the **Oracle of Blood** half-forms. A blood-shape, drowning grapple; jade
  bypasses its carapace.
KANSEN-HANA: *(two voices: hers, pleading, under the other)* Take it… take it away from me… *(then the other)*
Give it BACK.

## Scene 8 — "What Is in the Box" (ending)
**Beat:** Hana lives, free and terrified. The box is iron and very plain. It holds nothing anyone can see. It hums.
That night Mitsue dreams of a heart beating inside it, and wakes with the lid open on her pillow.
SHOSURO NORIE: *(by letter)* That box once held the heart of **Iuchiban**. It cannot be broken by any tool we own.
Keep it. Tell no one. Do not open it again.
MITSUE: *(reading, lid already open; she closes it)* …Again?
- **Reward:** a **Scorpion "Favor"**, a blood-red jasper scorpion pendant (one-use: Norie owes her a big favour; it
  unlocks a later path-action success).
- `[SYS]` **Key item: the Iron Box** (cursed: −1 VP at the start of every battle until destroyed in Ch4 at Tamori's
  Furnace). It whispers in field banter.
