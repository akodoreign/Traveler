# Script — Crane Ch3 · "The White Squid" — DRAFT v0.1

**Sources:**
- *The Edge of Treachery* Ch. 1 (Gearin), **re-dated** from Hantei XXIII to **early spring 1123**: Lady Sanae
  replaces Daidoji Toshiya as the one who sends Rensei.
- **Canon 1123** (*Imperial Histories* ch. 5): a dishonourable Lion samurai secretly stokes Crane–Mantis piracy. Here
  he is the pirates' secret patron.

Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Kakita Rensei · Rank 2, Lv 30–35 · the Crane coast and islands.
**Octopath shape:** port town → islands (one brother as mid-boss) → the Temples of the Sun (lore) → the storm
crossing → the White Squid Shrine (dungeon) → boss.
- **Gimmick:** the **fake charms** buff the pirate crews; break or steal a charm to strip its buff.
- **Choices:** 2.

---

## Scene 1 — "A Head for a Gift"
**Beat:** The **White Squid** pirates (wako) seized a Crane tribute meant for the Emperor. Lady Sanae, embarrassed,
lends Rensei a ship and a crew: "The Emperor will receive a new gift. Bring me the pirate captain's head." Rensei,
now Rank 2, is suddenly a *captain*. He has never been on a boat.
RENSEI: *(gripping the rail, grey)* Is it supposed to… move like this?
SHIP'S MASTER: It's the sea, captain.
RENSEI: Yes. Yes, of course. *(beat)* All of it?
`[SYS]` A **ship** unlocks on the world map (coastal travel).
- A rival clock (story only): the famous Imperial magistrate **Otomo Shinken** is also racing for the pirates' base.

## Scene 2 — "One Ship, Four Places"
**Beat:** Survivors swear the White Squid struck four ports on the *same day*. **Discern / Eavesdrop** at the docks:
there are **four brothers** with near-identical ships.
- **Ryohei the Eldest:** Mantis bushi, tactician, nunchaku.
- **Yosuke the Bold:** a ronin leader.
- **Chihaya the Quick:** a ronin with perfect balance.
- **Katsuo the White Brother:** an **albino maho-tsukai** at the White Squid Shrine, who makes **fake nemuranai
  charms** for every crew.
`[CHOICE 1]` *Ally with Yasuki Tessai* (a Crab lord ruined by the wako: money and a second ship, but he wants the
loot) · *go alone* (slower, cleaner).

## Scene 3 — MID-BOSS: "Chihaya the Quick"
**Where/When:** a cove fort on the first island
`[BATTLE]` **Chihaya**: Resolve 7. Weak: **Yumi**, **Air**. Perfect balance: immune to Knockdown. Her **fake charm**
shows on the enemy panel. Stealing it (Many Faces) or breaking it (Suki on the charm) strips her evasion buff. *(The
chapter gimmick's tutorial.)*
CHIHAYA: *(mid-fight, laughing)* You fight like a painting, Crane! Pretty and *still*!

## Scene 4 — "The Temples of the Sun"
**Where/When:** an Asahina temple on the coast
**Beat:** The Asahina examine a charm. It's a fake, made with Bloodspeaker techniques: the work of a descendant of
**Asahina Yajinden** (Iuchiban's artificer). The Asahina are horrified and want it hushed up.
- **Reward:** *Asahina ward-paper* (accessory: halves the fake charms' buffs on nearby enemies).
- Letters found on Chihaya are signed by **a Lion patron**: **Matsu Tadanobu** *(orig.)*, a dishonourable samurai
  stoking the piracy to bleed the Crane (canon 1123).

## Scene 5 — "Gathering Storm" (travel)
**Beat:** A storm crossing. Sailors swear they see the **Orochi** in the waves (a hidden superboss later). Yosuke the
Bold's ship attacks in the storm.
`[BATTLE]` **Yosuke's crew** on deck. Weak: **Katana**, **Water**. The fake charms are the gimmick; no extra hazard.

## Scene 6 — "Island of the Heavenly Sheen"
**Beat:** Ryohei the Eldest, the tactician, offers to surrender his brothers in exchange for safe passage.
`[CHOICE 2]` *Accept* (Ryohei leaves; the final boss is weaker, but Sanae will ask whose head he brought) ·
*refuse* (Ryohei joins the final battle).

## Scene 7 — "The White Squid Shrine" (dungeon)
**Beat:** A sea-cave shrine full of charm-workshops and tide-pools; the White Squid's spirit stirs in the deep water.

## Scene 8 — BOSS: "Katsuo the White Brother"
`[BATTLE]` **Katsuo** (+ Ryohei if he wasn't released):
- Katsuo: Resolve 12. Weak: **Jade**, **Katana**, **Fire**. Maho: pays HP, Taint on hit.
- **Gimmick: fake charms.** Each turn Katsuo hands a new charm to a pirate (or himself): +ATK, +evasion or regen.
  Steal them, or break them with Suki. A shattered charm stuns its wearer for a turn.
KATSUO: Every charm I make is a lie. *(holding one up, smiling)* And look how well they work. Believing is the only
magic left, little Crane.
RENSEI: *(Iaijutsu: Strike)* Then believe this.

## Scene 9 — "The Head of My Enemy" (ending)
**Beat:** Rensei stands over Katsuo with the order still in his ears: *the captain's head*.
- Rensei **brings Katsuo back alive** to face the Emerald Magistrates. (Otomo Shinken arrives too late and takes
  the credit gladly.) This is a fixed story beat, not a choice: Rensei has learned something since Ch1.
- Sanae reads the Lion patron's letters, and is quietly delighted.
LADY SANAE: You were told to bring a head.
RENSEI: I brought a confession, my lady. It has a head attached.
LADY SANAE: *(laughing for the first time in the game)* …Adequate.
→ **Ch4 "The Play No One Performs."**
`[SYS]` **Rewards:**
- Lv ~35.
- The ship (coastal travel).
- Asahina ward-paper.
- The Matsu Tadanobu letters (a Lion Ch4 tie: they help prove the war was engineered).
- *One Leg Stance* comes later (R3).
