# Script — Phoenix Ch1 · "The Silent Kami" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *The Blood of Family* (Phoenix side) + orig. Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Isawa Haruki · **Rank 1**, Lv 1–8 · **late autumn 1122** · Shiro no Kamatori → the Lion border.
**Crossroads:** joins **Lion Ch1** Scenes 7–8 (the O-Tamai climax).
**Systems introduced:**
- Talent **Attune**; **Water + Void** Affinity; **Void spells paid in VP**, starting with *Drawing the Void*
- field spells **Sense / Commune / Summon**; path action **Bless** (Theology-gated)
- guest **Shiba Ashimita**; pair-kata tease *Eyes of the Phoenix*

---

## Scene 1 — "Counting on Fingers" (prologue)
**Where/When:** Shiro no Kamatori, Hoju's study, evening
**Cast:** Haruki; his teacher **Isawa Hoju** (old, warm, absent-minded about everything except history)
**Beat:** Haruki copies Hoju's unfinished family history. He counts the five elements on his fingers when nervous.
Hoju teases him about it.
ISAWA HOJU: Five fingers, five elements. And when you run out of fingers?
HARUKI: Then I start again on the other hand, sensei.
ISAWA HOJU: *(chuckling)* Ten elements! The Council will be thrilled. Go to bed, Haruki. History has waited four
hundred years; it can wait until morning.
`[SYS]` Field spell tutorial: **Commune** with the garden kami (they gossip about the cook), **Summon** a little
water to put out a spilt candle, **Sense** finds a lost brush.

## Scene 2 — "The Kami Fall Silent"
**Where/When:** the same study, that night
**Beat:** A scream. Hoju is dead at his desk, bleeding from the eyes: maho. In the garden, Commune returns nothing:
**the kami have gone silent.**
HARUKI: *(at the garden shrine, hands shaking)* Please. Please answer. You always answer.
*(Nothing. Only wind. Haruki counts his fingers. Then counts them again.)*
`[SYS]` **Spirit-wasteland debuff** on this map (spells −1 tier). The status icon explains: "The kami are afraid."

## Scene 3 — "The Lowest Member of the Embassy"
**Beat:** Hoju's nephew, the arrogant heir **Isawa Kamasari**, declares the Lion guilty. A Phoenix embassy rides west
with the Imperial magistrate **Miya Yachiko**. Haruki is assigned to carry scrolls (Rank 1). Kamasari's yojimbo
**Shiba Ashimita** is told to "keep the boy from falling off his horse".
ISAWA KAMASARI: You were his favourite. You will carry his papers. Try not to weep on them.
SHIBA ASHIMITA: *(later, aside, wincing at a sound only he can hear)* Ignore him. My grandfather has been
screaming in my ear since I was born. Kamasari is much quieter.
- **Guest: Shiba Ashimita** joins (*Guardian*, *Grandfather's Storm* = spirit sense). Banter: Haruki offers to
  try to calm the ghost; it screams louder; they both laugh for the first time.

## Scene 4 — "The Jade Chains"
**Where/When:** Hoju's study (before departure) — optional search
**Beat:** In Hoju's chest: the **jade chains** he forged long ago. The lock is warm in Haruki's hand, and cold when
he faces west. Hoju's history says a Bloodspeaker was bound a generation ago by killing the young shugenja **Kaiki**.
- **Key item: jade-chain compass** (shared with Lion Ch1).
- *(Under the chains, a child's folded note in Haruki's own handwriting. He puts it away without reading it.)*

## Scene 5 — "What the Phoenix Burned For"
**Where/When:** the Magonojo river, the burned warehouses
**Beat:** Reading the ashes (**Reflections of Pan Ku** on a charred crate stamp) reveals Crane army supply marks.
The Phoenix **were secretly supplying the Crane**. Kamasari orders the crates thrown in the river.
HARUKI: Sensei never told me.
SHIBA ASHIMITA: Sensei was a historian. Historians know which things not to write down *yet*.
`[CHOICE]` *keep a stamped crate lid* (evidence; `[FLAG phoenix_supply_proof]`, useful in the Lion/Crane
politics later) · *let it burn*

## Scene 6 — "The Broken Shrine"
**Where/When:** the despoiled roadside shrine
**Beat:** A restless spirit wanders the wreckage. **Bless** path action tutorial: lead the confused spirit (an old
shrine-keeper) to rest. The compass is freezing here: *this* is where the skull was.
- `[BATTLE]` Shards of the broken shrine's guardian (weak to **Water**, **Bo**). First **Void spell** use: *Drawing
  the Void* (+VP) → Boosted **Path to Inner Peace** at VP 3 heals the whole party (tutorial for mass healing).

## Scene 7 — "The Silent Hurricane" (mid-boss)
**Where/When:** Akodo Hiruto's castle, the old west tower
**Beat:** Among the frightened dead of the Lion castle is **Ashimoshi**, a samurai trapped in a silent hurricane,
mad with it. Ashimita hears him first.
`[BATTLE]` **Ashimoshi** (ghost). Resolve 6. Weak: **Water**, **Void**; physical attacks only do half
(`enemies.md` spirits). His winds silence spells every other turn, so Haruki must time his casts.
*(After: Ashimoshi, calm at last, points west across the courtyard: "The one in the sleeping man.")*

## Scene 8 — Crossroads: O-Tamai (= Lion Ch1 Scenes 7–8)
- If Shizue is recruited: the full shared chapter; the route is chosen by Shizue's player choice.
- If not: **Akodo Ijako** stands in as the Lion contact; route **A · The Binding** is used.
- Haruki's unique line during the binding, when O-Tamai mocks the Phoenix for killing a child (Kaiki) to bind it:
HARUKI: *(very still)* Yes. We did. And I know exactly what that costs. *(he lifts the chain)* That is why I won't
let you make anyone pay it again.

## Scene 9 — "The Note" (ending)
**Beat:** The skull goes home in jade. The Council thanks Kamasari. Haruki is not mentioned. That night he finally
unfolds the child's note from Hoju's chest: in his eight-year-old handwriting, *"I'm sorry, Mieko. I didn't mean to.
Please wake up."* Hoju kept it for twelve years and never said a word.
HARUKI: *(alone, counting on his fingers, stopping at five)* …You knew, sensei. You knew all along.
→ **Ch2 "The Silent Court"**: Hoju's memorial court, where the second ceremony master is Mieko.
