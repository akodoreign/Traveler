# Script — Dragon Ch4 · "The Battle of the White Stag" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *Hindsight* Part III (Hepler & Hepler, 2000). Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Kitsuki Gendo · Rank 3, Lv 41–45 · **the year 400 → home (summer 1123)**.
**Octopath shape:** the war camp (hub) → a battle chain on three fronts (set-piece) → the harbour → the gaijin
flagship (boss) → the choice → home.
- **Set-piece:** the **three-front battle chain** (like the finale's Act 1 defence).
- **Choices:** 2.
- **History meter:** active to the end. Its final value colours the epilogue.

---

## Scene 1 — "Wearing a Dead Man's Face"
**Beat:** Gendo, disguised (*Many Faces* if Mitsue is present; otherwise make-up and Seikidera's armour), rides out
as the **Crane Champion Doji Seikidera** to rally the merchant fleets. He knows the man's every recorded habit: he
filed them.
GENDO: *(as Seikidera, to the fleet captains; internal)* He tilted his head left when he lied. I'll keep mine very
straight.
- **Yosai returns** out of the ink, singed and delighted: "Twenty-one! The ink tried to eat me. It didn't like the
  taste."

## Scene 2 — "Three Fronts" (set-piece: battle chain)
`[SYS]` **Three linked event battles**, in any order. Each won front gives a buff for the boss:
1. **The land battle:** hold the line until the **Dragon and Centipede** relief arrives (a defence for N turns).
2. **The Mantis council:** force the Mantis to reject the gaijin. Talk (Discern on **Gusai Iami**: his secret debt
   to the gaijin) or a duel with his champion.
3. **The lighthouse peninsula:** keep the **Isawa wards** up. Protect the boy **Isawa Sanro** (the future Asahina) in
   an escort battle: if he falls, History −30%.
- Each front gives the boss fight a buff (artillery, ward, Mantis ships). A lost front is still lost history, so the
  meter drops.

## Scene 3 — "The Harbour Choked With Wrecks" (dungeon)
**Beat:** Cannon fire, sharks, gaijin in the tunnels under the harbour. A short dungeon. The weapon-type enemies are
gaijin musketeers (weak to **Yari**, **Bo**). **History rule:** don't use the gunpowder barrels (each use: History
−5%).

## Scene 4 — BOSS: "Kakita Kumpei and the Gaijin Flagship"
**Beat:** On the flagship's deck, **Kakita Kumpei**, the failed artist who helped kill the monks and believed he was
saving the Empire, holds the **last vial of kami-ichor** (Togashi's blood), the party's only way home. **Akuro** is
not here: he has already gone, into the ink, toward 1123.
`[BATTLE]` **Kumpei + flagship cannons**:
- The cannons fire on a timer (part of the three-front buff system: if the artillery front was won, they fire
  half as often).
- Kumpei: Resolve 14. Weak: **Katana**, **Water**, **Air**. He paints "living ink" sigils that copy one party buff
  per turn.
- **The vial:** don't let him smash it. Break him before turn 8, or he throws it overboard (Yosai dives after it in a
  cutscene; the story is unchanged, but there's no reward).
KAKITA KUMPEI: I was going to save the Empire. *(beat)* Nobody ever asked me to paint anything else.
GENDO: Then paint me the year 1122, Kumpei. Exactly as it is. *(beat)* Nobody else can.
`[CHOICE 1]` *Spare Kumpei* (he paints the way home, weeping; a quieter epilogue) · *let the Emperor's guards take
him* (he paints it under guard).

## Scene 5 — "The Emperor's Offer" (Choice 2)
**Beat:** The boy-Emperor **Hantei Kusada** offers "Gendo-of-no-family" glory, a clan of his own, and a place in the
histories.
`[CHOICE 2]` *Accept* (History −50%: a dark epilogue vision, and Gendo goes home as a famous name that no one in 1122
recognises) · ***Refuse, and erase yourself*** (canonical): the record of "Gendo" is burned, and the victory is
credited to Seikidera's legend. **Honor +5, told to no one.**
HANTEI KUSADA: You'd give up being remembered?
GENDO: Majesty, I was an archive clerk for nine years. *(smiling)* I know exactly how little being remembered is
worth. It's the remembering that matters. Let them remember *him*.

## Scene 6 — "Home"
**Beat:** Through Kumpei's portal, back to the cove cave on Tortoise Island, summer 1123. Lord **Kitsuki Yasu** hears
the report, doesn't believe a word, and suggests a career as a jester. Gendo smiles.
KITSUKI YASU: Time travel. The year four hundred. A boy Emperor who liked your *spectacles*.
GENDO: Yes, my lord.
KITSUKI YASU: …You'd make a fine jester, Gendo.
GENDO: *(polishing his spectacles)* Thank you, my lord. I'll consider it.
- **Akuro's prophecy** stays with him: "The son will become the dark brother… seven mortals will rise." The finale
  and epilogue quote it.
→ **Finale.**
`[SYS]` **Rewards:**
- Lv ~45.
- **Honor +5** (refuse route).
- Seikidera's *Shukujo* is returned to the past (no item); a pressed cherry blossom from 400 (accessory: History).
- *Time Between Breaths* kata at Lv 51 (R4 = Mirumoto 3).
