# Script — Crab Ch2 · "Winter Court at Kyuden Hida" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *Mirror, Mirror* Part I (Brandes & Hepler, 1999). **Heavily softened** (no mutilation shown; the
victim's face is "taken" offscreen; no child endangerment). Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Hiruma Natsuko · Rank 1→2, Lv 18–25 · **winter 1122–23** · Kyuden Hida.
**Octopath shape:** castle-town hub (court) → investigation (path actions) → the basement donjon (dungeon) → boss.
- **Gimmick:** the troll break-out (the boss arena has the troll as a hazard).
- **Choices:** 2 (whom to accuse; the duel).

---

## Scene 1 — "One Glance from the Bear"
**Where/When:** Kyuden Hida, 6th floor, the audience hall
**Beat:** The karo **Kaiu Takakana** (drill-sergeant, naginata, four hundred funerals behind him) presents Natsuko to
**Hida Kisada**. The Great Bear, in the Armor of the Shadow Warrior, looks at her once and says nothing. Takakana
explains the job in the corridor: vet **Isawa Mutsuye**, the bride of Natsuko's own family daimyo **Hiruma Kage**,
before the betrothal is announced. *Quietly*, because a junior Hiruma is someone nobody at court will notice.
KAIU TAKAKANA: You have until the announcement. If she's Tainted, a Scorpion, or a fool, I want to know before
the Champion does.
NATSUKO: And if she's none of those?
KAIU TAKAKANA: Then you've wasted my time, and I'll thank you for it.
`[SYS]` Rank-1 rule: Kisada never speaks to her this chapter. Takakana's "flare speech" plays as a tutorial: red
flare (attack), green (retreat), **blue** (something no one has seen before). Natsuko gets one blue flare (key item,
used in Ch4).

## Scene 2 — "Court Is a Siege" (hub)
**Beat:** Exploring Winter Court:
- **Isawa Mutsuye:** kind, nervous, plainly in love with Kage.
- **Asako Kuhime:** her friend, lovely, attentive, *always* nearby.
- **Isawa Akiko:** 13, talkative ("except the bit about the carp pond").
- **Ide Keiyomi:** 16, a Unicorn girl who dances a gaijin dance.
- **Shiba Noboru:** Mutsuye's humble yojimbo.
- **Shosuro Shiko:** drunk, disgraced, venomous wit; a great gossip.
- **Yogo Shigeru** and **Kuni Taira:** creepy; they study a captive troll in the basement.
- **Hida Sachiko** ("Sach watch"), **Hiruma Ryoichi**, **Yasuki Kurako** (the flustered hostess).
- The archery contest (shooting at targets, softened from the source) → **one dialogue choice** (OCTOPATH-FIT).
- **Sumai** with the Wall Crabs at the barracks wins their trust (they tell her about the troll's chains, which
  someone has filed).
- **Guests offered:** the Mirror, Mirror pregens (Hiruma Goro, Ide Fujio, Daidoji Sakura, Kitsu Takeo, Yogo Mai,
  Isawa Michio), via side stories.
SHOSURO SHIKO: *(to Natsuko, sipping)* A Hiruma at court! How refreshing. You look at the exits like they owe you
money.
NATSUKO: They do. Nobody here knows where they are.

## Scene 3 — "The Girl Who Danced"
**Beat:** **Ide Keiyomi** is found dead in the women's shrine on the mountainside: maho, her face *gone* (implied,
shown as a veil and the horrified witnesses). Evidence surfaces at once:
- **Mutsuye** has fresh Taint (a *Sinful Dreams* curse; Haruki's *Speak to the Soul* or Michio can read it as
  "laid on her, not grown in her").
- Forged scrolls point at **Yogo Shigeru**.
- **Discern** on the murder knife's spirit (if Gendo is in the party): "held by a small woman's hand, a
  *beautiful* one".
- **Clue only a scout notices:** the "witness" who found the body, **Asako Kuhime**, has mud on her hem from the
  *Wall path*, not the shrine path.
**Kisada demands a culprit by nightfall.** Testimony, not truth, will decide.

## Scene 4 — The Accusation (Choice 1)
`[CHOICE 1]`
- *Accuse Mutsuye:* her yojimbo **Shiba Noboru** demands a duel → `[BATTLE]` vs. Noboru (non-lethal). She is
  confined.
- *Accuse Yogo Shigeru:* he is executed at once, and the real killer walks.
- *Name no one, and ask for one more night* (needs the Wall-path clue + Takakana's trust from Sumai): Kisada grants
  it with one sentence. **This is the canonical route.**
HIDA KISADA: *(the only line he speaks to her all chapter)* One night. Then I choose for you.

## Scene 5 — "The Donjon" (dungeon)
**Where/When:** Kyuden Hida's basement donjon and trap catacombs (secret exits behind the sake barrels)
**Beat:** Natsuko follows Kuhime's mud trail into the donjon. The troll's chains have been filed through.
- `[BATTLE]` donjon goblins and loose experiments (weak to **Yari**, **Jade**, **Tetsubo**).
- The trap catacombs leading to the Wall are a Crab dungeon: rollers, spiked pits, jade-dust trip lines.
  **Natsuko knows them** (an Athletics/Hunting interaction prompt shortcut).

## Scene 6 — BOSS: "The Puppet and the Troll"
**Beat:** In the deepest cell: the "killer", a **maho-crazed servant puppet** with the stolen face-veil, and the
troll breaking loose.
`[BATTLE]` **Maho puppet + loose troll**:
- **Gimmick: the troll is a hazard**, not a target. Each turn it lashes at whoever has the highest threat (Natsuko's
  taunt can draw it *away* from allies, which is her role). Breaking the puppet (Suki) makes the troll turn on *it*.
- Puppet: Resolve 8. Weak: **Jade**, **Fire**, **Yari**. It casts *No Pure Breaths* (a silence + damage curse).
`[CHOICE 2]` *Spare the puppet* (it is a bewitched servant; it dies the next day anyway, but his family is told he
was innocent) · *finish it*.

## Scene 7 — "Testimony, Not Truth" (ending)
**Beat:** The puppet "confesses" (it was made to). Kisada accepts it. Natsuko knows the real killer walked: someone
small, beautiful, with Wall mud on her hem. But **Mutsuye is Tainted anyway** (the curse can't be undone in time). She
leaves the court in disgrace that night. *(Her death is told, not shown: Ch3's opening.)*
NATSUKO: *(to Takakana)* The puppet didn't do it alone. Someone held its strings.
KAIU TAKAKANA: I know. *(beat)* So does the Champion. A culprit is not the same as the truth, Hiruma. The Wall only
needs a culprit. You and I need the truth. Keep looking.
*(Last shot: Asako Kuhime comforting a weeping Kage, her hand on his shoulder, her eyes very blue in the lantern
light.)*
