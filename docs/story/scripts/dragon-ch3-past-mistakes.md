# Script — Dragon Ch3 · "Past Mistakes" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *Hindsight* Part II (Hepler & Hepler, 2000). Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Kitsuki Gendo · Rank 2, Lv 30–35 · **the year 400** · Otosan Uchi, Shiro Izumida, the sea.
**Octopath shape:** the capital (hub) → three dinners → Shiro Izumida (dungeon) → war council → sea battle (boss).
- **Set-piece:** the **History meter** (persistent, carried over from Ch2).
- **Choices:** 2.
**History meter rules (OCTOPATH-FIT set-piece):**
- Anachronisms lower it: saying "Kitsuki", "Unicorn", "Emerald Magistrate", "Kaiu Wall" (it's *Hida's Wall*),
  "Asahina"; wearing glass spectacles openly; using a katana openly (most samurai carry straight **tachi**).
- Below 50%, party portraits change (a mon fades, a Crane-blue eye). At 0%, a game over ("The World That Should
  Not Be": a short bad-future cutscene, then retry).
- Help-the-history actions raise it.

---

## Scene 1 — "A Man With No Family"
**Beat:** The court's new sensation is "Gendo-of-no-family". The boy-Emperor **Hantei Kusada** wants to see his
spectacles. The **Gozoku** (three clan daimyo who rule through the throne) all want him.
HANTEI KUSADA: Can you see the future through them?
GENDO: *(History meter: careful)* Only very small print, Majesty.

## Scene 2 — "Three Dinners, One Hour" (Choice 1)
`[CHOICE 1]` Accept **one** invitation; the other two daimyo are insulted (each choice gives different clues):
- **Bayushi Atsuki** (corpulent, imperial, bribes rather than blackmails) wants a Bayushi girl given "access" to
  the young Emperor. **Refuse**: it would change the succession that leads to the first Empress. (History +.)
- **Doji Tomeko** (young, newly inherited, sheltered) relies on her Champion **Doji Seikidera** (the ancestral
  sword *Shukujo* rings for its true wielder). She wants limited sea trade.
- **Shiba Katsutoshi** ("the boss you have always hated"). His page is the boy **Isawa Sanro**, the future
  **Isawa Asahina**, already theorising about waking spirits in objects.
GENDO: *(at whichever dinner; internal)* Every history scroll I ever filed has these three in it. None of them got the
seating right.

## Scene 3 — "Why Ushimo Struck"
**Beat:** (With the Ch2 lead, or via the guards.) Ushimo's wife **Tsuhime** is dying of a gaijin-borne disease,
kept out of pain only by gaijin medicine. Her "doctor" drugged Ushimo's sake and planted one sentence. The doctor was
**Togashi Akuro** (a Chameleon tattoo: he wears faces).
- A duplicate of the portal painting hangs by the palace's south entrance. The elderly artisan **Kakita Miyoshi**
  painted it, paid in **anachronistic glass mirrors** (History −: Gendo must buy them back).
`[CHOICE 2]` *Help Tsuhime* (Haruki/Sayo can ease her pain; History +, and Ushimo becomes a Ch4 ally) · *leave the
past alone* (History stays as it is; Ushimo is a broken man in Ch4).

## Scene 4 — "Shiro Izumida" (dungeon)
**Where/When:** a small winter palace ten miles south
**Beat:** The groundskeepers have been massacred (shown as empty halls and overturned braziers; softened). **Musket
balls** in the walls. Burned Ikoma battle plans for the **White Stag**. Tortured spirits scream "It wrote US! Make it
un-be!" and scorch **an exact map of the gaijin invasion** into the floor.
- `[BATTLE]` Gaijin scouts with muskets (a ranged enemy type; weak to **Yari**, **Bo**). **History rule:** don't
  pick up the muskets.
- From the ridge: a gaijin army marching north.

## Scene 5 — "The Missing Champion"
**Beat:** The war council. Atsuki and Katsutoshi would close the gates; Tomeko is drowned out. **Doji Seikidera is
missing**: he rode to a Mantis shipyard to talk peace in secret with the trader **Gusai Iami** and the gaijin.

## Scene 6 — BOSS: "The Parley" (at sea)
**Beat:** Gendo reaches the parley ship too late. The "stableboy" (Akuro) **shoots Seikidera in the back of the
head**. Gaijin and pirates attack.
`[BATTLE]` **Gaijin vanguard + Togashi Akuro**:
- Akuro is **untouchable** (Crab tattoo: he absorbs wounds; Wasp tattoo: extra actions). He **can't be beaten**;
  the battle is won when the **vanguard captain falls** (Resolve 12; weak: **Katana**, **Water**, **Air**).
- Akuro leaves mid-fight, mocking them. *(He kills the Champion anyway: canon.)*
- No extra gimmick (the History meter is the chapter's set-piece; using gunpowder barrels costs History).
TOGASHI AKURO: *(on the rail, unhurried)* I saw how your world ends, little clerk. The son becomes the dark brother.
The jade clan goes black. The dead rule. *(beat)* I'm saving you from it. You should thank me.
GENDO: You're writing a history no one will ever read. *(beat)* I've filed a thousand of those.
*(The powder room explodes. Akuro is gone.)*

## Scene 7 — Ending
**Beat:** Seikidera is dead. Someone must wear his face to rally the merchant fleets for the coming battle, and the
only man in the room who has studied the Crane Champion's every recorded habit is a clerk from 1122.
DOJI TOMEKO: *(white-faced)* They will follow only him.
GENDO: *(slowly taking off his spectacles)* Then they'll follow him, my lady.
→ **Ch4 "The Battle of the White Stag."**
