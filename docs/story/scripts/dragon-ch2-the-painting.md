# Script — Dragon Ch2 · "The Painting" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *Hindsight* Part I end + Part II opening (Hepler & Hepler, 2000). **Softened:** the source's skinned
bodies become monks whose *tattoo-ink was lifted from them* by a ritual (the bodies are found, covered). Plot facts
only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Kitsuki Gendo · Rank 1→2, Lv 20–25 · **winter 1122–23 → the year 400** · Tortoise Island → Otosan
Uchi (past).
**Octopath shape:** island village → wrecked house → cove cave (dungeon) → arrival in the past → boss.
- **Gimmick:** *don't kill the boss* (killing Ushimo would change history).
- **Choices:** 2.
**Note:** Mirumoto secondary school unlocks at R2 during this chapter (the Dragon dojo scene, Scene 2).

---

## Scene 1 — "Tortoise Island"
**Where/When:** a small island village, by borrowed fishing boat
**Beat:** Gendo, Yosai (guest) and whoever else crossed reach **Tortoise Island**. A new wooden house with Dragon
alchemical gear. In the cellar, two more of Yosai's order-brothers, covered in cloth: their tattoos are *gone*, their
skin blank.
TOGASHI YOSAI: *(for once, no joke; then, quietly)* He took their tattoos. Not cut: *lifted*. The ink lived in
them, and he took it. *(beat)* Akuro always did say ink was just patience made visible.
- **An unnatural earthquake** brings the house down (a cutscene). Kakita Kumpei's boat has gone round the cove.

## Scene 2 — "A Sword You Rarely Draw" (R2 unlock)
**Where/When:** a tiny Dragon outpost dojo on the island's ridge (a Mirumoto veteran in exile)
**Beat:** At Rank 2 Gendo can take the **Mirumoto secondary school**. The old swordsman tests him: two swords, two
truths.
OLD MIRUMOTO: You hold the katana like a clerk holds a brush.
GENDO: I was a clerk.
OLD MIRUMOTO: Good. Clerks know that the second stroke corrects the first. Take the wakizashi too.
`[SYS]` Secondary school: Mirumoto R1 *Daisho Technique* (`schools.md`). The first kata pick follows at Lv 25
(*Thunder and Steel*, a pair kata with a shugenja).

## Scene 3 — "The Cove Cave" (dungeon)
**Beat:** A sea-cave holding a **miniature Otosan Uchi** that grows *older* toward its centre: roofs go from tile to
thatch, walls from stone to wood. A **field set-piece is not used** (the gimmick budget goes to the boss); the
miniature is the dungeon's visual theme. Enemies: tide crabs, Akuro's hypnotised guards.
- At the centre: a **portal painted with living tattoo-ink** on silk stretched between two posts. **Kumpei** and an
  old man (**Togashi Akuro**, wearing a fisherman's face) step inside it. Its edges begin to blacken.
`[CHOICE 1]` *Jump in after them* (canonical) · *hesitate*. (The portal pulls you in anyway; your companions remark
on it. Octopath-style: the choice only changes dialogue.)
TOGASHI YOSAI: *(already jumping)* Twenty!
`[SYS]` **Party note:** only **Gendo + up to 3** go through. Anyone else stays behind in the present. The guest
slot stays empty in the past (Yosai is "lost in the ink" until Ch4 and returns for the White Stag finale).

## Scene 4 — "Outside Time"
**Beat:** A breathless limbo of ink and stars. A fall into a **cherry orchard in winter**, in **the year 400**:
- Hantei VI "Kusada", 14 years old, polite, suspecting that he's a puppet
- the **Gozoku** clans rule through him
- gaijin are common, and recently purged
`[SYS]` A **History meter** appears (OCTOPATH-FIT: the past chapters' persistent UI, used in Ch3). Its first lesson:
in the year 400 there are **no Kitsuki**. Gendo must not say his family name.
GENDO: *(muttering, brushing petals from his spectacles)* No Kitsuki. No magistrates. Spectacles are probably
witchcraft. Wonderful.

## Scene 5 — BOSS: "Seppun Ushimo"
**Beat:** In the orchard, the Imperial guard **Seppun Ushimo** cuts down his partner and goes for the boy-Emperor.
Gendo is closest.
`[BATTLE]` **Seppun Ushimo** (perfect iaijutsu):
- Phase 1: a duel exchange (duel UI; Gendo's Mirumoto Daisho lets him use Kenjutsu for Iaijutsu).
- Phase 2: a skirmish. Resolve 10. Weak: **Bo**, **Yari**, **Water** (his despair; Path to Inner Peace *heals*
  him by mistake and lowers his guard).
- **Gimmick: don't kill him.** Ushimo **wants to die**. Below 25% HP he drops his guard and *asks* for it. Lethal
  damage is refused ("History shudders": the History meter drops, and he's left at 1 HP). The battle is won when he
  is **broken (Suki) below 25%**, or hit with a non-lethal skill (*Subdue*, Knockdown).
SEPPUN USHIMO: *(kneeling, offering his throat)* Please. Finish it. I'm at the end of my rope.
GENDO: *(Subdue: Bo strike to the wrist)* That's not your sentence. Someone put it in your mouth.
`[CHOICE 2]` *Ask him why* (it opens the Ch3 lead: his wife **Tsuhime** is dying of a gaijin-borne disease; her
"doctor" drugged his sake) · *hand him to the guards at once* (the lead comes later, from the guards).

## Scene 6 — "The Court's Sensation" (ending)
**Beat:** The boy-Emperor, saved, is fascinated by the stranger in glass spectacles. The court is abuzz. Three gozoku
daimyo send invitations *for the same hour* (Ch3's opening).
HANTEI KUSADA: You fought like a brush, sir. What is your name?
GENDO: *(beat; History meter twitches)* …Gendo, Majesty. Just Gendo.
HANTEI KUSADA: *(delighted)* A man with no family! How *mysterious*.
→ **Ch3 "Past Mistakes."**
`[SYS]` **Rewards:**
- Lv ~25.
- Mirumoto secondary school.
- Ushimo's thanks (a key NPC in Ch3).
- The History meter UI.
- Yosai's absence (he rejoins in Ch4).
