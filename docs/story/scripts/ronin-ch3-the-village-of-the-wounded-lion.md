# Script — Ronin Ch3 · "The Village of the Wounded Lion" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *Past Heroes* (Chapleau), **re-scaled** to 1123.
- The legendary rescue happened in the reign of Hantei XXVI (≈ 300 years ago), so its secret is now enormous:
  **the "rescued prince" crowned as Hantei XXVII was a peasant's son, and the whole Hantei line since descends from
  him.**
- The old survivor "Ji-ji" becomes the survivor's descendant (keeper of a family tale). **Mirumoto Hikaru** (ageless
  under a Togashi Crane tattoo) is still alive.
- **Softened:** the infant's death is implied only (an empty stone basin).

Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Sayo · Rank 2, Lv 30–35 · **early spring 1123** · the Village of the Wounded Lion → the Mountain of the
White Snow (between Phoenix and Dragon lands).
**Octopath shape:** the village (hub) → the climb (dungeon, three stages) → the frozen fortress → boss.
- **Gimmick:** the **bloodstone basin** keeps raising the ghost-guard until it is broken.
- **Choices:** 2.

---

## Scene 1 — "The Re-enactment"
**Beat:** With Jinnai's snake beads, Sayo reaches the **Village of the Wounded Lion**. Every fifty years the
descendants of the five legendary champions (Matsu, Hida, Bayushi, Doji, Mirumoto) climb the mountain to re-enact
the famous rescue of the stolen Crown Prince. This year's climbers are young, proud and very well dressed. They take
the ronin girl as their porter.
MATSU CLIMBER *(orig.: Matsu Hanae)*: You'll carry the ropes, wave-girl.
SAYO: *(cheerfully)* Of course, my lady. *(to herself)* And I'll be the only one who knows how to tie them.
- **The snow mounds at the village gate:** the villagers leave offerings and never say why.
- **Ji-ji's great-grandson** *(orig.: old Mosuke)* tells a family tale he's never understood: "Grandfather said the
  real prince never came down the mountain."

## Scene 2 — "The Climb" (dungeon, 3 stages)
**Beat:** Three stages: the cedar slopes, the ice ledges, the cloud bridge. **Field interactions** are rope points
(Athletics/Hunting prompts, Sayo's specialty). Each stage has a scene where the grandchildren's legend contradicts
what the mountain shows: a Crane's grave with a Lion arrow in it; gunpowder scorches on a gate; a Mirumoto's
abandoned armour facing *away* from the castle.
- `[BATTLE]` Snow beasts, frost spirits (weak to **Fire**, **Yumi**).
- **Mirumoto Hikaru** (ageless, silent, a living book of history in tattoo-ink) appears at each stage and walks
  ahead. He won't speak until he sees "the child in its bed of stone".

## Scene 3 — "The Truth in the Snow" (the fortress)
**Beat:** In the frozen fortress (the old winter palace), the **rebel ghost-guard** replays the night like a play
that never ends. The truth:
- There was no oni. A Crab lord married to the other heir had rebel guards **kidnap** the prince.
- The champions fought one another.
- Mirumoto fled at the baby's cry, on Togashi's prophetic order.
- The child **died** here (an empty stone basin of **bloodstones**; implied).
- Bayushi carried a *villager's newborn* down the mountain as the "rescued prince"…
`[SYS]` Each truth is revealed by a **ghost-replay interaction** (Discern-style prompts on 5 ghosts), not a puzzle.

## Scene 4 — BOSS: "The Ghost-Guard and the Basin"
`[BATTLE]` **Rebel ghost-guard ×3 + the bloodstone basin**:
- **Gimmick: the basin raises the dead.** Every 2 turns, a fallen ghost-guard rises again at full strength. The
  **basin itself** is a target (Resolve 10; weak: **Jade**, **Water**, **Bo**). Break it, and the guards fall for
  good.
- Ghost-guards: Resolve 5. Weak: **Fire**, **Katana**; physical damage halved (spirits).
- Mirumoto Hikaru **joins as a guest for this fight** (*Endless Strike*: up to 3 extra hits; `kata.md` §4).

## Scene 5 — "The Child in Its Bed of Stone"
**Beat:** With the basin broken, Hikaru finally speaks, after three hundred years.
MIRUMOTO HIKARU: *(voice like a door unused for centuries)* I ran. Togashi told me to run, and I ran, and I have
remembered it every day since. *(beat)* Someone must carry it down now. Not me. I've carried it long enough.
`[CHOICE 1]` *Tell the grandchildren the truth* (they are shattered; the eldest, Hanae, swears silence: "it would
burn the Empire") · *let them keep the legend* (Sayo carries it alone).

## Scene 6 — "Only One Comes Down"
**Beat:** The legend says only one champion returned. On the descent, in a blizzard, Sayo makes sure *everyone*
returns: a ronin girl tying knots for samurai who would never thank her. **Hikaru stays on the mountain.**
`[CHOICE 2]` What does Sayo tell the village? *The legend* (peace; the offerings continue) · *the truth, to old
Mosuke only* (his family's tale is finally whole: a quiet beat; `[FLAG mosuke_knows]`).
SAYO: *(at the snow mounds, leaving her own offering)* You weren't punished for anything. You were just in the way.
*(beat)* I know what that's like.

## Scene 7 — Ending
**Beat:** Sayo now carries a secret that could topple the Hantei dynasty: every emperor for three hundred years has
been a peasant's descendant. The Order of the Snake will want it. So will others.
SAYO: *(on the road, to no one)* Blood means nothing. *(beat)* Funny. That's the one thing samurai would kill to stop
anyone saying.
→ **Ch4 "The Oracle's Choice."**
