# Script — Unicorn Ch3 · "The Noblest Blood" — DRAFT v0.1

**Source:** *Fortune's Lost* Part III, first half (Brandes & Hepler, 1999). **Toned down** (the mass grave and the
possessed children are implied, never shown). Plot facts only; **all dialogue is original**.
**Traveler:** Ide Tamotsu · Rank 2, Lv 30–35 · **early spring 1123** · the Ojatara Valleys.
**Octopath shape:** the valley (hub) → the tower rescue → the woods → the Dragon camp → the jade mines (dungeon) →
boss.
- **Set-piece:** **Covered Ears**. With wax in, possession by hearing is impossible, but **spoken path actions are
  disabled** and dialogue happens through written notes (shown as handwriting). Remove the wax at safe points.
- **Choices:** 2.

---

## Scene 1 — "The Eight-Pointed Star"
**Beat:** A maho sacrifice victim is found at the centre of an eight-pointed star scorched into a field: a Seppun
courier, royal blood. (Akodo Rin, the stalker's original target, lives because of Ch2, and knows it.) Old **Iuchi
Kurosho**, maddened by his son's death, has summoned the **Shuten Doji of Regret**. Its servants' voices possess
anyone who hears them.
IUCHI KARASU: *(behind his porcelain mask, deadpan)* Regret. Wonderful. I was a prisoner in the Shadowlands for six
months and I never once regretted anything. Except leaving.
- **Guest: Iuchi Karasu** joins (*Jade Strike*, deadpan quips).
`[SYS]` **Wax earplugs** (key item from Ch2) → the **Covered Ears** status, toggled at map points.

## Scene 2 — "The Girl in the Tower"
**Beat:** The 9-year-old heir **Iuchi Shahai** has barricaded herself in a tower with wax in her own ears. Possessed
servants wander the halls below, singing. The party moves through with ears covered: no talking, only gesture
prompts and written notes. (A short set-piece walk; enemies are avoided or fought silently.)
SHAHAI: *(her note, pushed under the door in a child's hand)* ARE YOU REAL OR ARE YOU SINGING
TAMOTSU: *(his note back)* REAL. I BROUGHT RICE CAKES.
*(The door opens a crack. A small hand takes the rice cake.)*
- *(The source's secret, softened: Shahai is marked by the demon. It is revealed in Ch4, not here.)*

## Scene 3 — "The Lord in the Woods"
**Beat:** Daimyo **Iuchi Daiyu** ("touched by the Sun") is in the woods talking to the animals, who have kept him
safe. He has finally understood the war.
IUCHI DAIYU: The woodchucks told me everything, Tamotsu. Everything my karo wouldn't. *(beat, broken)* Twenty years
I listened to animals so I wouldn't have to listen to men.
`[CHOICE 1]` *Comfort him* (Compassion; Daiyu rallies his household) · *tell him the whole truth about his father*
(Kurosho; Honor +, but Daiyu is shattered until Ch4).

## Scene 4 — "Ride In Deaf" (persuasion)
**Beat:** The Dragon army under the blunt, resentful generals **Mirumoto Tsukaru** and **Tabezu** won't fight "a
Unicorn family feud". Tamotsu must persuade them: **Barter** (supplies, a trade route) and Etiquette, then
demonstrate the wax. (If Gendo is present he speaks Dragon to them; a banter bonus.)
MIRUMOTO TSUKARU: You want three thousand Dragons to ride into battle with wax in their ears?
TAMOTSU: I want three thousand Dragons to ride *out* of it again, general. The wax is the cheap part.
`[CHOICE 2]` *Promise the Mirumoto the jade from the mines* (they ride; a future trade cost) · *appeal to honour
alone* (needs high Honor; free if it succeeds).

## Scene 5 — "The Jade Mines" (dungeon)
**Beat:** The jade mines, silent for months. A grave nobody claims (implied: mounds, prayer strips, Karasu's silence).
They need **jade** to fight the demon in Ch4.
- `[BATTLE]` Risen dead (weak to **Jade**, **Fire**, **Bo**; immune to Terror).
- Covered Ears is off in the mines (the servants can't reach this deep), so normal play resumes.

## Scene 6 — BOSS: "What the Mine Remembers"
`[BATTLE]` **Risen dead of the jade mines** (a mass of the dead + a **mine-guardian** corpse in old Iuchi armour):
- Guardian: Resolve 12. Weak: **Jade** (the raw jade lying around: a free key-item attack), **Fire**, **Yari**.
- No extra gimmick (the budget went to Covered Ears).
- Defeating it frees the dead (motes of light), and Karasu prays for them in silence.

## Scene 7 — Ending
**Beat:** Carts of jade. A Dragon army with wax in its ears. A child who trusts Tamotsu. A lord awake at last.
Ahead: Shiro Iuchi in its wind tunnel, where old Kurosho waits with Regret.
TAMOTSU: *(to Karasu, loading jade)* Do you regret it? The six months?
IUCHI KARASU: *(beat)* I regret that I came back to *this*. *(beat)* No. I don't. Pass me another stone.
→ **Ch4 "Regret."**
