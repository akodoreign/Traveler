MATSU KIKU: (mortified) A Matsu painting like a Crane. If my family knew, they'd—

**Payoff:** MATSU KIKU: "Maybe I don't have to stop being what I am in battle. Maybe I just need something to put down afterwards."
[UPGRADE *Battle-Joy* → no self-debuff afterward (she "puts it down")]
