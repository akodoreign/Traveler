# SIDE STORIES — Guests 7–12: the Phoenix Inquisitor team (Michio's group)

All six are offered at **Kyuden Hida Winter Court** (Crab Ch2). Together they form an optional chain led by Isawa
Michio. Their secrets **mirror Nashiko**, so each SS2 ends with a small **"virtue echo"** that foreshadows the finale
debate. The Kitsu Takeo & Yogo Mai romance runs across their SS2s.
**Team bonus:** recruiting all six unlocks **SS-TEAM "The Inquisitors' Last Hunt"** (after Crab Ch4).

---

## 7. Hiruma Goro — Crab / Hida bushi
**Skill:** *Troll-Breaker* (bare-handed heavy hit, 2) · **Banter:** Natsuko
### SS1 — "Bodyguard for Hire" · Crab Ch2 · Lv 18+
(The barracks. A huge Crab with a bent katana at his hip arm-wrestles three Wall veterans at once, and wins.)
HIRUMA GORO: The Phoenix hired me to guard their inquisitor. He's very rude. I'm very bored.
HIRUMA GORO: (looking at Natsuko) A Hiruma! Wrestle me. If you win, I'll guard *you* instead.
[PA Sumai Goro] (win or a valiant loss)
HIRUMA GORO: (on the floor, laughing) Ha! Done. I'll tell the inquisitor I quit. He'll be thrilled.
[GUEST +Hiruma Goro]
### SS2 — "The Bent Sword" · after Crab Ch3 · Lv 32+
(Goro's family hall on the Wall. His elder brother wears their father's perfect sword. Goro's is bent.)
HIRUMA GORO: My brother got Father's sword. I got the one that bent on a troll's skull.
HIRUMA GORO: (turning it over) …Once, on the Wall, I felt an ancestor *proud* of me. Just once. Holding this.
HIRUMA GORO: Then my brother laughed, and it went away.
### Task — a troll attack on the brother's watchtower; the brother's perfect sword shatters
[BATTLE b_ss_goro_troll]
(Goro finishes the troll with the bent sword. His brother stares.)
HIRUMA GORO'S BROTHER: …How did that thing survive?
HIRUMA GORO: It's *bent*. Bent things know how to take a hit.
**Virtue echo — Courage:** HIRUMA GORO: "Courage isn't having the best sword. It's using the one you've got."
[UPGRADE *Troll-Breaker* → 3 uses; ignores armour]

## 8. Ide Fujio — Unicorn / Ide emissary
**Skills:** *Scandalous Letter* (enemy skips a turn, 1) · *Encourage* (buff, 2) · **Banter:** Tamotsu, Rensei
### SS1 — "Letters to Everyone" · Crab Ch2 · Lv 18+
(A dashing Ide in a corner of the court, writing furiously. Ladies read over his shoulder and gasp.)
IDE FUJIO: Letters! Romantic, scandalous, anonymous. The whole court reads them. Nobody knows they're mine.
IDE FUJIO: (lowering his voice) Except someone's started *forging* them. Nasty ones. Ruining people.
### Task — find the forger (Eavesdrop/Discern three suspects → a Yogo scribe paid by an unknown woman)
IDE FUJIO: A woman paid them. Lovely, they said. Blue eyes. (shuddering) I *never* write about blue eyes.
[GUEST +Ide Fujio]
### SS2 — "The One Who Read Too Closely" · after Unicorn Ch3 · Lv 32+
IDE FUJIO: (unusually quiet) There was someone. Years ago. A shugenja. She thought every letter I wrote was about her.
IDE FUJIO: They weren't. When she found out, she — (beat) — she turned to maho. To make me love her.
### Task — Fujio's ex-lover, now a maho-tsukai, is in a ruined teahouse; she wants him back
[BATTLE b_ss_fujio_maho] (talk-down possible: a Charm / Etiquette check)
IDE FUJIO: (to her) I never loved you. I'm sorry. I only loved writing about love. (beat) That was cruel of me.
**Virtue echo — Sincerity:** IDE FUJIO: "I wrote a thousand pretty lies. This is the first true letter. It's very short."
[UPGRADE *Scandalous Letter* → 2 uses]

## 9. Daidoji Sakura — Crane / Daidoji + Shiba
**Skill:** *Blank Scroll* (Void guard, 2) · **Banter:** Rensei, Haruki
### SS1 — "The Deserter" · Crab Ch2 · Lv 18+
(A Crane yojimbo in Phoenix colours: a Daidoji who left her family's service. Court whispers "deserter".)
DAIDOJI SAKURA: They tell you I ran. They don't tell you where from.
DAIDOJI SAKURA: A gorge. Filled with pitch. My general wanted to set it alight with three hundred peasants inside. "A trap for the Crab."
DAIDOJI SAKURA: I refused. I left. The Shiba took me in.
### Task — a Daidoji agent has come to bring her back in disgrace
[BATTLE b_ss_sakura_agent] (or Charm: shame the agent with the truth)
[GUEST +Daidoji Sakura]
### SS2 — "My Mother's Grave" · after Crane Ch4 · Lv 42+
(A Daidoji family graveyard. Sakura kneels at her mother's stone.)
DAIDOJI SAKURA: She died believing I was a coward. Everyone told her so.
DAIDOJI SAKURA: I've waited my whole life for her to approve of me. (beat) She can't. She's dead.
[IF haruki in party] HARUKI: (gently) Would you like to ask her? (Commune / Ancestral Guidance) [END]
(The mother's spirit — if summoned — says only: "Three hundred. I heard. You did well.")
**Virtue echo — Courtesy** (the Crane's weak virtue is Courage; Sakura answers with both):
DAIDOJI SAKURA: "The bravest thing I ever did was to politely refuse."
[UPGRADE *Blank Scroll* → party-wide Void guard, 2 uses]

## 10. Kitsu Takeo — Lion / Akodo cavalry
**Skill:** *Charge* (line attack, 2) · **Banter:** Shizue
### SS1 — "The Silent Ancestors" · Crab Ch2 · Lv 18+
(A Lion cavalryman of the Kitsu — the family that speaks with ancestors — who has never heard one.)
KITSU TAKEO: Every Kitsu hears the ancestors. My cousins, my sisters. Children of six.
KITSU TAKEO: I've never heard a word. They're *silent* for me. (beat) I ride very fast so nobody notices.
### Task — a cavalry race against Lion braggarts (a turn-race battle)
[BATTLE b_ss_takeo_race]
[GUEST +Kitsu Takeo]
### SS2 — "The Secret Wife" (with Yogo Mai) · after Lion Ch4 · Lv 42+
KITSU TAKEO: I'm married. Secretly. My family arranged it to settle a debt. She's kind. I'm kind to her. We don't love each other.
KITSU TAKEO: (quietly) And I love Yogo Mai. And she's Scorpion. And everything about it is impossible.
### Task — Takeo's secret wife, Kitsu Ayame, asks the party to help her leave him (she loves someone else too)
> CHOICE
  - "Help them both honestly." → a joint petition to the Kitsu elders (Etiquette); a dignified divorce
  - "Keep the secret." → nothing changes
(At the end, at the family shrine, Takeo hears — for the first time — a voice.)
ANCESTOR: …*Finally* you've stopped lying to yourself, boy. We couldn't hear you over the noise.
**Virtue echo — Honor:** KITSU TAKEO: "Honour was keeping a promise I never wanted. Now it's telling the truth about it."
[UPGRADE *Charge* → *Ancestor's Charge* (line attack + ancestor strike), 2 uses]

## 11. Yogo Mai — Scorpion / Yogo shugenja
**Skills:** *Ward* (2) · *Confession* (reveal and lower defence, 1) · **Banter:** Mitsue
### SS1 — "The Sweet Yogo" · Crab Ch2 · Lv 18+
(A gentle Scorpion shugenja serving tea with a sweet smile. Prisoners confess everything to her within minutes.)
YOGO MAI: I just ask nicely. (smiling) People like to tell me things.
### Task — the castle's goblin prisoner won't talk about the sabotaged traps; Mai asks the party to sit in
YOGO MAI: (to the goblin, sweetly) Tell me about the traps, little one. (the goblin sobs out everything)
YOGO MAI: (to the party, afterwards, a little too pleased) …I'm *very* good at this.
[GUEST +Yogo Mai]
### SS2 — "The Yogo Curse" (with Kitsu Takeo) · after Lion Ch4 · Lv 42+
YOGO MAI: The Yogo are cursed. Since our founder. Everyone we love betrays us, or dies.
YOGO MAI: (very quietly) That's why I like confessions. When people tell me everything, they can't betray me.
YOGO MAI: (beat) Takeo has never once lied to me. It terrifies me.
### Task — a Yogo elder insists Mai prove the curse by sending Takeo away
> CHOICE
  - "Stand by her." → (a debate-UI mini round with the elder: love vs. the curse)
  - "Let her choose alone." → (she chooses Takeo anyway)
**Virtue echo — Duty & Loyalty:** YOGO MAI: "The curse says everyone betrays us. So I'll be the one who doesn't."
[UPGRADE *Confession* → 2 uses; also reveals the enemy's next action]

## 12. Isawa Michio — Phoenix / Earth tensai, Inquisitor
**Skills:** *Amaterasu's Fire* (anti-Taint, 2) · *Earth Ward* (1) · **Banter:** Haruki
### SS1 — "The Rude Inquisitor" · Crab Ch2 · Lv 18+
(A brilliant, arrogant young Phoenix inquisitor lecturing the Crab about the Taint they've fought for a thousand years.)
ISAWA MICHIO: The Crab use jade like children use blankets. Crude. Wasteful.
NATSUKO: (if present) Want to see what the Taint does to a man who's never been on the Wall?
ISAWA MICHIO: (sniffing) I've read *everything* about the Taint.
### Task — Michio insists on examining the basement troll in person; it gets loose; he freezes
[BATTLE b_ss_michio_troll] (the party saves him)
ISAWA MICHIO: (shaken, then recovering his dignity) …The books did not mention the *smell*.
[GUEST +Isawa Michio]
### SS2 — "The Limit" · after Phoenix Ch4 · Lv 42+
ISAWA MICHIO: (alone, unusually quiet) I've read everything. Every book in Kyuden Isawa about the Taint.
ISAWA MICHIO: And none of it helped me save my brother. He was Tainted at sixteen. I knew everything, and I couldn't do anything.
ISAWA MICHIO: (beat) Everything I know has a limit. I hate that most of all.
### Task — a Tainted child at a frontier temple; the books say "nothing can be done"
[IF haruki in party] HARUKI: "The books said the same about the Darkfever." [END]
(Michio and the party ease the child with Purify Water, Strength of the Crow and Sayo's jade Remedy — not a cure, but
time.)
**Virtue echo — Righteousness:** ISAWA MICHIO: "Knowing isn't the same as doing. I'll stop hiding behind the first."
[UPGRADE *Amaterasu's Fire* → 3 uses; stronger vs. Tainted bosses]
