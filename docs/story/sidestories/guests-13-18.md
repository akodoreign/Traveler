## 13. Kitsuki Momo — Dragon / Kitsuki magistrate
**Skill:** *Perfect Recall* (reveal weakness, 2) · **Banter:** Gendo, Sayo
### SS1 — "The Plum Case" · Dragon Ch1 (Rakusho) · Lv 4+
(See dragon-ch1 S04: Momo joins optionally. Her SS1 is the stolen-plum case she was actually sent to solve.)
KITSUKI MOMO: Someone is stealing plums from the Rakusho temple orchard. One plum a night. Exactly one.
KITSUKI MOMO: I'm a magistrate. They gave me plums. (beat) I'll solve the *plums*.
### Task — a night stakeout in the orchard; the thief is Riichi, taking one plum a night for his sick mother
RIICHI: (caught, frightened) I remember exactly how many plums there are. I only took one each time. So it wouldn't be missed.
KITSUKI MOMO: (kneeling) …It was missed, Riichi. By me. (beat) Let's tell the priest together. I'll pay for the plums.
[GUEST +Kitsuki Momo]
### SS2 — "The Farmer's Daughter" · after Dragon Ch4 · Lv 42+
(Momo's home village: a plum farm in Dragon foothills. Her father, a farmer, bows to the ground before her.)
KITSUKI MOMO: (horrified, pulling him up) Father, *don't*. Please don't bow to me.
KITSUKI MOMO'S FATHER: You're samurai now, Momo-sama.
KITSUKI MOMO: (to the party, quietly) I saved my lord from a snake. So he made me samurai. And now my father bows to me.
### Task — samurai tax collectors are taking the village's seed-plums; Momo must choose a side
> CHOICE
  - "Use your magistrate's authority for them." → (Momo's first case using her rank for her own people; Glory −1, Honor +1)
**Payoff:** KITSUKI MOMO: "I thought becoming samurai meant I'd leave the farm. It means I can protect it."
[UPGRADE *Perfect Recall* → 3 uses; also reveals the enemy's next action]

## 15. Kitsu Reiko — Lion / Kitsu sodan-senzo
**Skill:** *Ancestor's Voice* (buff/debuff, 2) · **Banter:** Shizue, Gendo
### SS1 — "The Rebel Medium" · Dragon Ch1 · Lv 4+
(A spoiled young Kitsu medium with bells in her hair, bored on a diplomatic visit to the isles, and causing trouble.)
KITSU REIKO: The ancestors say *you* should take me sightseeing. (beat) They definitely said that.
### Task — a ghost haunts the isle's pirate shrine; Reiko is the only one who can speak to it — and she does, properly
KITSU REIKO: (to the ghost, gently, all the brattiness gone) …You're not angry. You're lonely. Your ship sank and no one came.
[GUEST +Kitsu Reiko]
### SS2 — "The Faked Voice" · after Lion Ch2 · Lv 25+
KITSU REIKO: (a confession) Once, when I was fifteen, I faked an ancestor's voice. So my friend would be allowed to duel.
KITSU REIKO: He won. He's a hero now. Everyone believes his ancestors approved.
KITSU REIKO: (very quiet) They didn't say anything. I made it up. And now the real ancestors won't talk to me at all.
### Task — at the family shrine, Reiko must confess to her friend, now a famous duellist
> CHOICE
  - "Confess." → THE DUELLIST: "…I knew, Reiko. You were terrible at the old man's voice. I won anyway." (the real ancestors laugh)
**Payoff:** KITSU REIKO: "They're talking again. Mostly they're laughing at me. I'll take it."
[UPGRADE *Ancestor's Voice* → 3 uses]
