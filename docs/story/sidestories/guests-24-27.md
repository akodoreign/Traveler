## 24. Akodo Ijako — Lion / Kitsu-trained sodan-seizo
**Skills:** *Call the Dead* (ancestor strike, 1) · *Mask Rite* (2) · **Banter:** Shizue, Haruki
### SS1 — "Good Morning, Ijako" · Lion Ch1 (joins in S03a) · Lv 4+
(Her SS1: the household servants avoid her — the girl who walks with the dead.)
SERVANT: (whispering) Don't look at her mask. She'll see your death in it.
### Task — a servant's dead child is haunting the kitchen; Ijako must walk the Lands of the Dead to guide her home (a short dream-map)
AKODO IJAKO: (lifting her mask afterwards, to the servant) She's gone where she's meant to be. She wanted you to know the soup was always good.
SERVANT: (weeping, then bowing to her) …Thank you, Ijako-sama. (beat) Good morning.
AKODO IJAKO: (startled; very softly) …Good morning.
### SS2 — "My Mother" · after Lion Ch4 · Lv 42+
AKODO IJAKO: My mother died bearing me. I've walked the Lands of the Dead a hundred times.
AKODO IJAKO: I've never looked for her. (beat) I'm afraid she'll blame me.
### Task — with Haruki (Ancestral Guidance) or alone, Ijako walks to the Lands of the Dead
(The mother: young, gentle, exactly Ijako's face without the mud.)
IJAKO'S MOTHER: Blame you? (laughing) Child, I *chose* you. I knew the risk. I'd choose you again.
IJAKO'S MOTHER: (touching the reed mask) Take that off sometimes. Your face is lovely. It's mine.
**Payoff:** AKODO IJAKO: (her mask off, in the morning sun) "Good morning, everyone."
[UPGRADE *Call the Dead* → 2 uses; summons her mother's spirit (a heal + strike)]

## 25. Hiro — eta undertaker
**Skill:** *No-dachi Sweep* (AoE, 2) · **Banter:** Sayo
### SS1 — "The Rusty Blade" · Ronin Ch1 (joins in S04) · Lv 4+
(His SS1: the no-dachi was his grandfather's; it's rusted; no smith will touch an eta's sword.)
HIRO: No smith sharpens for eta. I grind it on a river stone. It takes a month.
### Task — find a smith who will (the Crab barracks smith, if Natsuko is present; or Kaiu Shino; or Tamotsu buys the work)
CRAB SMITH: (if Natsuko) An eta's sword? (looking at it) …A no-dachi this old, this well-kept? (beat) I'll do it. Don't tell my sensei.
[UPGRADE No-dachi → sharpened (+damage)]
### SS2 — "The Tiger in the Blood" · after Ronin Ch4 · Lv 42+
(The weretiger's claw marks have been reddening since Ronin Ch1.)
HIRO: (one night, at the camp's edge, trembling) Sayo. The tiger's loud tonight.
HIRO: I keep dreaming of the samurai quarter. Of how easy they'd be. How they'd never run fast enough.
HIRO: (looking at his hands) I don't want this. I want to bury people kindly. That's all I ever wanted.
### Task — a cure: the sacred well Tomie Cho's family drank from; drink *clean* water from it to reverse the curse
[BATTLE b_ss_hiro_well_guardian] (a tiger-spirit guarding the well)
(Hiro drinks. The claw marks fade to white scars.)
HIRO: (after a long silence) …It's quiet. The tiger.
SAYO: Good. (grinning) I'd hate to have to Remedy a tiger. I don't have enough ginseng.
**Payoff:** (the finale montage shows Hiro among the eta of Cakon, his clean blade held high)
[UPGRADE *No-dachi Sweep* → 3 uses]
