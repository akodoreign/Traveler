# The Empress of Nothing — the play (Crane Ch4)

A kabuki play in four acts, "written by a survivor of the court of Komatsu", banned for four hundred years.
Kakita Rensei rehearses it in secret in S03 on the old stage in Kyuden Doji's north garden. Lady Sanae watches,
with Daidoji Uji and Kakita Yasurugi.

The play hides the demon bride's weaknesses in plain sight (from the forbidden wing's three lecterns):
- She sees all smooth stone as jade.
- A cut to her face undoes her.
- Call her Empress, and her guard falls for a breath.
- She reads only one heart at a time.

**Roles:**
- CHORUS: the nagauta singers, seated on the stage with the shamisen.
- LORD KOMATSU: of the Doji, played by the Old actor.
- THE BRIDE: later THE EMPRESS OF NOTHING, played by the Court actress.
- THE STEWARD: played by the Court actor.
- THE GUARD: a Kakita, sworn to Komatsu. Rensei's role: every move the arrows call is his.
- COURTIERS: the actors, doubling.

## Act One — The Court of Komatsu

*(Ki. The curtain draws back on a court four hundred years dead: lacquer and gold, Lord Komatsu on his dais, and
beside him, in white, a new bride who never blinks.)*

- CHORUS: In the court of Komatsu the lanterns burn gold, / and a bride comes in white who will never grow old.
- KOMATSU: My lady. The court has waited all spring.
- THE BRIDE: Then let it wait. I am worth the waiting.
- THE STEWARD *(aside)*: The new bride never blinks. The servants have stopped looking at her.
- THE GUARD: My lord - she casts no shadow on the screen.
- KOMATSU: Peace, Kakita. She is my wife.
- CHORUS: And the lanterns burned lower, and nobody said.

*(Mie: the Guard, his hand on his hilt, watching her.)*

## Act Two — The Mirror

*(A bronze mirror on its stand. The Bride gazes into it. The courtiers bring gifts.)*

- CHORUS: A mirror of bronze and a river-washed stone; / she sees only jade, and she sees it alone.
- COURTIER: My lady - jade, from the mountain. For you.
- THE BRIDE: Green as the deep sea. Lay it at my feet.
- THE GUARD *(aside)*: It is a pebble. Grey as ash. She cannot tell.
- THE BRIDE: Another. Bring me another.
- CHORUS: The colours are lost to her. The colours are lost.

*(Mie: the Guard, a grey stone in his open palm.)*

## Act Three — The Blade

*(Night in the palace. The Guard draws on her.)*

- CHORUS: Night, and the palace is still as a held breath.
- THE GUARD: Demon. I have watched you. Show me your face.
- THE BRIDE: Strike my heart, if you dare. But not my face - not my face!
- CHORUS: A cut to her face undoes her. She cannot bear to be marred.
- THE GUARD: Then I know where to strike.
- CHORUS: Steel, and the lamps going out one by one.

*(Mie: the Guard's blade a finger's width from her cheek. She does not breathe.)*

## Act Four — The Title

*(The whole court kneels in white. Two courtiers rise together.)*

- CHORUS: The court kneels down in a field of white.
- COURTIERS *(two voices at once)*: Lady of the Mirror - / Lady of the Blade -
- THE BRIDE: Which of you speaks? I hear - I hear neither.
- THE GUARD: EMPRESS.
- CHORUS: For a breath her guard falls. She wants it more than life.
- THE GUARD: Empress of nothing. Of nothing at all.
- CHORUS: And the court of Komatsu was ashes by spring.

*(The grand mie. The ki close the play.)*
