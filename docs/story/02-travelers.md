# Story Bible — 02: The Eight Travelers (DRAFT v0.1)

Original characters. Each has a **Talent** (unique battle command, like Octopath's), a **Path Action**, and four chapters.
"Source" names the research note a chapter adapts; "orig." means new material. Recommended levels follow Octopath's
curve (Ch1 ≈ 1–10, Ch2 ≈ 20–25, Ch3 ≈ 30–35, Ch4 ≈ 40–45) and will be tuned later.

Cast balance: 4 women (Crab, Lion, Scorpion, Ronin), 4 men (Crane, Dragon, Phoenix, Unicorn); ages 17–29.

## Starting rank & status (user rule, 2026-10-16)
- **Every traveler starts at Insight Rank 1 with low Status/Glory** — fresh from gempukku or a lowly post. The
  exception is the **courtier, Ide Tamotsu**, who starts at **Status 3–4** (user, session 2): an accredited emissary.
  The Ide are personable and loved by everyone, so doors open for him that stay shut for the others. That makes him
  the party's natural "face" at court, even while he is still Insight Rank 1.
- **Static characters stay static:** adventure and canon NPCs keep their names and their rank. The great names
  (Hida Kisada, Akodo Toturi, Bayushi Shoju, the gozoku daimyo of the year 400, clan daimyo, Emerald Magistrates)
  **treat the travelers as the minor samurai they are**: no honorifics from above, audiences are brief and given through
  intermediaries, tasks arrive via karo/sensei/senior magistrates, and nobody explains anything twice.
- **How the stories cope:** the travelers are the *assistants, yoriki, yojimbo, attendants and errand-runners* of the
  important people in each adventure. Several stories turn this into an advantage — nobody watches a Rank 1 samurai,
  so they see what high-Glory investigators miss (Natsuko vets the bride *because* nobody would suspect her; Gendo is
  only a junior yoriki whose senior falls ill; Sayo walks where no samurai would).
- **Progression mirrors Octopath:** Insight Rank is tied to level bands — **Rank 1 = Lv 1–20, Rank 2 = 21–40,
  Rank 3 = 41–50, Rank 4 = 51–60, Rank 5 = 61+** (≈ Ch1 Rank 1, Ch2–3 Rank 2, Ch4 Rank 3, finale Rank 4, post-game
  Rank 5). Glory/Status rises with deeds — later chapters show lords grudgingly learning their names. Per-level gains
  (skills, kata, kiho, spells, school techniques) are specified in DESIGN §4c.

| Traveler | Start | Position at the start |
|---|---|---|

| Kakita Rensei | Rank 1, low Status | Newly graduated Kakita student assigned as yojimbo |

| Shosuro Mitsue | Rank 1, low Status | Newly placed Shosuro actor-agent |
| Ide Tamotsu | Rank 1, **Status 3–4** | Accredited Ide emissary: welcome at most tables, liked by nearly everyone |
| Sayo | Rank 1, no Status | A ronin's daughter — outside the system entirely |

## 1. Hiruma Natsuko — the Crab scout ("I will not fail")
- **Age/look:** 20. Wiry, sun-and-ash weathered, a burn scar over one ear, hair cropped short for the Wall. Carries her
  late father's half-finished map of the road back to Hiruma Castle in an oiled case.
- **Personality:** dry gallows humour, blunt, kind to peasants and nezumi, hopeless at court etiquette, fiercely loyal.
  Believes the Empire forgets the people who die so it can sleep.
- **Wound/motive:** the Hiruma have been homeless for generations; her father died mapping a route back. She wants to
  finish the map — and secretly fears it's a fool's dream that will get more Hiruma killed.
- **Battle:** Hiruma Scout — **evasion tank** (draws and dodges hits, counterattacks; per the 3e Hiruma techniques).
  Weapons: **Yari, Yumi** (+ **Tetsubo** via a Hida secondary school at Rank 2). **Talent: "Razor's Edge"** — an
  evasive guard stance that costs no movement; covers an ally and counterattacks misses. (See `docs/rules/schools.md`.) **Path Action: Sumai** (wrestle NPCs; chance-based).
- **Finale virtue:** **Courage** — "Courage isn't following the path everyone walks. It's walking back into the dark
  every morning because someone has to."

## 2. Kakita Rensei — the Crane duelist
- **Age/look:** 17, months past gempukku. Slender, white-dyed hair tied high, impossibly correct posture, a borrowed fan he fidgets with.
- **Personality:** brilliant, earnest, anxious; tries to be perfect because his father demands it. Funny when he forgets
  to be formal. Afraid he's an over-sharpened sword.
- **Wound/motive:** top student at the Kakita academy, assigned as yojimbo to **Lady Doji Sanae**, "Keeper of the House
  Honour" — the Crane's quiet fixer of embarrassments. He wants to prove a duelist's blade means something beyond
  ceremony.
- **Battle:** Kakita duelist — single-target crits. Weapons: **Katana, Tanto**. **Talent: "Iaijutsu"** — *Focus* (skip a
  turn, build a stack) then *Strike* (huge single hit, guaranteed crit at max stacks). **Path Action: Charm** (lead
  NPCs; chance-based).
- **Finale virtue:** **Courtesy** — "Etiquette can be a mask for knives. It can also be the only thing that lets two
  enemies sit at one table long enough to stop a war."

## 3. Kitsuki Gendo — the Dragon investigator
- **Age/look:** 28. Prematurely grey streak, ink-stained fingers, round spectacles of gaijin glass (scandalous), a battered
  case of evidence tools. A Mirumoto sword he trained with and rarely draws. Spent years as a lowly archive clerk before
  being made a junior yoriki.
- **Personality:** prematurely world-weary, patient, sardonic, kind to the overlooked (eta, servants, simple boys with good
  memories). Talks to himself while thinking.
- **Wound/motive:** as a clerk, his evidence was overruled by high-Glory testimony and an innocent man died. He wants the
  truth to matter — and learns, in the past, how history itself is just testimony.
- **Battle:** Kitsuki/Mirumoto — two-sword multi-hit breaker; Discern reveals weaknesses. Weapons: **Katana, Bo**.
  **Talent: "Deduce"** — reveal an enemy's weaknesses and next action (Cyrus's Study Foe). **Path Action: Discern**
  (learn information; level-gated, honourable).
- **Finale virtue:** **Sincerity (Makoto)** — "Every clan asks its samurai to lie. I have lied too — to protect a truth
  larger than me. Sincerity isn't never lying. It's knowing exactly what the lie costs, and paying it yourself."

## 4. Akodo Shizue — the Lion tactician
- **Age/look:** 18. Tall, square-shouldered, hair in a practical war-tail, a go board always in her travel pack.
- **Personality:** calm, precise, competitive, warm with soldiers, cold with flatterers. Thinks three moves ahead and
  hates that people are not game pieces.
- **Wound/motive:** niece and strategist to minor daimyo **Akodo Hiruto** on the Lion–Phoenix–Crane border. Her cousin
  started a war with the Phoenix in one reckless night. She believes someone outside the clans wants the Lion to bleed.
- **Battle:** Akodo commander — party buffs, turn manipulation, counters. Weapons: **Katana, Yari**. **Talent:
  "Command"** — grant an ally an extra action / move an ally up the turn order. **Path Action: Duel** (challenge NPCs;
  level-gated, honourable).
- **Finale virtue:** **Honor** — "Lords use the word to keep us in line, yes. I've seen my cousin start a war in its
  name. Honour isn't what my lord calls it. It's what I answer for when no one is watching."

## 5. Isawa Haruki — the Phoenix shugenja
- **Age/look:** 20, a junior acolyte. Soft-spoken, long hair tied with a cord of five colours, ink and ash on his sleeves, a nervous habit of
  counting the elements on his fingers.
- **Personality:** gentle, scholarly, painfully conscientious, unexpectedly stubborn. Laughs too late at jokes.
- **Wound/motive:** at eight, his uncontrolled **Void** magic lashed out at a jealous playmate, **Isawa Mieko**, and
  split her soul in two. Her bright half became a gardener-turned-priest; her dark half slept. He has never forgiven
  himself, and has never told anyone.
- **Battle:** Isawa shugenja — elemental caster and the party's main healer. Weapon: **Bo**. **Talent: "Attune"** —
  choose an element for the turn; spells of that element are boosted. **Path Action: Bless** (lead NPCs; level-gated,
  honourable).
- **Finale virtue:** **Righteousness (Gi)** — "Where is justice? Not in the courts, you're right. I split a girl's soul
  when I was eight and no court ever judged me. So I judged myself, and I spent my life setting it right. That is
  righteousness: not waiting for someone else to do what is just."

## 6. Shosuro Mitsue — the Scorpion actress-agent
- **Age/look:** 23, newly placed agent. Many faces; her own is sharp and amused, with a small burn on her left hand she never explains.
  Default mask: a half-mask of red lacquer.
- **Personality:** witty, observant, generous in secret, merciless in public. Loyal to the Clan above herself — which is
  the problem.
- **Wound/motive:** trained by the Shosuro to become other people; she is not sure who is left underneath. Sent by her
  Shosuro sensei to quietly fix problems the Scorpion can't be seen fixing — the Champion does not know her name
  (which is why she's useful).
- **Battle:** Shosuro infiltrator — debuffs, poison, steal. Weapons: **Tanto, Yumi**. **Talent: "Many Faces"** — steal
  from or disguise as an enemy (Therion's Steal + a one-turn "copy enemy skill"). **Path Action: Eavesdrop** (learn
  information; chance-based).
- **Finale virtue:** **Duty** — "Lords never pay for their failures? Mine will. And I'll be there, because duty isn't a
  leash. It's the promise I keep even when I know what it will cost — and I know, better than anyone here."

## 7. Ide Tamotsu — the Unicorn diplomat-trader
- **Age/look:** 29. Round, bearded, bright silk with gaijin embroidery, a laugh that fills inns; a pony named Persimmon
  who hates everyone else.
- **Personality:** jovial, shrewd, sincerely kind; believes trade is how strangers become neighbours. Underestimated
  constantly — and uses it.
- **Wound/motive:** the Ojatara valleys are his trade routes and his friends' homes; the war has turned them into
  refugee camps. He was a pupil of old **Shinjo Miyahana**, as was a peasant boy named **Naohida**.
- **Battle:** Ide emissary — support: buffs, debuff-cleansing, money skills (Tressa-like). Weapons: **Yumi, Yari**.
  **Talent: "Barter Coin"** — spend gold for effects (hire help, bribe an enemy to skip a turn, buy a party buff).
  **Path Action: Barter** (obtain items for money; honourable).
- **Finale virtue:** **Compassion (Jin)** — "Lords sell daughters and starve villages, and call it order. I've stood in
  the refugee camps, lady. Compassion isn't pretending the world is kind. It's the rice you hand over anyway, even when
  there's no profit in it — *especially* then."

## 8. Sayo — the wave-woman (born ronin)
- **Age/look:** 18. Small, quick, river-tanned, patched travelling clothes, a basket of herbs, a plain borrowed-looking
  katana she's good with, a straw hat she hides under.
- **Personality:** cheerful, irreverent, practical; laughs at samurai pomposity and then quietly does their work.
  Doesn't bow quite low enough. Secretly hungry to belong somewhere.
- **Background:** born to a ronin mother on a river barge; never had a clan, a lord or a family name. Learned herbs from
  eta and peasant healers samurai won't touch. Has seen the Empire from underneath.
- **Battle:** wave-woman apothecary — second healer, items, versatility (Alfyn-like). Weapons: **Katana, Yumi**.
  **Talent: "Remedy"** — combine herbs into healing or offensive concoctions (Concoct). **Path Action: Pilfer**
  (obtain items; chance-based; failure costs her Honor in town — she has little to lose).
- **Finale virtue:** answers Nashiko's last claim — "*bushido is a child's dream*" — "Maybe. Nobody ever made me swear
  to it. No lord, no clan. I picked it up anyway. That's what makes it real."
