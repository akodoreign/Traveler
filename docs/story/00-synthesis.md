# Story Bible — 00: Synthesis (DRAFT v0.1 for user review)

Built from the research notes in `docs/research/`. This file is the backbone for the full bible: era, threads,
traveler concepts, chapter sourcing, and the gaps that need original arcs. **Nothing here is final.**

## 1. When: winter 1122 → spring 1123 IC

- **Why:** almost every pre-Coup adventure clusters in "the year before the Scorpion Coup": Fortune's Lost (Shoju
  distracting the courts), The Blood of Family (6 months before), Shadow of a Doubt (Toturi's Winter Court), Mirror,
  Mirror (Kisada's Winter Court), Of Family and Honor R2 (Lion–Crane war under way), Hindsight's present (1120).
- **The clock:** the game spans roughly one year — Winter Court season through the spring — with the **Lion–Crane war**
  escalating in the background and **Bayushi Shoju's coup** looming. The epilogue can end on the eve of the Coup:
  the travelers have saved what they could; history's great storm is about to break.
- ⚠ Exact canon dates (Toturi's accession, Arasou's death, the Coup's month) must be **verified against the
  sourcebooks** before locking the calendar.

## 4. The finale (draft)

- **Lead-in:** each traveler's Chapter 4 ends with a glimpse of the same impossibly beautiful woman with blue eyes.
  Shared clues: she never reacts to scalding sake; she mistakes smooth stone for jade (colour-blind); she reuses the
  same method twice; jade burns only her bare skin.
- **Gathering:** a Shadowlands host besieges a shrine (Of Family and Honor R2's Shrine of Osano-Wo) — a party-wide
  defence battle with event waves.
- **Journey:** Shinomen Mori (the Kumo hermit; signs of the waking Naga); a corrupted glade.
- **The debate:** Nashiko attacks the seven virtues one by one; each clan traveler answers from their own story; the
  ronin answers last ("I was never bound to it — I choose it"). Each good rebuttal strips one of her shields.
- **The battle:** her true form (black-edged throat wound pulsing with Fu Leng's blood; empty glowing sockets); jade,
  crystal and obsidian matter; she tries to flee — this time the travelers cut her off.
- **Death image:** her porcelain mask breaks into motes of light — the stolen souls go free.
- **Epilogue:** the eve of the Coup. Victory, but history's storm is coming.

## 5. Gaps → original arcs needed
- Chapter 4 for **Crab, Crane, Lion, Phoenix, Ronin** (the sources run out or are off-era).
- The **Phoenix** arc needs the most original writing (Howl is late-era canon).
- **Crossover chapters** (Octopath 2 "Crossroads"): Crab+Crane (Of Family and Honor R1), Unicorn+Scorpion
  (Fortune's Lost has both sides), Scorpion+Dragon (destroying the Iron Box at Tamori's Furnace), Lion+Phoenix
  (Blood of Family has both sides).
- Tone pass: several sources are extremely dark (Fortune's Lost, Mirror Mirror, Of Family and Honor). The game should
  imply, not show.

## 5b. Canon 1120–1123 events that can fill the gaps (from *Imperial Histories*)
- **Ronin (Sayo) Ch4 option:** the **Oracle of Fire chooses an eta woman** as successor in 1123 and the Scorpion try to
  stop it. Sayo (who learned medicine from eta) protecting the chosen woman is a perfect "caste is a lie" climax — and
  arguably better than the re-scaled Past Heroes plot. Could combine: the Past Heroes secret in Ch3, the Oracle in Ch4.
- **Phoenix (Haruki) Ch1/Ch3 option:** the **Darkfever** — an eclipse frees an oni in the Isawa Mori, a magical plague
  sweeps the Phoenix lands. Gives Haruki a healer's crisis and an in-era source.
- **Dragon (Gendo):** **Togashi Kokujin** went mad in 1120 — the renegade-ise-zumi theme of Hindsight now has a living
  contemporary echo; Kokujin's tattooed cultists can appear in the Phoenix Silent Court chapter.
- **Crab (Natsuko):** the lost **Hare Clan sword** recovered from the ruins of Shiro Hiruma (1123) — a reason for a
  Hiruma scout to guide someone into her family's lost castle (Ch4 coda or side story).
- **Crane (Rensei) Ch3:** canon has a Lion samurai stoking Crane–Mantis piracy in 1123 → the White Squid pirates'
  secret patron.
- **Scorpion (Mitsue) Ch4:** Shoju's visit to Togashi and the Bloodsword *Ambition* → Mitsue escorts him (or spies on the
  visit) and hears "a bloodline will end."
- **Finale:** the **Naga waking** in Shinomen (1122) is canon — the drag marks are real.
