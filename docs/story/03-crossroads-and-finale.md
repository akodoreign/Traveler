## The finale: "The Empress of Nothing"
Unlocks after all eight Chapter 4s (or a subset + a hidden condition, TBD — Octopath's "true ending" model).

### Act 1 — The Shrine of Osano-Wo
A Shadowlands host marches out of the Shinomen Mori across the Plains of Thunder against the Shrine of Osano-Wo (monks,
peasants, powdered jade stores). **Party-wide defence battle** with event waves (adapted from Of Family and Honor R2):
goblin swarm → hold the breach → burning goblin fanatics → ogre battering ram → save the abbot → close the breach → a
spidery horror. After the battle, a herald with blue eyes: "The Empress congratulates you. She awaits you in Shinomen.
Four days — or this shrine burns."

### Act 2 — Shinomen Mori
- A one-handed nezumi guide (**Stich'Kik**) who begs to be killed when it's over (the party can instead free him; he
  joins the Crab's nezumi friends from Ch1).
- Drag marks of enormous serpents — the Naga are waking.
- A mock seppuku scene carved with "Such is the true nature of your precious bushido."
- **Mamoru the Kumo**: an old hermit by the fire — courtesy test; rudeness spawns a spider fight.
- The corrupted glade.

### Act 3 — The debate
Nashiko (in her loveliest form, on a mat of leathery hide) attacks bushido tenet by tenet. Each answer is given by one
traveler (from their story's lived experience). Each strong answer **breaks one of her eight shields** (seven tenets + Sayo's answer) for the battle. Full
script: `scripts/finale-the-empress-of-nothing.md`.
A weak answer leaves the shield up — the final fight is harder but still winnable.

✅ **Verified** against the L5R 5e core rulebook, ch. 7 "The Clans' Views of Bushidō": Crab—Courage, Crane—Courtesy,
Dragon—Sincerity, Lion—Honor, Phoenix—Righteousness, Scorpion—Duty and Loyalty, Unicorn—Compassion.
**Design twist available:** the same page lists each clan's *least-valued* tenet (Crab—Courtesy, Crane—Courage,
Dragon—Duty, Lion—Compassion, Phoenix—Sincerity, Scorpion—Honor & Righteousness, Unicorn—Courtesy). Nashiko can
first attack each traveler through their clan's *weak* tenet (a mid-debate "doubt" beat), before they answer with
their clan's paramount one.

### Act 4 — The battle
- Phase 1: Nashiko flies and turns invisible; **crystal/thin jade** reveals her (items earned in Crane Ch4, Lion Ch2,
  Unicorn Ch3–4). Her seduced champions shield her (Blood Shouting — damage transfers to them).
- Phase 2: **Body of Damned Time** — an inky starfield; a turn timer ages the party (stat drain).
- Phase 3: true form — a black-edged wound in her throat pulsing with Fu Leng's glowing blood, empty blue-glowing
  sockets. Weak to jade, crystal and obsidian. Exploits from the stories: colour-blind (a smooth stone decoy), phobia of
  disfigurement (a disfiguring strike staggers her), vanity (calling her "Empress" baits her to drop guard for one turn).
- She tries to flee — and finds every path blocked by the allies the travelers made across eight stories (a callback
  montage: Kage's Crab, Toturi's Lion, the Mirumoto army, the Phoenix shugenja, the eta of Cakon…).
- **Death:** her body fades into an inky void; a white jewelled porcelain mask breaks into motes of light — the souls she
  drank go free. Something under the Shadowlands bellows in grief.
