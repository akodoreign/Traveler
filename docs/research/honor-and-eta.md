# Research: Honor and Eta ("What's Eating the Eta?")

- **Source:** `Honor and Eta.pdf` (9 pp). Dave Zenz, **D&D 3e Oriental Adventures** scenario set in Rokugan (Dungeon
  magazine style), for 1st-level PCs. Read in full: session 1.
- **Era:** unspecified. The city **Cakon** isn't a canon Rokugani city — trivially relocatable. Fits 1120.
- **Clans:** Crane noble (Mibu family — not a canon Crane family; treat as a vassal), otherwise caste-focused.
  **Ronin-friendly**: the heroes are "bold and honourable citizens" hired in a market.
- **Theme:** honour vs. caste; duty (gimu) vs. compassion (ninjo); the people the Celestial Order discards.
- **Tone:** light dungeon crawl; very Octopath-compatible.

## Plot
- A mercenary, **Daigoro**, killed Crane noble Mibu Ieko in a jealous brawl over the poet-geisha **O-Sode** at the
  **House of Tranquility**. Judge **Sono Kentanaga** (a rival family) adds a clause: if the execution isn't carried out
  in three days, Daigoro walks free.
- No eta will act as executioner: a man-eating "spirit creature" has been devouring them; they believe it punishes their
  unclean souls, and refuse all polluting work until it's gone.
- Bruised young noble **Mibu Kagemaro** hires the heroes in the market (throws silver to street musicians first) to save
  the eta and restore order — but he must not be seen to be involved. Suggesting a samurai do the execution himself
  disgusts him (that would make you eta).
- Encounters: a disguised Lion samurai and a ronin from Daigoro's band (**Tetsuo**) who ambushes them in an alley; an
  old beggar-monk at the gate who, if saved from a stray dog, gives a riddle ("Under the right arm of the strong is the
  gift…") leading to buried treasure under a cedar between two red stones; the eta village with its all-faiths shrine;
  **Hiro** the huge toothless undertaker with a rusty no-dachi who survived the beast; two **crow-headed tengu**
  duelling on a log bridge over an irrigation canal just to humiliate travellers; six **bakemono** (goblins) with
  baskets of stolen pilgrim loot; the lair.
- Villain: **Tomie Cho**, an old **weretiger** woman, last of a family cursed for drinking from a sacred well, too old to
  hunt rabbits, turned to the eta as easy prey. Waits in tiger form inside her mud hut.
- End: the eta return to work; Hiro performs the execution; Kagemaro owes the heroes **giri**. Hook: Hiro was clawed
  — he might become a weretiger and hunt the upper castes.

## Game adaptation ideas
- **Ronin traveler Chapter 1 candidate** — small, self-contained, low-level, and thematically perfect for a masterless
  samurai who stands between castes. The ronin can do what clan samurai can't (enter the eta village, deal with the
  mercenaries, even consider the execution dilemma).
- **Pilfer** path action fits (stealing back pilgrims' loot from the bakemono baskets); the tengu bridge is a cute
  mini-boss with a knock-off-the-bridge mechanic.
- Boss: weretiger (human → hybrid → tiger phases; fire forces her out of the hut).
- Moral beat: the eta's belief that they deserve the attacks; Kagemaro's genuine but caste-bound gratitude.
- Tie-in: Hiro's lycanthropy as a later-chapter callback.
