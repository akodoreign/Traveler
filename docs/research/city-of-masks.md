# Research: City of Masks

- **Source:** `City of Masks.pdf` (28 pp). Author not named. Read in full: session 1.
- **Era as written:** ~1170 IC (no Emperor, no Emerald Champion, after the Khan's march; Bayushi Paneki is Scorpion
  Champion). The city vanished in **1123** at the Coup. **For our 1120 era, re-date:** the city vanished centuries
  earlier (e.g. during an Iuchiban or Gozoku-era crisis) and returns in 1120. Its out-of-date fashions and a long-dead
  minor clan's booth still work as clues.
- **Primary clan:** **Scorpion** (Soshi shugenja's city). Lion and Unicorn armies poised to fight over it.
- **Theme:** identity, masks, souls; paranoia ("make your players a little uncomfortable"); a city that doesn't
  know it is dead.
- **Tone:** eerie; a few gory scenes (stitched-on faces, a torn-apart body) that would need softening.

## Premise (as written)
A large Scorpion trade city on the plains north of Shiro no Soshi, a day's ride from **Seikitsu Pass**, appears overnight
— a city no map shows. Long ago its head shugenja **Soshi Giseiki** discovered a cult of the **Lying Darkness**
(the Nothing) eating the citizens' souls and names. He bound every citizen's soul into a **mask**, leaving bodies as
husks, then used a second ritual (powered by shugenja lives) to hide the whole city in the **Realm of Dreams**. A recent
cataclysm in the dream realm shattered the wards and dropped the city back into the world. The cultists, cut off from
the Darkness, have gone mad and murderous.
- **Appendix B alternative (era-free!):** there was never a Lying Darkness; **the masks are a sentient parasite**
  (gaki-like) that feeds on wearers' souls. The ritual was to stop the masks spreading to the Empire. → **Use this.**

## Structure
1. **Rumours** on the road (a great rumour table: built in a night; zombies; faceless Goju; the Emperor's heir hidden
   there; "being there is like watching a play — they talk to each other, not to you").
2. Rest stop: a paranoid Lion fortress (**Shiranai Toshi**, Darkness City) *or* a too-hospitable Unicorn hot-spring
   village (**Hisatu Kensu**) where nobody will speak of the city. Everyone has **mask dreams** (faceless monk; a smith
   forging your face; puppets with your faces worn smooth by sand).
3. A dying Lion scout gives one hero a red-and-black **mask "meant for you"** (it holds Giseiki's soul) and travel
   papers bearing a long-dead lord's chop.
4. Optional: audience with the Scorpion Champion and a truth-sensing Soshi shugenja who hire the heroes.
5. **The city:** everyone masked, dressed decades out of date, nobody drinks the sake they order; **the unmasked are
   ignored** ("I have nothing to say to the faceless") → the heroes must **get masks** (from the blacksmith, the
   Badger carver, a tattooist, a silk seller) — and the masks slowly absorb their Void/soul.
6. Investigation of missing magistrate teams across three districts (Hospitality, Merchant, Temple); ~20 named,
   masked NPCs, each identified *by their mask*.
7. Set pieces: a scout who is in the wrong body; a possessed Lion who kills two Unicorns and is shot mid-seppuku; a
   man torn apart in the market while masked citizens step through the gore without noticing; monk cultists with
   **stitched-on faces** trying to rip off the heroes' masks.
8. **The shadow hunters** (black-clad survivors led by governor **Shosuro Osuko**) reveal the history. Final: storm the
   Temple District and stop the abbot **Munashii** praying to a Shadow Dragon — or a mass battle against the whole city
   — while Giseiki (in a hero's body or Osuko's) performs the counter-ritual.

## The mask rules (great mechanics source)
- Empty masks absorb the wearer's Void a point at a time each hour in the city; when full, removing the mask leaves the
  body "dead". Put a mask on any husk to animate it with that soul.
- A mask that already holds a soul makes two souls contest the body.
- Cultist masks hold a fragment of Nothing and grant a little of its power.
- **Citizens react to each other by mask colour (clan) and material (element)**: wood fears metal, earth fears wood,
  water(shell) fears earth, fire fears water, metal fears fire; porcelain/glass (air) fears stone; stone fears silk.
  The mask is the only consistent "person", since bodies can be swapped.

## Key NPCs
Soshi Giseiki (soul in the mask), Shosuro Osuko (governor, owner of the Sweet Cup sake house, Crimson Blossom inn,
Sweet Plate; white porcelain mask; secretly leads the hunters), Kaiu Naname (smith, forged the original masks), Yasuki
Kasamashi (con man, Kolat, fake-jade trinkets, real jade mask), Munashii (retired Akodo abbot of the Monastery of the
Silent Self — the villain), Ichiro Itami (Badger carver, cultist), Agasha Kusuri (poisoner, cultist), Togashi Sumi
(tattooed-face riddler: "You seek Master Giseiki… on the face of the one who wears him"), Kakita Yuko (12-year-old
puppeteer), Moto Zhang (drunk, "The P easant Dream" sign), Yogo Meyako (geisha house), Hida Dorin (drinking-and-punching
contest), Daidoji Hagane vs. Yasuki Kasamashi (feuding stalls), Isawa Kasiko (surviving magistrate), Miya Hatomu (old
cartographer who was disgraced for mapping a city that "didn't exist").

## Game adaptation ideas
- **Scorpion traveler chapter (Ch3?)** — a "dream city" dungeon-town. Huge Octopath fit: a town where NPCs **ignore you
  until you wear a mask** (an equipment-driven dialogue gate), with mask *materials* as a social puzzle.
- **Mask equipment mechanic:** while in the city, equipped masks slowly drain max SP/Void; the clock pushes the
  chapter forward.
- Boss ideas: stitched-face monks (target "mask" to Break), the possessed Doji magistrate (iaijutsu duelist), Munashii
  (only crystal hurts him fully — Crystal as weakness), mass-battle finale as a defence-wave fight.
- The cartographer's vindication and the dying-scout's gift are strong emotional beats.
- Tie to Nashiko? Probably keep separate — this is a standalone eerie chapter. Could tie to the **Phoenix** traveler
  (Realm of Dreams cataclysm) or the **Dragon** traveler (Togashi Sumi).
