# Research: The Edge of Treachery

- **Source:** `The Edge of Treachery.pdf` (9 pp). Scott Gearin. **A campaign outline + player's guide**, not a full
  adventure — only Chapter 1 has real detail; Chapters 2–6 are one-line teasers. Read in full: session 1.
- **Era:** reign of **Hantei XXIII** (post-Iuchiban, pre-Kaiu Wall; **no Unicorn, no Kitsuki, no Wasp**). Does *not*
  fit 1120 IC as written → re-date and swap the named lords if used.
- **Primary clan:** **Crane** (Daidoji). Fealty campaign: all heroes serve **Daidoji Toshiya** (heir to Daidoji Kazuo).
- **Focus:** court intrigue + mass battle; naval/pirate opener.

## Chapter 1 — The First Stones (the usable part)
The **White Squid pirates** (wako) seized Toshiya's cargo, including a gift for the Emperor. Toshiya sends a new
gift: the pirate captain's head.
- The pirates are **four brothers** with near-identical ships, which creates the legend of one ship striking places
  hundreds of ri apart on the same day:
  - **Ryohei the Eldest** — Mantis bushi, tactician, nunchaku.
  - **Katsuo the White Brother** — **albino maho-tsukai** who stays at the **White Squid Shrine** on the main cache
    island and makes fake **nemuranai** (charms) for the crews.
  - **Yosuke the Bold** — ronin, natural leader.
  - **Chihaya the Quick** — ronin, perfect balance.
- Rival clock: **Otomo Shinken**, famous Imperial magistrate, racing to the pirates' base; if she wins, only the
  Emperor benefits. Ally with a price: **Yasuki Tessai**, a southern Crab lord who lost money to the wako.
- Passages: Toshiya's command & choosing a ship captain → search north (the magistrate; the **City of Broken
  Flowers**, a ghost city destroyed centuries ago by a giant oni) / search south (Tessai; wreck survivors) → battle with
  the middle brother → **Temples of the Sun** (Asahina) to research the charms → coast at midnight (a wako attack /
  chase) → **Gathering Storms** (sailors swear they saw the **Orochi**) → **Island of the Heavenly Sheen** (eldest
  brother + the spirit of the White Squid) → **Battle at the Pirate Shrine** (the Albino) → **The Head of My Enemy**.
- Risk: a breach in Asahina charm-craft secrets would upset the balance of power.

## Useful setting notes
- Player-PC idea: **Muso Kaigen**, hidden grandson of **Asahina Yajinden** (Iuchiban's artificer — cf. Blood of Family
  handout) haunted by bloody urges.
- House rules worth borrowing: "every single skill can save your life"; **low skills** corrode Honor unless taught by
  your school for your lord's service; Stealth as a normal war skill; **Position** (rank) vs. **Glory** (fame).
- Locations: Chiji Province (Sakaiyo Castle & town), Umi Province (Shinohata village), City of Broken Flowers.

## Game adaptation ideas
- **Crane traveler Chapter 3/4 candidate**: a sea arc — four pirate brothers as four boss fights across islands
  (Octopath-style "multiple bosses who look alike"), ending at the White Squid Shrine with the albino maho-tsukai.
- Fake-charm nemuranai → an item mechanic (pirate crews get buffs from charms; Discern/steal them to remove buffs).
- Orochi sighting as a hidden superboss.
- Asahina Yajinden's bloodline → links to Iuchiban/Bloodspeaker lore (Blood of Family) for the Phoenix/Crane threads.
