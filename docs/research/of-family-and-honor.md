# Research: Of Family and Honor (Rounds One & Two)

- **Source:** `Of Family and Honor.pdf` (77 pp). Robert Hobart, RPGA tournament, 2000. Read in full (narrative
  sections; stat blocks and pregen sheets skimmed): session 1.
- **Era:** Round One = 7 years before the "standard" L5R start (c. 1116 IC); Round Two = 10 years later,
  "24th year of the reign of Hantei the 38th", during the Lion–Crane war (≈ the Coup year). Young Daidoji Uji,
  young Hida Yakamo, Asahina Tamako.
- **Primary clans:** **Crab & Crane** (Round One border crisis); Round Two in **Scorpion** lands (Ryoko Owari, Plains
  of Thunder, Shinomen Mori).
- **Theme:** divided loyalty — Empire vs. family. "Against Fu Leng, sometimes [dying with a free soul] is the only
  victory possible."
- **Villain:** **Doji Nashiko** again (see `mirror-mirror.md`). Two adventures by different authors use her.

## Round One — the Crab–Crane border
**Nashiko's plot:** she shuttles across the border wearing two faces — **Doji Miyako** (white-haired Crane courtier
at Garden Under Shadow City) and **Kuni Miyubi** (black-ponytailed Kuni apprentice at Yasuki Yashiki). She seduces
generals on both sides, plants maho masks to justify a Crab raid on an Asahina estate, then provokes a Crane reprisal
raid on Clear Water Village (Yasuki fishing fleet). Each side denies its own raid. Six Emerald Magistrates are sent.

**Beats:**
1. Arrive at **Garden Under Shadow City** (Daidoji port, tidal land-bridge to Crab lands). Formal dinner, lantern
   garden. Clues: generals besotted with Miyako; Kakita Yumi jealous of her; Lion emissary Ikoma Tadaka catches her
   lie ("Doji Moteuchi" died on the *northern* border) → she fakes an assault, Tadaka is cut down in an instant duel.
2. Ride to **Yasuki Yashiki**. Crab dinner (scalding sake: Miyubi doesn't flinch — **her tell**). Two Hida generals
   wrestle (sumai) over a remark; the loser, shamed, confesses the Asahina raid. Kuni Akuja shows real maho relics
   "found" by Miyubi.
3. **Border conference.** Get Hida Yakamo and Daidoji Uji to talk alone. At night Nashiko visits three Crane
   generals' tents and one Crab's, triggering both armies to mobilise for a "pre-emptive" strike. Heroes must stop
   it — confront leaders, duel the dishonoured, or wake Uji and Yakamo.
4. **Aftermath:** confessions, seppuku; Hida Chuto instead goes into the Shadowlands "looking for Hida".
5. **Final confrontation:** Miyako's chamber; six floating portals show each hero's own child. "Serve Fu Leng and
   they live." Canon assumption for Round Two: the parents **surrender**.

## Round Two — the children, ten years later
- Six heirs (Hiruma Shinji, Matsu Hiro, Otaku Ameiko, Asako Kimi, "Ikoma Asuka"/Shosuro Miaka, Togashi Hametsu)
  meet at the **Inn of the Golden Swallow, Ryoko Owari**, then defend the **Shrine of Osano-Wo** on the **Plains of
  Thunder** from a Shadowlands army (battle-event chain: goblin swarm, stand in the breach, burning goblin fanatics,
  ogre battering ram, save the abbot, close the breach, spidery horror).
- The enemy commanders are their **fallen parents**, now akutsukai (Matsu Ayame with a saw-edged sword, red eyes;
  Otomo Kaigen with blue eyes on an onikage; hunchbacked Shosuro Gamaro; skeletal Asako Igemi; bloated fork-tongued
  Ide…; winged Hiruma Tadoshi).
- Guide: **Stich'Kik**, a one-handed, tortured nezumi slave ("he good-good, yes"). Asks to be killed afterwards.
- **Shinomen Mori:** Naga drag-marks (the Naga are waking); a mock-seppuku scene carved with "Such is the true nature
  of your precious bushido"; **Mamoru the Kumo** (shape-changing spider-hermit — be polite and he leaves you alone).
- The glade: Nashiko's **debate against bushido**, then the parents fight; Nashiko flees ("Your Emperor will die").

## ★ Nashiko's attack on the seven virtues (the finale backbone)
She attacks bushido tenet by tenet. Text paraphrased for our use:

| Tenet | Her attack |
|---|---|
| **Honesty** | Every clan expects its samurai to lie for their lords. |
| **Courage** | Following the path everyone follows is cowardice, not courage. |
| **Compassion** | Lords starve peasants over the levy and sell daughters to geisha houses. |
| **Courtesy** | Etiquette is a mask for knives — the Scorpion are the most courteous of all. |
| **Honor** | A word lords use to keep you in line; lying for a guilty superior. |
| **Sincerity** | Merely the ability to lie well. |
| **Duty** | Lords never pay for *their* failures. |

**Seven tenets, seven Great Clans.** In the game's finale each clan traveler can answer one attack from their own
story's lived experience, and the **ronin** — outside the code — answers her last claim ("bushido is a child's
dream") by *choosing* it freely. Mechanically: a debate battle where each correct rebuttal strips one of her
"Resolve" (Suki) shields before the fight. (Canonical clan↔virtue pairings should be checked against the
sourcebooks before locking this in.)

## Nashiko — additional facts from this adventure
- Invulnerable except to **crystal, jade, obsidian**, or magic. Invisible but **visible through crystal or thin jade**.
- **Soul Drinking:** eaten souls cannot reincarnate while she lives; their faces appear on her innards.
- **Blood Shouting:** wounds transfer to anyone who has tasted her saliva (her seduced generals).
- **Body of Damned Time:** becomes an inky starfield; onlookers see their own death, hair turns white, they age.
- True form: open black-edged wound in her throat pulsing with Fu Leng's glowing blood; blue-glowing empty sockets.
- Vanity makes her **reuse the same method on both sides** — the clue that cracks Round One.
- Bargains "more or less honestly" — she wants two generations of servants.
- Never risks her own life; flees when her minions fall.

## Game adaptation ideas
- **Crane traveler and Crab traveler crossover chapter** ("Crossroads" in Octopath 2 terms): the two travelers
  arrive on opposite sides of the border and must each unmask one of Nashiko's two faces. Path actions: Crane
  *Charm* at the Daidoji dinner; Crab *Sumai* at the Yasuki dinner (beat the general → confession).
- The scalding-sake tell and the "same method twice" deduction → a Discern/Eavesdrop clue minigame.
- **Final chapter** structure inspired by Round Two: a Shadowlands army at a shrine (party-wide defence battle
  with event waves), journey through Shinomen Mori (Kumo hermit, Naga signs), the corrupted glade, the virtue
  debate, then Nashiko's true form.
- **Avoid** the canonical "parents become akutsukai" arc for player characters (it hijacks travelers' agency);
  but a traveler's mentor or relative falling to Nashiko is a strong personal stake (e.g. the ronin's former lord).
- Stich'Kik → a sympathetic nezumi guide NPC (maybe the same nezumi tribe as Tamago Maho's).
