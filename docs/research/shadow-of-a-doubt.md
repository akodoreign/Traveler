# Research: Shadow of a Doubt

- **Source:** `Shadow Of A Doubt.pdf` (49 pp). Keith J. Weepie & Robert Hobart, RPGA classic tournament, 1999.
  Narrative and event chain read in full; NPC/pregen sheets (14 NPCs, 6 pregens) skimmed: session 1.
- **Era:** pre-Coup. **Akodo Toturi** is Lion Champion and hosts Winter Court at **Shiro Akodo**. Fits 1120 IC.
- **Primary clan:** **Lion** (Akodo). Big Crab (Kaiu) presence; one Crane spy; one Moto; Dragon/Phoenix pregens.
- **Theme:** duty vs. love; a good man's slow fall; testimony vs. truth (a Lion advisor's word outweighs a
  "despised Crane's").
- **Tone:** comparatively gentle — no gore until the very end. **Closest in tone to Octopath already.**

## Premise
Akodo **Kafu** (Toturi's most trusted advisor) and Akodo **Nishi** (Toturi's favoured cousin, a budding strategist)
loved each other, but Toturi betrothed Nishi to **Kaiu Yutaka** (Kaiu Battle Master) to seal a Lion–Crab treaty.
Both accept duty and part. A cowardly escaped oni, **Oni no Ianwa**, which feeds on fear and despair, starts visiting
Kafu at night and bullies him with its fear aura into ever-worse acts, draining his honour and Tainting him.

## The eight visitations (escalation clock)
1. A scarf from a friend. 2. A flower from the castle garden. 3. The painting & plaque of **"Duty"** from the
Central Dojo (he blames Crane guest **Daidoji Atsuko**). 4. The Crane **banner of Sorrow's Path** (Kanashimi no
Komichi), a great Lion trophy. 5. His **ancestral katana** — point of no return. 6. His **love letters with Nishi**
— his will collapses; he becomes a willing puppet. 7. **Toturi's seal and mon**, unasked; frames Atsuko, who is
arrested. 8. Final: either the heroes confront the oni, or it orders Kafu to **assassinate Nishi at the wedding**.

## The Oni no Ianwa fight
Floating slug larger than a horse, ring maw of teeth, dripping acid. **No physical attacks**: fear aura (Fear 6)
makes heroes attack each other; resisting costs Void each round. **Coward**: one solid hit with jade/crystal/nemuranai
and it flees ("I will return and devour your souls" — a lie). Kafu can be *talked* into joining the fight.
- Kafu redeemed → seppuku at dawn: "The sky is quite clear today." Nishi's soft sobs.
- Not redeemed → hanged, name struck from records: "There never was an Akodo Kafu."
- Ignored → Benjiro dies; Kafu kills Nishi at the altar; Toturi beheads him; "Thy will be done."

## Shiro Akodo layout
Central Dojo (bushido paintings in sequence like a scroll; Sorrow's Path battle mural), South Dojo (Kafu's), North
Dojo (Nishi's; painting "Honor"), Central Garden (winter flowers), Hall of Honors (ancestor portraits; dinners),
Hall of Akodo (trophy room: mostly Crane, then Scorpion), Kitchen, Library (Lion war histories: Akodo One-Eye,
Matsu Joruri at Sorrow's Path, Battle of Kenson Gakka "Humility's Lesson"), Morning Meditation Chamber, Pantry,
North/South baths, guest rooms; upper floor (restricted): **Chamber of Combat and Tactics** — contingency war
plans against every clan.

## Game adaptation ideas
- **Lion traveler's chapter (Ch2 or Ch3).** Perfect Octopath chapter shape: a town (castle) with a calendar of
  festivals; each festival is a **minigame** (painting = choose-the-theme puzzle; bokken bracket = series of 1v1
  duel battles; history night = dialogue choice; dance = timing minigame); prizes are jade gear that matter in the
  boss fight. The **Duel** path action (Lion) is the bokken tournament.
- Escalation clock: show a missing item after each event (environmental storytelling).
- Boss gimmick: **Ianwa** has no attacks of its own — it **charms party members** each turn; only jade breaks its
  guard (Jade weakness = its Suki); flees on first Break. Then a talk-down of Kafu (dialogue) decides the epilogue.
- Lion traveler hook options: Nishi's friend/sworn sister, or Kafu's student, or the guard captain blamed for the thefts.
- Moto Benjiro: possible recurring NPC who connects to the Unicorn traveler (the Moto curse).
- Daidoji Atsuko: recurring Crane spy → crossover with the Crane traveler.
- Link to the finale: the oni could be one of Nashiko's errand-demons; the stolen "Duty" plaque turns up in her lair.
