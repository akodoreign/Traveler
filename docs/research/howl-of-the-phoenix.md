# Research: Howl of the Phoenix

- **Source:** `Howl of the Phoenix.pdf` (24 pp). Marcin Kosiedowski ("Bayushi Todori"), Polish fan contest entry for
  the *Race for the Throne* story arc; English is rough. Read in full: session 1.
- **Era:** **post-Four Winds, after the Khan's march** (~1167–1168 IC): empty throne, Elemental Council occupying
  the capital, Mantis as a Great Clan, Sezaru ("the Wolf") and Kaneka (the Shogun) dead. **Heavily tied to late canon**
  — the weakest fit for our 1120 era. Needs a near-total rewrite; keep its structure and ideas, drop the canon cast.
- **Primary clan:** **Phoenix** (Asako, Isawa, Shiba). Every Great Clan + Mantis + Dragonfly attend.
- **Themes:** undying love; madness and a "dark side" of the Phoenix; loyalty and remembrance; peace has a price.

## Structure
1. **Shadow in the Mountains** — Asako **Keiki** (monk) invites every clan to a "silent court" disguised as the
   funeral of Isawa Sezaru and his wife Angai. Each clan's lord sends envoys with a private task (nice design: every
   clan gets a secret agenda). Guests gather at a village below **Zumiki-mihari** (an old Otomo watchtower) — three inns
   (White Songbird, Yellow Lantern, Hidden Poem geisha house). A drunk Matsu tests the guests' manners. Two Imperial
   magistrates accuse the Phoenix of bandits on the roads; Isawa may *erase the memory* of the night by ritual. Next
   day a mob of tattooed peasant cultists (followers of the false-monk **Kokujin**) led by bloodspeaker
   **Hioninmaru** ambush the caravan in the snowy pass; uninvited Unicorn and Mantis envoys ride to the rescue.
2. **Silent Court** at **Honored Treaty City** (Mamoru Kyotei Toshi; built to honour Lion–Phoenix peace). A week of
   side-plots per clan: a Crab–Shiba dojo friendship; a Crane yojimbo with Kumo blood stealing a spell scroll; a Lion
   historian finds a scroll about **"the Consumer"** (an undying dark side of the Phoenix reborn in great shugenja)
   and is murdered for it; a drunk Shiba commander to be sobered up; a Utaku girl's blood-feud duel to stop; Mantis
   trade stall burned (arson investigation); a secret mountain **torii shrine to a "traitor" Phoenix Champion** that a
   mob wants to burn — the heroes must defend it with words.
3. **Ceremony of Sadness** — funeral procession; the Shogun's daisho laid under a waterfall; lanterns on the lake;
   a Water Dragon visits unseen; the Dragonfly gift of **Sezaru's book against bloodspeakers** to be copied for every
   clan → a quarrel; a Unicorn strangles a Crane diplomat who betrayed him; an Akodo–Shinjo love match defuses it;
   final prayer at a red sea-cliff torii where the dead lovers' presence is felt.
4. **Howl of the Phoenix** — at the torii, **Isawa Sayuri** (second ceremony master; her dark half has taken her) fights
   the heroes; the Wolf's purified remnant (inside gentle priest Isawa Hiroshi) saves them, then asks "Who am I?".
   Endings depend on how the heroes answer. Epilogue: a white and a black wolf howl by the torii.

## The twist (worth keeping, era-free)
- "The Consumer" is a **lie** written by Sayuri's good half as a warning against her own dark half (split from her
  when a young prodigy's Void magic shattered her mind). Her dark half remembers nothing of the warning.
- A purified "monster" can be reborn as an innocent — the question "what is my role?" is the climax, not the fight.

## Key NPCs (canon names to replace)
Asako Keiki (idealist monk host), Isawa Sayuri (the villain, sweet then cold), Isawa Hiroshi (troubled young priest),
Matsu Hisako (fakes drunken rage to test guests), Ikoma Tadahisa (historian, murdered), Shiba Takeishi & Haruo
(brothers), Bayushi Tomo vs. Utaku Saya (feud from the Coup era), Doji Senzo (double-dealing diplomat), Shinjo
Aritomo, Akodo Isoshi (forbidden love), Tonbo Kageru/Miyato (Dragonfly), Hioninmaru (cowardly bloodspeaker).

## Game adaptation ideas
- **Phoenix traveler chapter (Ch3 or Ch4)** rebuilt for 1120 IC: a Phoenix-hosted inter-clan "silent court" (e.g. a
  memorial for a fallen Elemental Master or for the victims of an Iuchiban-cult outbreak). **Natural crossover hub:**
  every traveler's clan sends someone — an Octopath-2 style "crossroads" chapter where all eight can meet.
- Per-clan secret agendas → optional side stories in town.
- Split-soul shugenja villain = Phoenix traveler's mirror (Phoenix traveler could be the one whose Void power
  shattered someone as a child → guilt-driven arc).
- Torii-defence debate: a non-combat "persuade the mob" scene using each party member's clan argument (the same
  mechanic as the finale's virtue debate — good foreshadowing).
- Snowbound pass ambush by tattooed cultists → links to the Dragon traveler (false ise zumi tattoos, cf. Hindsight's
  rogue Togashi).
- Memory-erasure ritual → a chapter that replays a night with a missing memory.
