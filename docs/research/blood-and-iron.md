# Research: Blood and Iron

- **Source:** `Blood and Iron.pdf` (30 pp). Dave Laderoute, L5R 3rd edition. Opening (court at Kyuden Bayushi,
  mission, road south) and the full climax/aftermath read in detail; the long middle (Kudo town index and NPC
  interviews) skimmed via search: session 1. **Middle section still worth a full read later** for town NPCs.
- **Era as written:** late (~1168 IC): no Emperor, no Emerald Champion, after the Unicorn attack on Toshi Ranbo;
  Bayushi Paneki Champion; Kokujin the Mad Prophet abroad; Scorpion famine from Bishamon's blight. **Re-date** for 1120.
- **Primary clan:** **Scorpion** (Paneki's covert mission) + **Mantis** (jurisdiction over unaligned lands) + minor
  clans **Kitsune (Fox)** and **Suzume (Sparrow)**; Tortoise representative as a red herring.
- **Theme:** "We walk in the darkness to protect the Empire from a darkness greater still." Disparate clans working
  together; a mundane-looking political feud with a supernatural root.

## Premise
**Kudo**, a prosperous farm town on the east bank of the **Kawa no Kin (River of Gold)**, near the **Shinomen Mori**,
in an unaligned "no man's land" (once Hare Clan land) is jointly and uneasily governed by the Kitsune and Suzume,
nominally overseen by a Mantis magistrate. It's being considered as the seat of a new **Minor Clans Court**;
violence escalates, the Suzume governor survives an "assassination attempt", and the Mantis magistrate does nothing.
The Scorpion Champion, short of agents, has the heroes made temporary **yoriki** of a Mantis chief magistrate and
sends a mixed-clan team to investigate.

## Structure
1. **Kyuden Bayushi** — a memorial service; eavesdropping minigame in the garden and at dinner (numbered rumours that
   matter: a **Miya caravan of artifacts from a ruined capital**; bandits and an unknown band fighting them on the
   River of Gold; two courtiers sharing **blood nightmares** on the two nights the caravan stayed). Secret meeting in a
   willow-grove teahouse on a pond peninsula. Castle: beautiful façade hiding a fortress of secret passages; a
   legendary hedge-maze.
2. **The road south** — rice blight; **Traitor's Grove** (Hyashi no Uragirimono), where Scorpion traitors are
   punished; murders in the border town of Shutai; Ronin Plains.
3. **Kudo** — investigation: the Kitsune governor is "changed" (he's dead and replaced by an **Oni no Kommei**), the
   Suzume assassination was probably invented, the Mantis magistrate and his staff have a virulent "pneumonia",
   townsfolk from one district quietly "flee". The caravan was robbed in town; all items found dumped by the river
   except **a small iron box**.
4. **The nudge** — the sick vanish overnight; something blotted out the moon flying west.
5. **Across the river** — a drifting **barge full of bloodless corpses** (the magistrate, the real Kitsune governor);
   animals fleeing the woods; the woods turning into a Shadowlands marsh; a **heaving heart-like clearing with a pit of
   blood**; the Kitsune aide **Kitsune Hana**, possessed by a **Greater Kansen**, holding the **Iron Box**, summoning
   the **Oracle of Blood**.

## The Iron Box of Iuchiban
- For centuries held **Iuchiban's heart**; discarded in the ruins, then collected as a museum "artifact".
- Holder can cast any maho once a day, easier, with **no blood cost**; also acts as a **maho sensei** (corrupts its
  owner). Does not register as Tainted. Indestructible except in **Tamori's Furnace** (a volcano in Dragon lands).
- Its call woke a Greater Kansen (corrupted kami; invisible yellow-green cloud; only crystal/magic hurts it).
- Ritual: separate the Kansen from the box to stop it; after 10 rounds each round risks completion.
- **Oracle of Blood**: a living pool of blood in human shape (drowns victims; flows through cracks; jade bypasses its
  carapace) — Iuchiban's link to the mortal world.
- Reward: a **Scorpion "Favor"** — a blood-red jasper scorpion pendant, near-impossible to forge, redeemable once.

## Game adaptation ideas
- **Scorpion traveler Chapter 1 or 2.** Excellent Octopath shape: court opening with **Eavesdrop** path action
  (literally the adventure's opening minigame), a road chapter, an investigation town with many suspects, a river
  crossing, and a horror boss in a living clearing.
- **Thread to the big picture:** combine with *The Blood of Family* (O-Tamai, "beloved of Iuchiban"), *Edge of
  Treachery* (Yajinden's grandson), and *Fortune's Lost* (Shuten Doji, Iuchiban-era lore) → a **Bloodspeaker
  conspiracy** subplot that can run under Nashiko's main plot, or be her instrument. For 1120: Iuchiban is entombed;
  his relics stirring is the omen.
- **Iron Box as a key quest item** whose destruction (at Tamori's Furnace — Dragon lands) could be a crossover
  chapter between the Scorpion and Dragon travelers.
- Boss: Kansen-possessed Hana — must "Break" her grip on the box (a Suki gimmick: breaking her shields drops the
  box and stops a turn counter). Oracle of Blood: jade weakness, drowning grapple.
- Minor clans (Kitsune/Suzume/Tortoise) give the Scorpion chapter a fresh cast.
- The jasper "Favor" → an Octopath-style story item / one-use path action unlock.

## Addendum (2026-10-31): the middle section, read in full
- **Kyuden Bayushi:** the memorial is for Imperial Chancellor Bayushi Kaukatsu, before the Shrine of Fukurokujin,
  presided over by Seppun Kiharu; everyone wears some white. Garden rumours are overheard by a "courtly whisper"
  roll; the Soshi pair *want* to be overheard (practising gossip). Paneki's aide **Moshi Yumako** prepares the yoriki
  papers; the Mantis chief magistrate is **Tsuruchi Okame**. Yoriki powers: one month, five miles around Kudo; enter,
  question, arrest (no torture). (Our version: Norie relays all of this; the static names stay off-screen.)
- **Traitor's Grove:** hung helmets, masks, armour and bones; anything with "eyes" faces **south** (a divination
  hint). Taking from the grove brings a curse (Unlucky) until the birthday of **Bayushi Tesaguri**, the first
  Scorpion slain there; Jurojin's shrine lifts it.
- **Shutai:** "a wretched hive" on the flood-plain; the Scorpion keep it shabby on purpose (cheap labour, scapegoats).
  **Shosuro Noburo** (Rank 2 shinobi) shadows newcomers; he links the "ghastly murders" 14 days ago to the caravan's
  stay. The murder room is in **Megu's** sake-house-cum-brothel: a client slit a woman's throat, drew a blood
  pattern (a half-finished maho summoning) and died beside it unmarked. Megu wants 2 bu for the room, 5 to talk.
- **West-bank option:** a pyre of 14 bandits under a white-spider banner ("So end all bandits...") with two
  bloodless, throat-slit bodies added later; a cedar clearing with a *completed* summoning (where the Kommei came
  through). The Spider Clan is post-1150: **not used** in 1122.
- **Kudo (Town Map index names):** Dreaming Carp (mid inn), House of Radiant Fog (high), Suzume Hostel (pay with a
  chore), Place of Dancing Coins (river-men's sake house by the docks; the quarantine shed). The Suzume District is
  plain and swept (samurai do chores: "noble poverty"); the Kitsune District is a riot of trees and bright houses.
  The **Kitsune Hall** has columns that sprout leafy branches (green light). The Mantis Magistrate's House flies a
  **yellow quarantine banner**.
- **People:**
  - **Suzume Soseki** (governor, ~50, old soldier) with his aide **Suzume Rika**, who feeds him lines on notes. The
    "assassin" was the Kommei in a peasant's shape: a yellow-green cloud, fear, a day of nightmares.
  - **Kitsune Hyobe** (governor, big, booming laugh) is dead: the **Kommei no Oni** wears him, hood up because its
    back shows the last face it took. It eats raw fish at night (our addition).
  - **Kitsune Hana** (older shugenja, green robes with gold leaves) leaves the hall at once ("she'll be back").
  - **Kasuga Yodo** (Tortoise courtier, go-between) privately warns that Hyobe flipped from backing a Minor Clans
    Court to threatening war within a week.
  - **Yoritomo Rui** (young Mantis yoriki, kama, bluster) runs the magistracy alone: Meiji and three seniors have a
    "pneumonia" (Choking Death); her wall map of flags shows the flight is from the Suzume District by the river.
- **Street flare-ups:** drunk Suzume youths beating a Kitsune (intervene or lose Honor); two old women feuding
  (comedy); a frightened Kitsune boy shooting an arrow at the party; armed Kitsune vs Suzume who turn on outsiders.
- **The stolen caravan items:** a bloody Crane banner from their Otosan Uchi embassy, a broken katana from a Lion's
  skeletal hand among goblin bones, two marble Fortunes (Fukurokujin, Benten), and the iron box. All but the box
  were found on the riverbank the next day.
- **The nudge:** the sick vanish overnight; a drunk dock-worker saw something blot out the moon, flying west.
- **Across the river:** the Old Farmstead, the Sake Works, Temple Hill, the Abandoned Barges (one drifts, gulls and
  ravens together). Small bare footprints appear from nowhere at the water. Insects, then birds, then beasts flee.
  The woods sicken into a Shadowlands marsh; escorts turn back.
- **Climax rules:** the Kansen uses its maho and the Box (Summon Greater Kansen, Harvest of Death), never its
  Taint-Touch; separating it from the Box ends the ritual and every Box-powered spell. After round 10 each round
  risks completion; the Oracle then joins or soaks away (1 round). Outcomes: full / partial / failure.
- **Aftermath:** Paneki is impassive; each PC gets a jasper "Favor". Mention the Box and he demands it for "safe
  disposal". The Box is a maho sensei (it teaches its keeper).
