# Research: Past Heroes + Entanglements & Tied Snakes (a two-part story)

- **Sources:** `Past Heroes.pdf` (16 pp, Leprecon XXII) and `Entanglements and Tied Snakes.pdf` (17 pp, its sequel),
  both by JP Chapleau. Read in full: session 1.
- **Era:** reign of **Hantei XXVI → XXVII** (~300 years before the standard campaign; "the fourth year of the reign of
  Hantei XXVI[I]"). **Off-era** for 1120 — but the structure transplants well (see adaptation).
- **Clans:** Past Heroes: Lion (Matsu), Crab (Hida/Kaiu), Scorpion (Bayushi/Yogo), Crane (Kakita), Dragon (Mirumoto).
  Entanglements: Emerald Magistrates (Seppun, Kuni, Shiba, Ide, Miya) + the Order of the Snake.
- **Theme:** "History is not as it seems"; **On** (face) — *the appearance of the truth IS the truth*; who has the right
  to rule; caste.

## Past Heroes — the legend vs. the truth
**Legend:** fifty years ago, on the night the Crown Prince was born at a winter hunting palace (the **Castle of the White
Snow**, between Phoenix and Dragon lands), a creature of Fu Leng stole him. Five champions (Matsu Kabuto, Hida Kito,
Bayushi Fushita, Doji Tetsumaru, Mirumoto Kichi) climbed the **Mountain of the White Snow** to an oni's castle; most died
gloriously; Bayushi brought the baby down to the **Village of the Sleeping/Wounded Lion** and died telling the tale.

**Each generation re-enacts the rescue.** Now the five champions' **grandchildren** climb the mountain for the
ceremony that crowns the new Emperor (the old one takes the tonsure).

**The truth (discovered on the climb):**
- No oni. **Hida Kito** — married to the only other heir — had rebel Imperial Guards (mostly Crab and Lion) kidnap the
  prince so he could become Prince Consort.
- On the way, Matsu shot an Imperial Guard Crane; Matsu and Doji fought, Doji died; Bayushi blasted the gates with
  gunpowder; Mirumoto **fled at the baby's cry** on Togashi's prophetic orders; Hida killed the infant (a wakizashi
  through the heart, in a stone basin of **bloodstones**); Matsu killed Hida, then committed seppuku writing "I have
  failed" in his own blood.
- Bayushi, wounded, reached the village, **sent a villager with his own newborn son to the Emperor as the "rescued
  prince"**, then killed every villager, burned them on a pyre (the snow mounds at the village gate), and threw himself
  on it. One peasant boy (now senile "Ji-ji") survived — the reigning **Emperor Hantei XXVII is a peasant's son**,
  Ji-ji's younger brother.
- Why nobody questioned it: the lie flattered every clan.
- Mirumoto Kichi is alive, ageless under a **Crane tattoo** Togashi gave him ("a living book of history"), now going by
  **Mirumoto Hikaru** — forbidden to speak until he sees the child in its bed of stone.
- Ending: if anyone tells the truth → civil war. Best outcome: only one returns and repeats the legend.

## Entanglements & Tied Snakes — the sequel
- Emerald Magistrates chase a "cult" to the **Village of Floating Flowers** (river stop between Crab, Scorpion and
  Sparrow lands): soap-opera villagers, a sleazy Yasuki merchant in love with a branded eta prostitute, a geisha-house
  Madame from Ryoko Owari, a beloved harbourmaster who is secretly a cultist; the snake den under his floor mats.
- **The Order of the Snake** is no cult: a century-old secret society founded to remove **usurpers** and protect ruling
  lines (members bear hidden snake tattoos; they barely know each other). Leader: the **Great Naga**.
- Trap room: "Only Snakes may pass" — crawl on your belly through white sand or be bitten by shadow-snakes (humiliating
  for high-Honor samurai). A giant stone serpent's mouth leads to a portal → a bodiless **judgement**: "This man is an
  impostor. He has killed thousands and will kill thousands more. What is your judgement?" → the heroes are then in a
  room with **the Emperor**.
- The Great Naga is the **Emerald Champion Doji Fuhito**, their own boss, who never orders anything — he only lets
  judgement be "executed." One magistrate (a Seppun) would be next in line for the throne.

## Game adaptation ideas
- **For 1120 IC**, change the stakes from the Emperor to a **daimyo line or a Great Clan's heir** — or keep it as
  **legend-era backstory** that the **ronin traveler** uncovers (e.g. the ronin is descended from the Village of the
  Wounded Lion, or their disgraced lord fell for discovering the secret).
- **Ronin traveler core idea:** a masterless samurai who learns that a revered lineage was founded on a peasant child —
  so caste is a lie. Ties beautifully to the finale's virtue debate (the ronin's answer to "bushido is a dream").
- Re-enactment pilgrimage up a snowy mountain → a strong dungeon (rock shelter with frozen yari, frozen Imperial Guard,
  courtyard of frozen corpses, the pagoda, the bloodstone basin).
- Ageless Crane-tattooed Mirumoto Hikaru → recurring Dragon NPC who "remembers" (links to Hindsight's theme of history).
- Order of the Snake → a secret society the Scorpion or Phoenix traveler could encounter; their "judgement" portal is a
  memorable set piece. Doji Fuhito-style "never gives the order" villain mirrors Nashiko's indirect methods.
- The snake-crawl trap = an Honor-cost choice (crawl and lose face, or take damage).
