# Research: ...And Does Not Descend

- **Source:** `And Does Not Descend.pdf` (24 pp). Gareth Hanrahan, Gaelcon 99. Read in full: session 1.
- **Era:** pre-Coup, unspecified year. Fits 1120 IC.
- **Primary clans:** **Crane** (the disgraced Kakita son; Lady Doji Isahime, "Keeper of the House Honour") in **Lion**
  lands (Lord Akodo Goroso near **Beiden Pass**). Scorpion manipulator; Kitsuki investigator.
- **Theme:** "There is no greater shame than a fallen son. There is no greater heroism than a noble fight against
  injustice." Breaking under pressure and rebuilding; the Celestial Order vs. compassion (jin).
- **Tone:** Robin Hood / *Seven Samurai*. **Very Octopath-friendly.** Only light violence.

## Backstory
A Crab lord, **Hida Jinjo** (prompted by Scorpion **Bayushi Depura**), mocked Crane youth at the court of **Kakita
Morihoshi**. Morihoshi boasted his son **Kakita Morishu** — a prodigy whispered to one day surpass Kakita Toshimoko —
was better than any Crab, and sent him (not yet 16, a month before gempukku) to the **Kaiu Wall**. Morishu looked
into the Shadowlands, broke, and fled. Depura offered a cover-up: marry Morihoshi's daughter to Jinjo to buy the
witnesses' silence. Then Morihoshi ordered his own son's death.

Morishu wandered as a ronin looking for a place to die, saved a beaten peasant (headman **Torishigawa**) by killing
two Lion samurai with two perfect cuts, and was sent to the forest bandits. As **the Green Ronin** he became their
Robin Hood against cruel Lord **Akodo Goroso**, found friends who didn't demand perfection, and secretly married
Torishigawa's daughter **Sariko** (now pregnant).

## The 36-hour plot
- Two Crane samurai (**Kakita Adoru**, proud talker; **Kakita Yasurugi**, silent, inhumanly calm killer) carry the
  family **wakizashi** and Morihoshi's letter "inviting" seppuku. Bandits rob and strip them and tie them to a tree.
- Morishu opens the box, sees the sword, panics, flees.
- Eta firewood-gatherer **Ebisu** finds the sword in a glade by a waterfall and tries to trade it for sake; youths beat
  him. **The heroes arrive at dusk** at **Pleasant Eaves of Mountains' Cloak Forest Village** and rescue him.
- **The sword:** *Heaven's Glory Reflected in the Soul's Steel* — ancient Kakita masterwork; its subtle power makes
  those who gaze on it reflect on honour and measure themselves against the ideal samurai.
- Forest: night ambush by bandits using paper lanterns on pulleys and a twin-sister "ghost maiden" luring travellers to
  a pit. Bandit camp is a village of runaway peasants (25 fighters, 40 non-combatants). **Imuro** (Little John, bo
  fighter, father cut down for insulting a samurai) pretends to be the Green Ronin.
- Morning: Morishu, witless, is captured by **Akodo Tama**'s men; Goroso proclaims dawn execution in his castle yard.
  Sariko wails at the gates. Bandits plan a rescue from inside the crowd.
- **Resolutions:** forced seppuku in the cell; bandit rescue at the execution; or **a duel** (testimony by challenge —
  Goroso names a hero as his champion vs. Morishu); possible kharmic mutual-kill. Epilogue poem:
  *"Only the Phoenix rises, / And does not descend / And everything passes / But nothing is truly lost."*
- Morihoshi's letter ends with a death haiku: *Autumn smoke rises / Sword reflects pyre's light / A samurai's death.*

## Game adaptation ideas
- **Crane traveler chapter (Ch1 or Ch2).** A Crane traveler in Isahime's role (an honour-keeper sent to quietly
  resolve the scandal) has a built-in moral arc: hide the shame, or do what's right?
- **Alternative: the RONIN traveler's backstory.** The Green Ronin structure — a disgraced samurai who found a cause
  among peasants — is a great template for our ronin, *without* using Morishu himself.
- Octopath fit: village → forest dungeon (lantern-lure puzzle, pit traps) → castle → execution-yard climax with a
  choice. Path action hooks: **Pilfer** (ronin) to steal the cell key; **Charm** (Crane) on Lady Tatanko; **Duel**
  (Lion) as Goroso's champion; **Eavesdrop** (Scorpion) on Depura.
- Key item: *Heaven's Glory Reflected in the Soul's Steel* (an Octopath-style "divine weapon" for the Crane traveler?).
- Boss candidates: Akodo Tama (+ ashigaru), Kakita Yasurugi (silent duellist — great mid-boss), or Goroso himself.
- The Depura / Hida Jinjo / Morihoshi's-daughter thread could tie the Crane and Crab travelers together.
