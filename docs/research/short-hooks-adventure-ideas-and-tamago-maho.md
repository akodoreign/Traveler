# Research: Adventure Ideas (hook collection) + Tamago Maho

Both read in full: session 1. Short pieces; useful for side stories and ronin material.

## Adventure Ideas (`Adventure Ideas.pdf`, 9 pp — fan "Challenge / Focus / Strike" hooks)
| Hook | Gist | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **The Ronin's Sword** (Jason Saice) | A ronin guards a frost-rimed blade he won't discuss; after he's killed it goes to the greediest/lowest-Void hero and only they can draw it. It makes higher-ranked strangers believe it was theirs (accusations, magistrates); if given away, it sends an **air-kami copy of its owner** to steal it back while they sleep. Cure: a **vow of poverty** for one year without speaking of the curse; then its greed-bound kami are freed and it loses all power. | **Ronin traveler**: their past or a chapter. Lovely "cursed equipment" quest. |
| The Wayward Charge | Escorting a "demure" noble girl on a shrine tour; she drinks, flirts, vanishes with a handsome ronin — secretly the son of a rival shugenja family (love, or mutual spying). | Side story. |
| The Retainer | The lord's trusted watcher on your mission is secretly paid to stop you; accusing him without proof costs face. | Side story; fits Scorpion/Crane. |
| Black Opium | A renegade Dragon alchemist near Ryoko Owari makes cheap, potent black-flowered opium and is building an idol that converts whole warehouses. | Scorpion side story. |
| The Broken Sword | Your katana snaps at the end of a perfect kata; the daimyo forbids seppuku — the sensei swapped the blade as a test for promotion. | Lion/Crane character beat. |
| The Deadly Message | Deliver a sealed message — it reads "Kill these messengers." The recipient is a great retired sensei; delivering anyway proves devotion. | Excellent Octopath chapter twist (e.g. Lion or Dragon). |
| The Violent Husband | An honourable lord beats his wife, who believes she deserves it. | Serious content — only with care; probably skip. |
| The Noble and the Ronin | A spoilt noble attacks a devout ronin on musha shugyo and calls for help "apprehending the criminal". | **Ronin traveler's recruitment scene** candidate. |
| Thief! Thief! | Heirlooms and a cat vanish; a shady servant is blamed — the thief is the lord's kleptomaniac grandmother. | Comic side story. |

## Tamago Maho (`Tamago Maho.pdf`, 4 pp)
- City short of rice and **eggs**; an angry Crab bushi berates a serving girl over egg-drop soup.
- Rash of trinket thefts (fans, mirrors); a ronin gang "The Hidden Ones" is blamed and raided — but they don't have the
  missing mirror or food.
- A **nezumi** (Grasping Paw tribe) rummages through a hero's bag at night; its pack hides in nearby unstable caves
  that stink of rotten eggs.
- **Seppun Ryozo**, a mad Imperial guardsman, believes he can cast maho without blood using **specially marked eggs**
  (he has no power at all); the orphaned nezumi he raised are fiercely loyal. Talk him down (his aunt **Seppun Satomi**,
  an Otomo-trained courtier, rewards discretion) or fight; loud combat can collapse the cave; if he crawls away
  wounded, a real **oni seeking a name** finds him…
- **Use:** a gentle, comic-then-creepy **Crab traveler Chapter 1** or a side story; nezumi allies are a nice recurring
  element (cf. Stich'Kik in *Of Family and Honor*). The oni-gets-a-name ending is a seed for a later chapter.
