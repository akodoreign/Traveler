# Research: Hindsight (Parts I–III + Cast)

- **Source:** `Hindsight Part I/II/III.pdf` (24+30+22 pp) + `Hindsight-Cast.pdf`. Chris & Jennifer Hepler,
  Origins 2000, L5R 1st ed. **Part I read in full; Part III's ending + appendices read; Part II and Cast still to read
  in full** (session 2 to-do).
- **Era:** present day is **1120 IC, pre-Coup** — *exact fit*. Parts II–III take place in the **year 400** (Hantei VI
  "Kusada", the **Gozoku** era: three clan daimyo — Bayushi Atsuki, Doji Tomeko & champion Doji Seikidera, Shiba
  Katsutoshi — rule through a puppet Emperor; gaijin common, recently purged).
- **Primary clan:** **Dragon** (Kitsuki investigators; murdered ise zumi; renegade Togashi Akuro). Mantis (Yoritomo's
  islands), Crane (artisan accomplice).
- **Theme:** "Hindsight": history as remembered vs. as it happened; temptation to fix the past; reincarnation (meeting
  your enemy's past life); the humility of refusing glory.

## Part I — Under the Skin (Isle of Silk, 1120)
- Kitsuki daimyo **Kitsuki Yasu** sends the heroes: a skinned body found in a garbage heap on the **Isle of Silk** is
  **Togashi Nobue**, one of Togashi Yokuni's hand-picked ise zumi troubleshooters.
- Audience with **Yoritomo** (blunt, bluffing, careful not to offend the Dragon) and corrupt Storm Legion captain
  **Tadayasu** (shaved head, armour at court, kama-carrying thugs; Nemesis of a pregen) who saddles them with a
  minder, **Kosai**.
- Clues: Earth spirits at the dump only scream "BLOOD! FIRE! PAIN!" (maddened by Togashi blood); cheerful eta mortician
  **Muika**; temple school at **Rakusho** where simple, eidetic **Riichi** has memorised **deliberately altered Tao
  verses** — a code left by three successive monks (Ayaru, Jotaro, Nobue).
- The code leads up the stairs of the ruined **Kyuden Gusai** (torn down after Gusai Rioshida's hostage plot): fourth
  step → footpath; three stones 33 steps apart with arrows; 20 stones up a wall → hidden notes.
- Notes: all three were tracking **Togashi Akuro**, an elderly ise zumi whose final tattoo gave him a vision he couldn't
  bear; he can change his appearance; no Taint where he's been.
- **Kakita Kumpei** (failed artisan, Nobue's "helper") hides in a boathouse acting terrified — actually Akuro's
  accomplice who helped kill all three monks.
- **Tortoise Island**: a new wooden house with Dragon alchemical gear; two skinned bodies in the cellar; an unnatural
  earthquake collapses the house; hypnotised ronin shugenja & Mantis ruffians wreck the heroes' boat; tide-fight.
- **Cove cave**: a miniature model of Otosan Uchi growing more ancient toward the centre; a **portal painting on the
  monks' skin**; Kumpei and Akuro walk inside it. The heroes jump in as its edges blacken.

## Part II — Past Mistakes (Otosan Uchi, year 400) — read in full, session 1
- **Arrival:** a breathless limbo outside time, then a fall into a cherry orchard as Imperial guard **Seppun Ushimo**
  cuts down his partner and goes for the boy-Emperor **Hantei Kusada** (14, polite, suspects he's a puppet; heir is a
  6-month-old son; his ancestral no-dachi *Akkai-uo*). Saving him makes the heroes the court's sensation.
- **Butterfly effects** (great game mechanic for a "past" chapter): every anachronism risks history; GM changes a hero
  (mon disappears, eye colour turns Crane-blue, clothes become peasant rags, ancestor spirits scream). Things that
  don't exist yet: Emerald Magistrates, the Unicorn (not returned), Tortoise/Wasp, Asahina and Kitsuki families,
  the Kaiu Wall (it's "Hida's Wall"), Kuni Witch Hunters; Mantis are the **Gusai** family; Hiruma Castle still stands;
  most samurai carry straight **tachi** (katana are rare luxuries). A Crab–Crane pair is suspicious (100 years after
  the Yasuki break).
- **Three dinners, same hour** from the three gozoku daimyo:
  - **Bayushi Atsuki** — corpulent, Roman-emperor style, bribes rather than blackmails, ran the previous Hantei too;
    wants the heroes to get a Bayushi girl "access" to the teenage Emperor (would change the succession that leads to
    the first Empress, Yugozohime); later in life secretly digs a second pass under the Spine of the World.
  - **Doji Tomeko** — young, newly inherited Crane daimyo, sheltered from the gaijin purges, wants limited sea trade;
    relies on her Champion **Doji Seikidera** (Sir Lancelot with anime hair; ancestral sword *Shukujo* that rings for its
    true wielder; appalled by the purges he had to carry out).
  - **Shiba Katsutoshi** — "the boss you have always hated", Logic-Proof (+10 to read him), ordered the purges;
    history books say he was noble (Scorpion histories say he was a thug who silenced his critics). His page is the boy
    **Isawa Sanro** — the future **Isawa Asahina**, already theorising about waking spirits in objects (the origin of
    tsangusuri/Asahina charms).
- **Why Ushimo struck:** his wife **Tsuhime** is dying of a gaijin-borne disease, kept out of pain only by gaijin
  medicine; Akuro, wearing her doctor's face (**Chameleon tattoo**), drugged his sake and planted one suggestion:
  "you're at the end of your rope." Goal: kill the Hantei so the clans feud and nobody enforces the gaijin ban.
- **Tracking Akuro:** a duplicate of the portal painting hangs by the palace's south entrance (commissioned from
  elderly artisan **Kakita Miyoshi**, paid in anachronistic glass mirrors) by "Shosuro Matsuo" — whose bear mask made
  him easy to impersonate.
- **Shiro Izumida** (small winter palace 10 miles south): groundskeepers massacred, musket balls in the walls; burned
  Ikoma battle plans for the White Stag; tortured spirits scream "It wrote US! Make it un-be!" and scorch an exact map
  of the gaijin invasion into the floor. From the ridge: a gaijin army marching north.
- **War council:** Atsuki and Katsutoshi close the gates; Tomeko is drowned out — and **Seikidera is missing**: he
  rode to a Mantis shipyard to secretly talk peace. At sea, the "stableboy" (Akuro) shoots him in the back of the head
  during the parley with Mantis trader **Gusai Iami** and the gaijin. Ship battle; powder room may explode.
- **Akuro's vision** (he explains, half-mad): "The son will become the dark brother, the shadow, the throne. The jade
  clan will go black… The dead will rule, and out of all hope, seven mortals will rise. One of them will be HER."
  — i.e. he saw **the Clan War**: the Scorpion and Crab betraying the throne, plague, the Akodo destroyed, Fu Leng on
  the Emerald Throne. He wants gunpowder to stay so "peasant hands work steel" and history bends away from that end.
- **Akuro's tattoos:** Chameleon, Crab (absorbs wounds), Wasp (extra action), Pine (ignore wound penalties), Spider
  (poison skin) — or Baku (steal a sleeper's technique). He carries the last vial of **kami-ichor** (Togashi's blood).

## Part III — The Battle of the White Stag
The heroes must replace history: win the land battle; have someone pose as the Crane Champion to bring the merchant
fleets; force the Mantis to reject the gaijin; keep Isawa enchantments up until the Dragon and Centipede arrive; fight
the sea battle under cannon fire (lighthouse peninsulas, harbour clogged with wrecks, gaijin in the tunnels, sharks);
keep the future Isawa Asahina alive; and catch Kumpei — the only artisan who knows what 1120 looks like, their only
way home (one vial of kami-ichor left).
- **Akuro's motive:** he believes gaijin gunpowder technology could let Rokugan defeat Fu Leng. **Appendix 2 "The
  World That Should Not Be"**: if he wins, Akodo Minobe is never born → no one stops Iuchiban in 510 → by 1120 the
  east is Iuchiban's industrial undead empire; the Unicorn invent flight. (A superb "bad future" vision.)
- **The real ending test:** refuse the Emperor's offer of glory and erase yourselves from history. Reward: Honor 5,
  told to no one. ("It has been our experience that playtesters don't do this.")
- Kitsuki Yasu doesn't believe the time-travel story and suggests employment as a jester; 3 Glory for the murderer.

## Game adaptation ideas
- **Dragon traveler's arc (Ch2–4).** Perfect Octopath structure:
  - Ch2: Isle of Silk murder mystery (Discern path action; code-breaking puzzle on the Gusai ruin stairs).
  - Ch3: the past — year 400 court as a "flashback world" with its own NPCs (Octopath 2-style "past" chapter).
  - Ch4: Battle of the White Stag (multi-front event battle like Of Family and Honor R2), then the choice to go home
    unremembered — the Dragon traveler's quiet, perfect ending.
- Kakita Kumpei is a great sympathetic villain (failed artist; believed he was saving the Empire).
- **Bad-future vision** can be shown as a dream/illusion sequence — and ties to the Bloodspeaker thread (Iuchiban).
- Portal painting = a Crane artisan mechanic (paintings as fast-travel / story items).
- Gunpowder/gaijin pepper motif recurs (Fortune's Lost bomb cave) — the "temptation of foreign weapons" theme.
- Caution: time travel can bloat scope. Keep the past segment to one chapter.
- **Huge link to our ending:** Akuro foresaw *exactly* the catastrophe our epilogue points at (the Coup and Clan War
  beginning). The Dragon traveler learns the future is coming and must still choose not to cheat it — the thematic
  mirror of the finale's "choose bushido freely". Akuro's prophecy can be quoted in the epilogue.
- "Butterfly" changes → a visual/statistical mechanic in the past chapter (party members' outfits/portraits change
  if a "History" meter drops).
