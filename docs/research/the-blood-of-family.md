# Research: The Blood of Family

- **Source:** `The Blood of Family.pdf` (20 pp). Gareth Hanrahan, Gaelcon 2000. Read in full: session 1.
- **Era:** six months before the Scorpion Coup; Lion–Crane war beginning. **Fits 1120 IC.**
- **Primary clans:** **Lion** (minor Akodo house of Lord **Akodo Hiruto/Hirotu** — the text spells it both ways) and
  **Phoenix** (the Isawa house of **Kamatori**, Shiba vassals). Imperial (Miya) agent provocateur; Crane duellist.
- **Theme:** "Honour, duty, loyalty — but sometimes the code isn't too clear." Guilt without intent; the karmic weight of
  killing; family duty.
- **Tone:** moderate; ghosts and possession; suitable for Octopath with light softening.

## Premise
Hiruto's estates sit where Lion, Phoenix and Crane lands meet. The Phoenix were secretly supplying the Crane army.
Hiruto's hot-headed younger son **Gusai** raided and burned the Phoenix warehouses on the **Magonojo river** — and
his cousin **Ozaki** (who wants a war to win Glory and be named heir) ordered men to also despoil nearby shrines.
Samurai **Akodo Hojiki** broke open a shrine holding the bound skull of **O-Tamai**, a Bloodspeaker ("beloved of
Iuchiban") imprisoned a generation ago. O-Tamai rides Hojiki's sleeping body, casts maho, and kills **Isawa Hoju**,
the last surviving Phoenix who bound him. The Phoenix trace the maho west to the Lion. O-Tamai *wants* to be found
in a Lion body to drag the Phoenix into the war and destroy them.

## Structure
1. **The Phoenix arrive** with an Imperial magistrate (Miya Yachiko); Hiruto orders his own children to investigate.
2. **Free-form castle investigation.** The castle is a *spiritual wasteland* (O-Tamai devoured the local kami;
   non-maho spells are harder). Speak with the dead: Hoju's kindly ghost; Ashimoshi (a samurai trapped in a silent
   hurricane, mad); O-Tamai posing as a murdered peasant's ghost to accuse Hojiki. Clues: the real jade stone Hojiki
   bound to his wound lies on the bathhouse floor (O-Tamai swapped it for an illusioned pebble); Ozaki's looted
   statue of Isawa.
3. **Escalating events:** scouts report Crane troops; Kitsu Togawa is choked from inside in the chapel; visiting
   Matsu sensei Nagai is possessed and attacks the Phoenix; O-Tamai, impatient, stages a flashy maho attack in Hojiki's body.
4. **Confront Hojiki** — a good man whose body was stolen; technically guilty. Party splits (Phoenix want custody).
5. **Investigate O-Tamai:** ride to **Shiro no Kamatori** (five towers, walls glazed and copper-edged so it looks
   built of fire in the sun); Hoju's unfinished family history (handout); the four despoiled shrines; the **jade
   chains** enchanted by Hoju — the lock grows cold near O-Tamai (a hunting compass).
6. **Climax (three options):** (a) **Binding** — summon O-Tamai into a body (a volunteer or Hojiki), wrap the jade
   chains, defeat his red-shadow oni (soul-damage); (b) **The Duel** — Crane duellist **Kakita Mizochi** arrives under
   truce to settle the border by iaijutsu; O-Tamai offers "Hojiki's" maho to guarantee the Lion's win; (c) **The Slayer
   of his Father** — a red shadow creeps across the court wall toward Lord Hiruto to possess him.

## Lore nuggets
- **Iuchiban** handout: the Bloodspeaker of year 510, entombed in jade; returned 200 years later as a spirit leaping
  body to body; defeated at the **Battle of Stolen Graves** when a **tattooed monk** (Togashi) trapped the spirit
  in his own body. His lieutenants (e.g. the artificer **Yajinden**) may also live as spirits.
- Sodan-seizo, Kitsu ancestor-masks, the Lands of the Dead (Meido).
- Imperial houses (Otomo/Seppun/Miya) secretly keep the Great Clans in balance — a recurring conspiracy note
  (cf. *Entanglements & Tied Snakes*).

## Game adaptation ideas
- **Lion traveler Chapter 1 candidate** (or a Lion/Phoenix crossover): small home castle, a clear mystery, a
  "possession" boss, and three branching climaxes. A Lion traveler from a minor Akodo house fits naturally — e.g.
  the investigating child.
- **Phoenix traveler tie-in:** the Phoenix traveler could be Hoju's apprentice carrying his unfinished history.
- Mechanic: **jade-chain compass** (key item that grows cold — a "hot/cold" search on the map).
- Boss: O-Tamai's red-shadow oni deal **"soul damage"** (damage to SP/Void instead of HP).
- Duel scene with Kakita Mizochi → Lion **Duel** path action showcase; refusing O-Tamai's cheat is an Honor choice.
- Spirit-wasteland zone: elemental skills weaker in the castle until O-Tamai is bound (a map-wide debuff).
