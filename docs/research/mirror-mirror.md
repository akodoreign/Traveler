# Research: Mirror, Mirror

- **Source:** `Mirror, Mirror.pdf` (51 pp) + `Mirror, Mirror-Cast.pdf` (6 pregens with dark secrets: Isawa Ryoji,
  Hiruma Yasuji, Daidoji Kame, Yogo Sumiko, Ide Masafumi, Kitsu Kikuzo). Brandes & Hepler, Origins '99, a
  companion to *Bearers of Jade*. Rejected by AEG as "cranked up to 11". Main text read in full: session 1.
  (Cast file not yet read.)
- **Era:** pre-Coup. Hida Kisada is Crab Champion, Shiba Ujimitsu Phoenix Champion. **Fits 1120 IC.**
- **Primary clan:** **Crab** (Kyuden Hida Winter Court, Kaiu Wall, Shadowlands). Phoenix, Unicorn, Lion, Scorpion guests.
- **Theme:** "When you look for secrets, be ready for what you find." Failure and trying again (Osano-Wo, the Hiruma,
  the villainess). Horror; mirrors — the villainess's past is not unlike the heroes' own secrets.
- **Tone warning:** extremely dark (mutilation, child-endangerment, sexual menace, soul-eating). Game version must
  be pulled back hard; keep the dread and the court tension, not the gore.

## Structure (three parts at Kyuden Hida's Winter Court)
1. **"...On the Wall"** — Kisada orders the heroes to quietly vet **Isawa Mutsuye**, bride of Hiruma daimyo
   **Hiruma Kage**, before the betrothal announcement (4-hour clock). Court social scenes: dance contest, archery
   contest (shooting dogs "to keep up appearances" — normally goblins), gossip from Shosuro Shiko, the creepy Yogo
   Shigeru torturing a troll with Kuni Taira. Then **Ide Keiyomi is murdered** by maho (*No Pure Breaths*), faceless.
   Evidence is planted to frame Mutsuye (now Tainted by a *Sinful Dreams* spell) and Yogo Shigeru (forged scrolls).
   Kisada demands a culprit at the report: accuse Mutsuye → duel her bodyguard; accuse Shigeru → he's executed.
   Either way, the real killer walks. Mutsuye ends up Tainted and commits suicide.
2. **"Tears and Smoke"** — Kage, grief-mad, slips into the Shadowlands. The heroes follow through the Wall's trapped
   tunnels: tar chasm, Lava Trees (upside-down forest), giant toothed poppy fields, onikage demon-horses, a broken
   Hiruma guard (mercy or rescue?), and **Toshi no Kijo** (troll ruin coliseum) where Kage smashes pottery. Talk him
   back. Then hide from **Moto akutsukai** scouts (undead Unicorn riders, skeletal wings), and survive a fake
   "Crab patrol" of maho-bujin + an **Oni no Kommei** that swaps two heroes' bodies.
3. **"The Fairest One of All"** — Kage is forced to marry **Asako Kuhime** at once. The Shadowlands army attacks;
   every Wall trap has been sabotaged from inside. The heroes split: scouts at the Wall, or bodyguards herding the
   court into the cellars. In a pickle barrel: **the real Kuhime, faceless, dead two weeks.** The bride upstairs is
   **Doji Nashiko**. Final act: stalk her through the dark castle, lure her away from the children via the trap
   tunnels (jade-dust trip line, rollers, spiked pit) out to the Wall where the Crab army can surround her.

## Doji Nashiko (the Akutenshi) — key facts
- Crane courtier who sold her soul to Fu Leng **centuries ago**. Only child; loving father died; stepfather abused
  her for 14 years. Could only gain justice by marrying someone of higher Glory than him → the Emerald Champion
  **Doji Komatsu**, who jilted her for a prettier face. She turned to maho, seduced and destroyed his whole court.
- Vain; obsessed with beauty; wants to be called **"Empress"**; **colour-blind** (all smooth rock looks like jade to
  her); **phobia of disfigurement**; hates other beautiful women, underestimates men; **can only read one mind at
  a time**. Doesn't know a play was written about her.
- Powers: shape-change (1 round to a person), soul drinking, invisibility, flight, mind-reading, Command the Taint,
  Blood Shouting (wounds shift to anyone she's kissed), Body of Damned Time (ages onlookers), Touch of Death,
  summon oni, Sinful Dreams. Jade only burns bare skin (she hides it with illusioned gloves).
- On death: inky void + a white jewelled porcelain mask breaks into motes of light (freed souls); something under
  the Shadowlands bellows in grief.
- If she escapes: recurring enemy at court, wearing faces.
- **Also the villain of *Of Family and Honor*** → strongest candidate for the game's final antagonist.

## Locations
Kyuden Hida (7 floors: Kisada 7, audience 6, Crab nobles 5, high-Glory 4, guests 3, court 1–2; basement donjon;
secret exits behind sake barrels; trap catacombs to the Wall), women's shrine on the mountainside, the Kaiu Wall,
the Shadowlands: tar chasm & rope bridge, Upside-Down Forest (Lava Trees), toothed poppy fields, acid bogs,
Toshi no Kijo (troll ruin), the Changing Lands.

## Shadowlands bestiary used (all good enemy designs for us)
Goblins / warmongers, ogres, zombies, onikage (demon horses), Moto akutsukai (winged undead riders), maho-bujin,
Oni no Kommei (body-swapper, face on the back of its head), Oni no Tsukakoro (bouncing bone cage), giant millipede
oni (carry troops), Oni no Kyoso, Oni no Sanru (fliers), Oni no Kamu, Oni no Ashi, Oni no Tobehifu (skin strips
that burrow), Oni no Satsujinko (Taint cloud), Mamono (resurrects at night unless cut in three and buried apart),
Lava Trees. Named generals: Oni no Tsume, Oni no Akuma (afraid of Kisada), Oni no Chi.

## Game adaptation ideas
- **Crab traveler's Chapter 2 or 3** (court murder mystery at Kyuden Hida → Shadowlands rescue), and/or **the
  finale's lead-in**: Nashiko is seen here first, wearing a friend's face, and escapes — she becomes the thread
  through every story.
- Crab traveler hook: a Hiruma (family without a home) → personal stake in Kage, Hiruma Castle, and "I will not fail".
- **Path Action Sumai** used to win the Wall Crabs' trust; **Discern** (Dragon) to read the knife's spirit
  ("held by a small woman's hand").
- Dungeon: the trapped tunnels as a puzzle-dungeon; the Shadowlands crossing as a **Taint meter** zone (hazards
  raise Taint; jade items lower it).
- Boss ideas: Oni no Kommei (swaps two party members' commands/stats for the fight!), Oni no Tsukakoro
  (multi-target bounce), Nashiko's stalking sequence (no-combat stealth segment; she can be anyone).
- Status effect: **Taint** (stacking debuff; jade cures; at max → "Command the Taint" charm).
- Item: blue flare (story), jade-studded tetsubo, Kaiu blade.
