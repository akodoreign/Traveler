# Research: Fortune's Lost

- **Source:** `Adventures In Rokugan/Fortune's Lost.pdf` (45 pp) + 6 pregen sheets. Brandes & Hepler, Tastes Like
  Phoenix Games, GenCon 1999 tournament (L5R 1st ed). Read in full: session 1.
- **Era:** the year before the Scorpion Coup — the Lion–Crane war is on, Doji Satsume is Emerald Champion,
  Bayushi Shoju is scheming ("more important things"). **Fits our 1120 IC era exactly.**
- **Primary clan:** Unicorn (Iuchi) vs Scorpion (Soshi). Dragon (Mirumoto army) has a big supporting role.
- **Tone:** war crimes, refugees, then full Shadowlands horror. Very dark: mass graves, torture, possessed children.
  **The game version needs to tone this down** to Octopath's level (implied, not shown).

## Premise
An unsanctioned shugenja-family war in the **Ojatara Valleys** (Spine of the World Mountains, between Shiro no Soshi
and Shiro Iuchi; south of White Shore Lake). Twenty years ago Iuchi Kurosho crushed a Soshi land-grab; now Bayushi
Shoju has nudged the Soshi into trying again (as a distraction from his coup). Both sides committed atrocities.
The Emerald Champion sends **three magistrate teams** (a Scorpion-leaning one, a Unicorn-leaning one, and the
neutral PC team holding the swing vote) to run a war-crimes tribunal.

## Structure (three parts)
1. **Fault Lines** — open-ended investigation of eight grievances; meet both courts; visit the atrocity sites.
   Climax: a **gunpowder bombing** of the inn at the Town of Buried Leaves, then a raid on the bombers' cave.
2. **Trails and Tribulations** — the tribunal (haggling for votes), a stalker targeting the royal-blooded PC,
   tracing the bomber's sensei and mother, an ambush by an **Iuchi shugenja hit squad** that rises out of the earth
   inside a silence bubble. Then a **maho sacrifice** victim is found — something has been summoned.
3. **The Noblest Blood** — the **Shuten Doji of Regret** is loose. Possession spreads by *hearing* its servants.
   The heroes plug their ears, rescue the Iuchi heir and daimyo, find the Emerald Legions already possessed,
   persuade the Mirumoto army to fight, fetch jade past risen dead from the mass grave, and storm Shiro Iuchi.

## The eight grievances (great "case file" structure)
1. Assassins killed the Iuchi guard captain and the daimyo's wife (assassins bear Shadow-Brands → Soshi).
2. Jiochi village well poisoned, ~750 dead (it was the Iuchi interrogation camp; Soshi silenced it with oleander).
3. A Shosuro acting troupe massacred by Iuchi troops.
4. Fields outside Shiro Soshi salted (Iuchi general "Takanobu", a Shinjo by birth: "Wars are for winning").
5. Temple of Jizo at Motobu destroyed by Fist of Osano-Wo (Soshi hid soldiers in it; Iuchi blew it up).
6. Soshi Katsu's funeral peace broken (by Katsu's own 14-year-old son, Ichihito, after a false truce).
7. Both sides padded armies with other families' troops.
8. Jade production stopped; the Jade Mines hide a mass grave nobody claims.

## Lore nuggets worth reusing
- Rokugani justice: **testimony by Glory beats evidence**; accusations only within your Glory bracket. Perfect for a
  "trial" minigame: gather testimonies, not just clues.
- War law: wars must be petitioned to the Emperor and are limited to named families; three-day mourning peace for
  a fallen daimyo; non-combatants protected; land belongs to the Emperor.
- **Shuten Doji** — three demon "sensei of maho": **Fear** (spreads by sight; repelled by **obsidian**), **Regret**
  (by hearing; hurt by **jade**), **Desire** (by touch; banished by **crystal**). Destroyed the Snake Clan.
  Kill every host or it returns. Banished for years equal to the slayer's Honor.
- Gaijin pepper (gunpowder) — saltpeter from bird droppings; banned by the Emperor.
- Meishodo (Unicorn amulet magic): cast in one round but no Raises.

## Game adaptation ideas
- **Best fit: the Unicorn traveler's 4 chapters** (or a Unicorn-led cross-story with the Scorpion and Dragon travelers).
  - Ch1: Arrive in the valley; refugees; a grievance or two as tutorial investigation; boss = bomb-cave guerrillas (Naohida? or a lieutenant).
  - Ch2: Tribunal; testimony-gathering; the stalker / bell trap; boss = Iuchi shugenja hit squad (silence bubble — disables spells for a turn!).
  - Ch3: Perfume Petal Blossom, Nihime's story, the sacrifice; boss = risen dead at the Jade Mines.
  - Ch4: Shuten Doji of Regret. **Battle gimmick: "Covered Ears"** — a party-wide status that blocks possession but
    disables Path Actions / some commands; its servants' wail inflicts possession on uncovered allies.
    Jade element is the Suki key; Nihime's curse breaks Kurosho's guard.
- Unicorn traveler hook: personal tie to the Iuchi (a cousin of Daiyu? a ward of Hiroichi? Miyahana's other student
  — i.e. Naohida's fellow pupil, which makes Ch1's bomber a childhood friend).
- **Barter** path action fits: the Unicorn traveler trades with refugees/merchants without shame.
- Item ideas: Gaijin telescope (reveal enemy weakness), wax earplugs (key item), jade powder, obsidian shard,
  the clapperless bell.
- Mechanics: the **trial** as a debate minigame where testimony carries "Glory weight".
