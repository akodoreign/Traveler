# Rokugan Traveler

An unofficial, non-commercial **Legend of the Five Rings** fan JRPG in the style of *Octopath Traveler*: eight
travelers, eight roads, one empire. HD-2D pixel-art characters walk voxel maps of Rokugan, and every road ends on the
Plains of Thunder, where the travelers finally meet.

Built with the RPG-Cobo engine. **Alpha** — playable from the first chapter of every road to the
finale and post-game, and still being tested and polished.

---

## The eight travelers

| Traveler | Clan | |
|---|---|---|
| Hiruma Natsuko | Crab | a scout of the Kaiu Wall |
| Kakita Rensei | Crane | a duelist of the Kakita Academy |
| Kitsuki Gendo | Dragon | a magistrate who follows the evidence |
| Akodo Shizue | Lion | a tactician |
| Isawa Haruki | Phoenix | a shugenja |
| Shosuro Mitsue | Scorpion | a shinobi of many faces |
| Ide Tamotsu | Unicorn | a diplomat and merchant |
| Sayo | Ronin | a ronin's daughter and herbalist |

Each road is four chapters. Pick a traveler, walk their road, and at its end choose the next. When all eight roads are
walked, the finale opens — and after it, a post-game in the deep Shadowlands.

## Features

- **Turn-based battles** with shields and weaknesses, Void Points (VP) to boost attacks and spells, and per-clan
  battle styles. Each traveler has a school, techniques and Insight Ranks.
- **Jobs and traits** — each traveler's own job, plus generic jobs unlocked through side quests (Yojimbo, Courtier,
  Sergeant, Sohei, Champion). Wear a job and up to four traits.
- **Companions, guests and hirelings** — story companions fight beside you; guests answer the Support call once a
  round; hirelings stand in the back row for a few charges.
- **A world to wander** — connecting roads between the clans' lands, side caves of every difficulty, side quests,
  odd jobs, shops and shrines to rest and save.
- **Clan castles** — twelve full-map grounds districts in a 3-by-4 grid, from reception through the courtyard garden
  to a four-floor keep, with walkable stairs, open upper-floor stairwells with descending treads, visible indoor doorways,
  clan-themed gardens and four residents' fetch errands per castle (32 total): seals, incense, court supplies and
  council records. Stair arrivals land clear of the opening on the next floor.
  Grounds entrances are marked in the castle towns; existing story halls keep their original routes. Kyuden Togashi's
  grounds open after the garden audience, and ruined or besieged story locations retain their encounter layouts.
- **Journal and quest marker** — track a story objective or side quest, and a floating star shows the way.
- **Winter Court mini-games** — the archery contest, a horse race, and a game of Memory with the court's faces.
- **Original soundtrack** in a medieval Japanese style, and portraits for every speaking character.
- **Update / Verify** from the title screen keeps your copy current with this repository.

## How to play (Windows)

1. Download this repository (**Code → Download ZIP**, or `git clone`) and unpack it anywhere.
2. Double-click **`Play Rokugan Traveler.bat`**.
3. On the title screen choose **New Game**, then the traveler whose road you want to walk.

Use **Update / Verify** on the title screen to fetch the latest version later. A yellow notice on the title screen tells
you when one is available.

**Controls** (keyboard; a gamepad works too): arrow keys move, **Z** confirm / talk, **X** back / jump, **C** menu,
**Esc** pause. Save at shrines, waystones and inns.

See `HOW TO PLAY.txt` for more, and `NOTES.md` for the full development log.
